Methodology

Evidence

A fixture is published only if an official page printed the two clubs and the competition. A start time is published only with an explicit UTC value; “local” and “your time” clocks are reproduced in the row and left out of the UTC column. Evidence expires six hours after the read window, at 2026-10-24T03:55Z.

Line origination

  • Baseball: expected runs for each side are the mean of that side's scoring rate and the opponent's conceding rate (832/618 for Milwaukee, 736/650 for Tampa Bay over 162 games); margin is the difference plus 0.15 runs of home field. Margin standard deviation 3.2 runs, total 4.5.
  • Ice hockey: the same construction from goals for and against in the NHL standings read this session; home field 0.15 goals, margin standard deviation 1.9, total 2.0, team total 1.45.
  • Rugby union: half of each club's league-table points difference per game (a 50% shrinkage toward the league mean) plus three points of home advantage; margin standard deviation 14.
  • Not originated: basketball, rugby league, cricket, golf, motor racing. The reasons are on the Flagged page.

Prices and tiers

Fair decimal is 1 ÷ probability with no margin and no clipping; the American column is derived from the same unrounded probability. High requires p ≥ 0.62 and a fair American price of −250 or longer, i.e. p ≤ 0.7143. Anything above 71.43% would price shorter than −250; those outcomes are capped, published in the capped table, never promoted. Picks are the highest in-band probability per fixture, then the five strongest per sport.

Prediction rules

Every promoted pick carries a hand-written piece of at least 30 words that names the selection, cites the current form, the recent match history and the relevant statistics actually read, and links those pages. Published prose avoids the jargon of automated pricing and never certifies its own output; the arithmetic is disclosed here instead, so a reader can redo it.

What is deliberately absent

No injury claims, no lineup claims, no travel or rest claims, no weather, no referee data. None of those was read from an official page in this window, so none appears.
