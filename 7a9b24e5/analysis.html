2. Where the window binds

Money-line prices rarely land inside it. Milwaukee's single-game price is 56.8%, comfortably under the 62% floor, and no baseball side today prices anywhere near a 62%–71% win probability because a 162-game record separates these two clubs by well under a run. The window fills instead wherever a line can be moved a point at a time: the run line, the puck line, team totals and rugby handicaps. All fourteen promoted selections are line markets, and each one names the side we expect to come out ahead — the Bulls and the Stormers are laid to win by more than their start, while Leinster, Ospreys, Benetton and Milwaukee are backed to cover while losing narrowly at worst.

3. Four Series games, one underlying estimate

World Series games 1–4 pair the same two clubs, so the expected total (8.75 runs) and the expected margin are identical across them and four picks are repeats of two ideas. That is disclosed rather than hidden: the run-line selections in Games 1 and 2 are the same bet twice, and the totals in Games 3 and 4 are the same bet twice. A reader who wants one position per idea should take one of each pair.

4. What the desk refused to publish

  • Rugby League World Cup. Round 1 produced five results between ten teams with no shared opponents, so the margin inputs cannot rank teams across pools. Pricing Australia v Fiji off those numbers produced an absurd “Australia +14.75”, which is exactly the failure mode we are guarding against. No line was published.
  • Basketball. The NBA season is one or two games old. A 2-0 start is not a strength estimate, so Boston v New York and the other four games are listed without prices.
  • Golf, motor racing, cricket. Fixtures are confirmed but no official input supports a probability: a leaderboard is a scoreboard rather than a strength input; the F1 points table did not render; and the ICC fixtures carry no form data in the page read.
  • Snooker, darts, handball, volleyball, Gaelic sports, greyhounds. No upcoming fixture could be read from an official source inside the window. The attempted URLs are on the Flagged page.
