1. Scope

Match list covers all sports on the brief EXCLUDING: soccer/football, boxing, UFC, MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials.

2. Data collection

  • Fixtures collected via web_search with links to official league/federation/club sites (MLB.com, NBA.com, NHL.com, ATPtour.com, unitedrugby.com, ESPNcricinfo, ITTF, BWF, PSG.fr, NCAA athletics sites, etc.).
  • OLBG.com itself is not reachable from this sandbox (TLS blocked; outbound limited to GitHub/npm/PyPI hosts), so no OLBG odds were viewed or copied.
  • Where a fixture could not be confirmed by an official source, the value is left blank and flagged.

3. Originated lines

  • All betting lines (moneyline pick, American odds, handicap, match total) are originated by this session. They are analytical estimates, not quotes from any bookmaker, and not copied from OLBG or any other sportsbook.
  • Markets investigated per match: win match (moneyline), handicap (spread/line), match total (over/under line). Tournament-based sports (golf, snooker, darts, badminton, table tennis) are listed as outright-tournament markets only because individual matchups are set by draw later.
  • Each row carries one match-winner selection.

4. Confidence levels

  • high — strong verified basis (recent form, head-to-head, standings, home advantage confirmed by official sources). Includes a 30+ word human-written prediction.
  • medium — reasonable basis, some verified info, less certainty.
  • low — speculative, tournament outright, or draw-dependent matchups not yet known.

5. Predictions (high confidence only)

  • Unique, human-written, at least 30 words each.
  • Include team/player, current form, recent match history, and a clear betting selection (who will win, or over/under).
  • Built from verified information from official/trusted sources; every claim links to a source. No robotic repetition, no hallucinated stats.

6. Verification policy

  • Every table row has at least one source link.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified or partial.
  • Nothing is inferred or filled in. Unverified items are listed on the Flagged for Review page for manual review.

7. Irregularities flagged

  • Tennis: individual QF/SF matchups at Almaty, Lyon, Brussels not yet determined at collection time (draw-dependent) — left blank.
  • URC Leinster v Munster venue: BBC lists TBC; All.Rugby lists Aviva Stadium — marked partial.
  • Volleyball venues inferred as home venues — marked partial.
  • Contingent World Series games (G5–G7) only exist if required — lines left blank.
  • Rugby League Super League 2026 season completed; no upcoming fixtures in window.
