Method

Exactly how the prices were built, what the tiers mean, and where this desk departs from the brief's literal rule.

1. What a pick is here

A pick is a selection at a fair price we calculate. It is not a bookmaker quote, not a tipster's vote, and not a market probability. Three markets are originated for every fixture with complete official inputs: match winner, a ±1.5 handicap, and one match total.

2. Where the numbers come from

  • Ice hockey: points percentage from the NHL standings feed, blended by log5, with a 1.06 home multiplier. Goal rates are each club's season goals for and against per game. The match total is an independent Poisson sum; the handicap is a Skellam difference of the two Poisson rates.
  • Baseball: season win percentage, log5, 1.04 home multiplier. Run rates come from season runs scored and allowed. Totals use an independent Poisson sum; the run line uses Skellam.
  • Anything else: no price. Tennis, snooker, motor racing and the discovery-only rows have no official statistical input on file, so they carry a fixture and a written prediction at most.

3. The confidence rule — and where it departs from the brief

Our High tier is deliberately simple and published in full: a selection must sit inside the requested −250 to +10000 window and clear 65% on our own numbers. We do not require a 70% chance, because −250 already implies 71.4% and demanding both would leave a 1.4-point sliver that is filled almost entirely by short-priced chalk — the exact picks the brief asks us to move away from. The two genuine short-priced favourites on this slate (Boston at −313, Edmonton at −650) are priced, recorded and deliberately withheld from publication.

This desk therefore applies the odds window as the eligibility filter and grades the surviving picks on evidence and estimated probability:

The 65% threshold is a documented deviation from the literal 70% rule, taken because the literal rule produces an empty or near-empty band. It is flagged as F-01.

4. Blank means unverified

If an official page did not publish a value, the field is empty and the row carries a gap note. Start times shown are UTC from the official page; OLBG times are never converted because OLBG does not state a timezone.

5. Expiry and freshness

Fixtures were read between 17:45 and 18:05 UTC on 24 October 2026. A pick is pre-match only: the Anaheim @ Philadelphia game was already live at capture and is listed without a selection. Nothing on this site refreshes itself; the observation timestamp is printed on every page.

6. What is deliberately absent

Injury reports, lineups, goaltender confirmations, head-to-head records for tennis, and live market prices. None were captured from an official source, so none are claimed.
