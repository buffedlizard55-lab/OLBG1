OLBG, reverse engineered

The route structure, the 15 sport IDs, what each hub row actually contains, and what it is missing.

What OLBG is, structurally

OLBG is a tipster marketplace. Each sport has a hub at /betting-tips/<Sport>/<sportId>. Each row on a hub is one selection, not one match: the event name, the market, the most-tipped selection, the tip split (“13/19 Win Tips”, “68%”), and a relative time (“Today 15:15”). The full market set sits behind an event page.

The 15 sports in this brief map to the IDs below. Football (1), horse racing (2), American football (11), boxing (16), cycling (17) and specials (18) are out of scope and were not harvested.

What the pages do and do not give you

  • They give: event names, market names, the most-tipped selection, a tip count and share, and a display time.
  • They do not give: a timezone, a bookmaker price in the fetched body, form, injuries, or any guarantee that the fixture is on. Tip shares are votes, not probabilities.
  • They do not certify anything. That is why every fixture on this site needs an official league page before it can carry a price.

Access notes

  • Fetching is done through a page-reading tool, not a browser farm or a hidden API. No identity rotation, no proxy pool.
  • The PDC tournament page returns a consent wall in the fetched body; the darts rows are therefore discovery-only.
  • Premiership Rugby and the United Rugby Championship render fixtures client-side; both returned no fixture data (one 404).
  • The Italian volleyball league site returned only chrome. HBL returned a 404 on the German schedule path and a client-side wall on the working path.
  • OLBG's golf hub returned “No tips found.” at capture; the greyhound hub returns race cards without trap, grade or going data.

Why the desk is small

Because the alternative is invention. A hub row that says “Trentino v Cisterna, Cisterna 2/2 tips” is not enough to write a 60-word prediction with form, statistics and availability. Nine of the fifteen sports have no price in this window and three had no fixture at all, and the site says so on the coverage table and on the flagged page.

What this is. An independent research notebook that records what official sources published, what we could price from those numbers, and what we could not confirm. Not affiliated with OLBG. No guarantees, no staking advice. 18+.
