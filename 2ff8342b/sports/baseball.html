Milwaukee Brewers to win

Selection: Milwaukee Brewers to win

Milwaukee returns home for Game 2 of the World Series holding a 103-59 regular-season record, the best run differential in the majors at plus 214, and a 55-26 home mark. The Brewers have won five straight and eight of their last ten, while Tampa Bay went 43-38 on the road and 5-5 over the same ten-game span. Logan Henderson takes the ball against Drew Rasmussen, and Milwaukee's 832 runs scored are 96 more than Tampa Bay managed across 162 games. The selection is the Brewers moneyline.

Milwaukee Brewers to win

Selection: Milwaukee Brewers to win

Milwaukee's offence is the reason to take the road side in Game 3. The Brewers scored 832 runs across 162 games, 96 more than Tampa Bay, and finished 48-33 away with a plus-214 run differential that was the best in the majors. Robert Gasser is scheduled against Freddy Peralta, and Tampa Bay's 55-26 home record is the counter-argument, but the Rays managed a 5-5 close to the season.
