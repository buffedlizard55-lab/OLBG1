Ottawa Senators to win

Selection: Ottawa Senators to win

Ottawa has banked 14 points from eleven games and is 4-2-0 at the Canadian Tire Centre, while the Rangers have collected just one win in six road games despite a perfect 6-0-0 record at home. New York has scored 37 goals and conceded 29, but eight of those goals for came in a single home stand and the road split is 1-4-1. Ottawa's 31 goals for and 30 against suggest a tight game, and home ice plus the sharp travel split points to the Senators. The selection is Ottawa on the moneyline.

Nashville Predators to win

Selection: Nashville Predators to win

Nashville is not the side the standings suggest at first glance: it has conceded 30 goals in nine games, 3.33 per game, while Pittsburgh has shipped 38 in eleven, 3.45 per game, and has lost six of those eleven. The Penguins' 37 goals for flatter a 4-6-1 record, and only three of their wins have come at PPG Paints Arena in six home starts. The Predators are 1-1-2 on the road, so this is a narrow call rather than a confident one, and our fair price of -131 reflects a game that could go either way. The selection is Nashville on the moneyline.

Washington Capitals to win

Selection: Washington Capitals to win

Carolina Hurricanes to win

Tampa Bay Lightning to win

Selection: Tampa Bay Lightning to win

Tampa Bay has allowed 29 goals in ten games, 2.90 per game, against Utah's 36 in eleven, and that defensive gap is the basis for backing the road side. The Lightning are 7-3-0 overall but only 2-3-0 away, while Utah is 3-1-0 at the Delta Center and has scored 41 goals, so the risk is real. The counterweight is that Utah has conceded four or more in four of its eleven starts and lost its last game. At a fair price of -113 the Lightning's superior goals-against rate is worth the travel risk. Selection: Tampa Bay moneyline.
