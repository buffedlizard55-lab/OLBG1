F-01High

The −250 floor and the >70% rule almost never overlap

A fair price of −250 implies a 71.4% chance. Requiring both 'more than 70%' and a price no shorter than −250 leaves an effective band of roughly 70.0%–71.4%. Every genuine short-priced favourite on this slate (Boston at −313, Edmonton at −650 on our numbers) falls outside the requested odds window, so it cannot be published as a High pick under the brief. This desk therefore treats the odds window as the eligibility filter and grades confidence from the evidence, and it publishes the conflict rather than manufacturing in-band High picks.

F-02High

Twelve sports have at least one row. Only ice hockey, basketball and baseball have the official statistical inputs needed to originate a line, so nine sports are unpriced. F-03Medium

OLBG displays times without a timezone

OLBG event times appear as 'Today 15:15', 'Tomorrow 05:00' and so on, with minute offsets that do not map cleanly onto any single zone. No OLBG time is converted or presented as UTC on this desk; official start times are used wherever they exist and are left blank otherwise.
