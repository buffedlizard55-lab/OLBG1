ED71-F-01 · high · OLBG display clock
OLBG hub pages render start times in America/New_York EDT (UTC-4) with no timezone label printed anywhere. Proven by a greyhound event whose name embeds the UK clock '5:12 Towcester' rendering as 12:12 (17:12 BST = 16:12Z = 12:12 EDT); by NHL MTL@CHI rendering 20:07 against nhl.com's 8 p.m. ET; and by PDC darts rows rendering 13:15-16:45 which is 19:15-22:45 CEST, inside the promoter's published 1900-2300 CEST session.
Impact: A reader who assumes UTC or UK time reads every kickoff four hours early. All times published by this desk are converted with the documented +4h offset. — observed on olbg.com
ED71-F-02 · medium · Start-time padding
OLBG start times are padded by +5 to +7 minutes with no label. NHL MTL@CHI renders 20:07 against an official startTimeUTC of 00:00:00Z (20:00 ET); other rows show +5.
Impact: OLBG renderings are never published as the kickoff. The official time is used and the OLBG rendering is kept alongside so the padding is auditable. — observed on api-web.nhle.com
