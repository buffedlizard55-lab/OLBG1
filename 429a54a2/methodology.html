Methodology

The rules that decide what appears in a cell, and what stays blank.

1. Fixture truth

A row is accepted only if the participants, competition and date were read on a league, tour or governing-body page. OLBG supplies the route and the event name; it never certifies the fixture. If only a secondary source lists a match, the row is published blank with a flag.

2. Start times

Times are UTC. Where the official page gives only a local time with a stated venue, the row records the start and the local label. Where the page renders a time in the reader's own zone, or gives no zone, the start is left blank. No timezone is guessed.

3. Prices

A price exists only when both sides have an official quantitative input. Ice hockey: log5 on points percentage; goals for and against per game give the puck line and totals through independent Poisson scores. Handball: attack and defence ratios around the LNH mean. No home edge, no margin, no clipping. Fair decimal is 1/probability; the American equivalent is derived from it.

4. Confidence

High needs both inputs, p ≥ 0.70 and no contradicting official context. Medium is 0.60 to 0.70. Everything else is Low. The requested -250 to +10000 band is shown as a separate flag because a probability above 71.43% cannot sit inside it; those picks are disclosed instead of relabelled.

5. Blank and flagged

An unconfirmed value is empty in JSON and empty in the table. It is never filled from memory, a media report or a previous session's file. The review page lists every such row with the reason and the official link that was checked.

6. What is not claimed

No worldwide coverage, no calibration, no injury feed, no bookmaker quote, no edge, and no guarantee. The NBA and opening-week samples are specifically not used to invent strength.

Reproduction

The dataset, prices and pages are generated from one script:

python3 scripts/s429a54a2_build.py
python3 scripts/s429a54a2_validate.py

The build is deterministic for a fixed snapshot string, and the validator re-checks every table row for an official https link, every High pick for the probability and band gates, and every prediction for length and repetition.
