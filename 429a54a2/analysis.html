Where the prices come from

Ice hockey uses log5 on the league's own points percentages plus team goals for and against per game. Handball uses attack and defence ratios around the LNH league mean. No home edge, no market input and no clipping are applied. The consequence is visible: the Carolina price of -245 and the Montreal price of -250 are what the arithmetic produces, not what a bookmaker would hang.

Nothing else is priced. Tennis has a seeding order but no rating scale; cricket has fixtures but no team ratings read; the NBA has official fixtures and one-game records that cannot support a rating; rugby has line-ups but no official table read this session; snooker has frame results but no strength input. Each of those is a blank, counted on this page and explained on the sport page.
