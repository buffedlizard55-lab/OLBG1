F-CCB-02 · datasets/markets_catalog.json over-claims originated market coverage

Severity: high

The catalog lists e.g. tennis 'Set Betting & 1st Set Winner', darts 'Most 180s', baseball 'First 5 Innings', golf 'Each-Way / Top-5 Fair Odds' as originated_markets_covered, but data/lines.json (model v2.0) prices only winner / draw-no-bet / handicap / total. This session originates the derivable subset (1,619 markets) and registers the rest as investigated-not-priced in data/market_taxonomy-ccb6bde2.json.

Evidence / where to review:
  • datasets/markets_catalog.json
  • data/lines.json

F-CCB-03 · Small-sample record ratings unshrunk in v2.0 (56 ratings from <=12 games)

Severity: high

Win%/points% from 4-11 games were fed in at face value: EuroLeague 4-2 vs 2-4 (6 games) produced a 33.4-point rating gap and a HIGH-confidence 74% on Real Madrid; NHL 0.800 points% from 10 games rated 80.0. v2.1 applies empirical-Bayes shrinkage toward 50 (factor n/(n+10); composite standings ratings count games double). 12 of the 31 v2.0 highs came from such inputs; v2.1 retains 19 highs.

Evidence / where to review:
  • data/model_calibration-ccb6bde2.json
  • data/market_expansion-ccb6bde2.json

F-CCB-04 · Baseball venue double-count in v2.0

Severity: medium

World Series fixtures use home-record vs road-record split ratings (which already embed venue effects) and v2.0 then added the 0.540 home logit on top. v2.1 prices split-input fixtures at a neutral 0.500 venue term; Brewers Game 1/2 moved 1.51 -> 1.61 and Rays Games 3/4 dropped from high to medium.

F-CCB-05 · Confidence thresholds inconsistent across sports (high bar 0.580-0.780)

Severity: medium

A 58% baseball favourite was 'high' in v2.0 while an Aussie Rules favourite needed 78% and handball 76%. Thresholds were left unchanged in v2.1 for comparability, but the desk displays the pick probability next to every tier so the inconsistency is visible, and this finding recommends a uniform policy in a future model version.

F-CCB-08 · Snooker ranking curve is hot at the extreme tail for short formats

Severity: low

The WST rank curve implies #1 beats #57 at 92.8% in a best-of-7 (per-frame q~0.78). Plausible directionally but almost certainly too strong for a race to 4; refitting the slope needs outcome data the repo does not hold, so v2.1 leaves slopes unchanged and flags the tail instead of inventing a correction.

Evidence / where to review:
  • data/model_calibration-ccb6bde2.json snooker_rank_pairs

F-CCB-09 · OLBG consensus samples are often 1-3 tips; consensus % is not a calibration target

Severity: low

Of 55 comparable rows the model sided with the OLBG consensus favourite in 34; many '100%' consensuses are 1/1 or 2/2 tips, and several reference handicap/set markets rather than the winner market. Consensus is displayed for transparency but never fitted to.

Evidence / where to review:

F-CCB-10 · Best-of totals in v2.0 used an ad-hoc closeness heuristic

Severity: low

model.py priced tennis/snooker/darts/volleyball totals as 0.44+0.14*closeness rather than from the unit-score distribution, so the totals book was not consistent with the winner price. The expansion derives totals, correct scores and unit handicaps from the exact race-to-N distribution implied by the winner price.

Evidence / where to review:
  • scripts/model.py total market block
  • scripts/ccb6bde2_market_engine.py
