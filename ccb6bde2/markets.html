OLBG1 — Deep Markets Desk

Reverse-engineered OLBG coverage for 17 sports (football/soccer, horse racing, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, cycling & specials excluded). Recalibrated own lines (model v2.1) across every derivable market. Snapshot 21 Oct 2026.

Markets Explorer — every market investigated, sport by sport

The rule: a market is originated only when its probabilities are exact mathematical consequences of sourced inputs already in the verified dataset (governing-body ratings, documented margin/total distributions, official race formats). A market that needs a statistic we do not have from an official source is listed as investigated — not priced with the missing input named. We never invent a rate to fill a market.

v2.0 priced four families per match (Winner, Draw No Bet, one Handicap line, one Total). The repo's markets catalog claimed much wider “originated” coverage that the delivered lines file did not contain (finding F-CCB-02). This desk closes the derivable gap: 1619 expanded markets across 126 matches.
