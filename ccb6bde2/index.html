What changed vs the root dashboard (v2.0)

  • R1 — small-sample shrinkage: 56 ratings were built from ≤12 games (EuroLeague 6, handball groups 4, NHL 8–11). At face value they manufactured false certainty — a 74% “high” on Real Madrid from six games. v2.1 shrinks record ratings toward neutral; 12 of 31 v2.0 highs demote.
  • R2 — baseball venue fix: home/road split ratings already contain home advantage; v2.0 added it twice.
  • R3 — official ATP re-verification: Tommy Paul No.15 / Benjamin Bonzi No.100 (atptour.com, 19 Oct 2026) turns a 50/50 baseline row into a rated high-confidence pick. Four fixture ranking labels corrected; WTA rows that could not be verified stay blank.
  • Market depth: from 4 market families to a full book per match — see the Markets Explorer.

Excluded per brief (not covered anywhere in this repo): soccer/football, horse racing, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, cycling, specials/entertainment.
