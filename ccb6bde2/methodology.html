Methodology — how every price on this desk is derived

Inputs (sourced facts only)

Fixtures, rankings, records and seeds come from governing bodies (league/tour/federation sites and APIs) as recorded per-row in data/fixtures.json, plus this session's ATP re-verification from the official PIF ATP Rankings of 2026-10-19. Every match block links its official source. If a value could not be confirmed, it is blank and flagged — e.g. the WTA rankings table returned no rows (script-rendered), so Leylah Fernandez, Aliaksandra Sasnovich and Bejlek stay unrated on this desk.

Winner model (v2.1)

Logistic in rating points: p_home = sigmoid(logit(p_venue) + (r_home − r_away)/scale), per-sport scale and venue baselines in scripts/model.py, draw mass for draw-sports as in v2.0. A strength edge is applied only when both sides carry a sourced rating; otherwise the match is priced at the venue baseline and capped at LOW.

Recalibration R1–R3

  • R1 shrinkage: record-derived ratings from ≤40 games are shrunk toward 50 by n/(n+10) (composite standings-mapped ratings count each game double). Rankings that aggregate long histories (ATP/WTA/WST/PDC/ICC/IRL) are not shrunk.
  • R2 baseball: home/road split inputs priced at a neutral 0.500 venue term (splits already embed venue).
  • R3 tennis: ratings re-derived from the official ATP list of 2026-10-19.
  • Not changed: logit scales, confidence thresholds. The audit found the scales plausible (Calibration page) and refitting without outcome data would be invention, not calibration.

Expanded-market derivations

  • Race formats (darts Bo11 legs, snooker Bo7 frames, tennis Bo3 sets, volleyball Bo5 sets): the per-unit win probability q is solved from the match price by inverting the negative-binomial race formula, so correct scores, total-unit ladders and unit handicaps are exactly consistent with the winner price. Units are assumed i.i.d. (A2).
  • Margin/total sports: margin and total are independent normals with the sport's documented sigmas (A1). Winning-margin bands, alternate ladders, and team totals (score = (Total ± Margin)/2) are closed-form integrals of those normals.
  • Tennis games (A3): expected sets from the exact set distribution × 9.7 games/set, blended 50/50 with the documented baseline total; volleyball points (A4): set distribution × 44.0 points/set.
  • Half / First-5 markets (A5): expectation split pro-rata, sigma by the square root of the time share.
  • Hockey regulation: continuity-corrected normal margin (|margin| < 0.5 goals = regulation draw).

What is deliberately not priced

Any market needing a per-player rate we cannot source officially — 180s, centuries, aces, top batter, anytime scorers, player props, full-field golf/greyhound/F1 books. Each is listed in the Markets Explorer with the missing official input named. An empty price is honest; an invented one is not.

Odds conventions

Book prices include a symmetric 5% overround (fair and US formats shown alongside). Every market is a true partition: probabilities sum to 1 (validated). Lines end in .5 — no pushes.
