What was actually broken

  • Small samples at face value (F-CCB-03): 56 ratings came from ≤12 games. A 4-2 vs 2-4 EuroLeague start became a 33-point rating chasm. Fix R1 shrinks record ratings toward 50 by n/(n+10) (composite standings ratings count games double, as they already embed margin information).
  • Baseball double-count (F-CCB-04): home/road splits plus a separate home logit. Fix R2 neutralises the venue term for split inputs.
  • Missing/stale tennis ratings (F-CCB-06): fixed from the official ATP list of 2026-10-19, read this session.
  • Confidence bars are inconsistent (F-CCB-05) — left unchanged for comparability, displayed openly:
