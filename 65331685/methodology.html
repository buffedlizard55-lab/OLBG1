Line origination

  • Record markets: Beta(2,2) shrinkage on raw win rates (NHL: W/(W+L+OTL); rugby: (W+0.5D)/P), combined with log5, no home edge.
  • Runs/goals/points: paired per-game rates (attack v defence averages), independent Poisson scores conditioned on no tie for the win market, half-point totals ladders.
  • Handicaps and spreads: gaussian margins with disclosed sport deviations (NBA 11.5, NHL 1.9, rugby 14, handball 4, MLB 4.3 runs).
  • Specialist links: rank-Elo proxy for tennis, semi-clock logistic for the dogs, elite-spine index for league internationals, tournament frame share for snooker — each listed in the constants table.
