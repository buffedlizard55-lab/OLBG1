Method in brief

Record inputs are shrunk toward .500 with a Beta(2,2) prior and combined with log5; NHL overtime losses count as defeats and rugby draws split 0.5/0.5 (both disclosed). Runs, goals and points come from paired per-game rates with independent Poisson scores conditioned on no tie; fixed half-point ladders keep every two-sided market zero-vig. Handicap and total rungs use gaussian margins with the disclosed sport deviations. Greyhound prices normalize the read semi-final clocks; rugby league prices use the elite-spine representation index; tennis prices use the rank-Elo proxy shown in the constants table.
