Methodology

Source data: the repository's v3 verified fixtures, originated lines and predictions (data/v3/), themselves priced from governing-body standings/rankings with the documented v3 model. This desk applies one additional strict filter (High, >70%, −250…+10000) and republishes the surviving picks with their original unique ≥30-word predictions. No value was inferred: rows without an official source in v3 could not enter the High set. Direct sandbox fetches to league sites are TLS-blocked, so live re-fetch in this session used the page-fetch tool (NRL men's Round 2 team lists, AFLW ladder and PDC schedule pages returned successfully on 2026-10-22); all other rows retain their v3 verification-ledger status and are labelled accordingly, not re-certified.

Prices are model estimates with the v3 margin convention, never presented as market prices. This is analysis, not betting advice.
