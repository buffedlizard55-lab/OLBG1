Model & Grading Rules — methodology

Evidence-first, zero-vig fair prices, no invented inputs.

Every official URL resolves to exact host in pipeline.config.PUBLISHERS. OLBG URLs are discovery only. Values not directly observed on official publisher page remain null/blank and flagged. Originated prices labelled model estimates, never bookmaker quotes. High requires both p>0.60 and fair American inside -250…+10000, plus verified two-sided inputs and unique ≥30-word prediction.

Verification rules (non-negotiable)

  • Every data row carries at least one link to an official source (league, governing body, tour, promoter or team itself). Media outlets, aggregators and betting sites are not official sources.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source: leave it blank and flag it. Never infer, interpolate or fill it in from memory.
  • Model-derived values (originated prices) are explicitly labelled as estimates and never presented as sourced facts.
  • Nothing marked verified until official source actually fetched and read.

Pricing models

See Analysis page for per-sport model table. All models are zero-vig: decimal = 1/p, American derived from unrounded decimal, no margin, no clipping. Probability_exact preserved unrounded so reviewer can re-derive prices. Tier boundaries applied to probability rounded to 6 decimals to avoid binary noise (log5 on 4-2 vs 3-3 is exactly 3/5 and binary noise 0.5999999999999999 had silently dropped picks in earlier desk d7d19a67).
