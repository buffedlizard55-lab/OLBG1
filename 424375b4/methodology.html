Methodology

Reverse engineering OLBG

All 17 in-scope OLBG sport hubs were read live inside the harvest window. Hubs publish event cards with a most-tipped selection, a market label and a tip share (e.g. "7/8 Win Tips, 88%"). Those shares are votes, never probabilities, and never enter a price. Hub start labels are inconsistent across sports (the same site showed NHL tips labelled 19:07, Pro D2 tips 13:00 and NBA tips 00:10 — flag F-01), so they are recorded as discovery metadata only.

Verification

Every row is graded A, B or C (see sources). Class A rows were confirmed against the league or governing body's own site or API. Unconfirmed cells are blank and flagged — never inferred.

Origination model (experimental fair prices)

  1. Strength from official records with Beta(2,2) shrinkage: no draws s=(W+2)/(W+L+4) (NHL defeats include overtime losses); draws split s=(W+D/2+2)/(M+4) (rugby).
  2. Winner probability is log5: p_h = s_h(1−s_a) / (s_h(1−s_a) + s_a(1−s_h)).
  3. Where official scoring rates exist (NHL goals, rugby points), paired per-game rates feed independent Poisson scores: margins are Skellam, totals Poisson sums. These are research model lines, not bookmaker offers.
  4. Basketball line and total markets are withheld: no official scoring-variance input was read this session.
  5. Fair decimal = 1/p. American derived consistently. The −250…+10000 flag uses the unrounded price. No margin, no clipping.

Confidence

High requires all of: p ≥ 70.0%; fair American inside −250…+10000; class A/B fixture; a written prediction citing official facts. Favourites shorter than −250 are published as out-of-window disclosures. Medium is 0.55 ≤ p < 0.70 (class A/B); Low covers everything else including every class C row.

Evidence expiry

Fixture reads are treated as stale after 6 hours and statistical reads after 24; rebuilds do not rejuvenate them. Nothing on this desk is current beyond those windows.
