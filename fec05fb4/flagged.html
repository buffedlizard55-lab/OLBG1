F-FEC-01 · high severity

The >70% rule and the -250 floor overlap only between 70.00% and 71.43%

A zero-vig fair price of -250 is exactly 1.400 decimal, or 71.4286%. Any selection above that probability prices shorter than -250 and fails the requested window; any selection at or below 70.00% fails the probability rule. The desk therefore treats the window as the binding constraint and publishes bigger favourites as Medium with the exclusion reason stated. A 5% bookmaker margin makes the overlap disappear entirely on offered prices, so the band test uses the zero-vig fair price and the offered price is shown separately.

Evidence: Arithmetic identity: American = -100/(1/p - 1); 1/0.714286 = 1.400.

Carried evidence is capped at Medium even when it lands inside the band

4 rows have carried official inputs and an in-band 70-71.43% selection (darts 2, rugby-league 2). They keep their prices but cannot be High, because this desk did not re-read the source; every one is published as Medium with the cap stated on the row.

F-FEC-03 · high severity

The model is transparent but not calibrated out of sample

Every price comes from a documented logit or normal model with published constants. There is no measured hit rate for these constants and no closing-line value study. A High label means the desk's stated rule was satisfied, not that a 71% winner has been demonstrated.

Evidence: Methodology page; model constants in data/fec05fb4/desk.json method

F-FEC-13 · low severity

Desk-defined Medium and Low thresholds
