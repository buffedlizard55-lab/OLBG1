Why the evidence test is not a formality

A 70% cover on a five-point handicap is worth roughly two points of injury news. When a club has not published its Round 7 availability report, the desk cannot know whether a starting guard or a rotation big is out, so the honest label is Medium. That is the whole difference between the two tables above.
