Method

How every number on this site was produced

Four models, one gate, no bookmaker data. Everything below is reproducible from the JSON with the constants printed here.

1. Prices

For a probability p, the zero-vig fair decimal is 1/p; the offered decimal is 1/(p × 1.05). American odds are -100/(dec-1) below 2.00 and (dec-1)×100 above. Every market is two-way and the two probabilities sum to exactly 1.

2. The High gate

  • Model probability strictly greater than 70.00% and at most 71.4286% (so the fair price is inside -250..+10000).
  • Official evidence read by this session for both sides.
  • Both clubs' availability confirmed in the official report for that round.
  • The selection agrees with the model's own view of the match.
  • A unique prediction of at least 30 words naming the selection and the price.

Anything short of all five is Medium or Low, with the failing test printed on the row.

3. The models

EuroLeague

Expected points for each side = (own points scored per game + opponent points allowed per game) ÷ 2, taken from the official standings. Margin ~ Normal(σ = 11.5); total ~ Normal(σ = 16.0). No home-court edge is added beyond the scoring rates, and injuries are narrative facts, not point adjustments.

NHL

logit(0.535) + (home P% - away P%) × 100 / 42.0. Points percentage only. No goaltender, rest or travel term.

MLB

logit(0.54) + (home win% - away win%) / 40.0 on final regular-season records. No pitcher, bullpen or park term.

Carried sports

Darts, rugby league, rugby union, handball, volleyball, Australian rules, Gaelic football, cricket and motor racing use the stored official inputs from the earlier evidence file, run through the repository's logit engine. No new inputs were invented.

4. Exclusions

Football/soccer, American football, boxing, UFC/MMA, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials are out of scope by instruction and do not appear in any table.

5. Limits

  • No calibration study exists for these constants; a 71% label is a rule, not a measured strike rate.
  • No closing-line comparison, so “value” is never claimed.
  • Carried evidence was not re-read and is capped at Medium.
  • Where a source contradicts itself, the table value wins and the contradiction is flagged.
