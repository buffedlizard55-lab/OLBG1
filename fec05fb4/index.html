What the numbers say

  • Why only two Highs. 87 upcoming fixtures carry a verified start time; 39 of them are priced inside the desk's rules. Every other in-band line belongs to a game where at least one club had not filed its Round 7 availability report, or to a sport whose official source could not be re-read this session.
  • The band is a knife-edge. Between 70.00% and 71.43% the fair price runs from -233 to -250. A stronger favourite cannot be priced inside the requested window at all; the desk publishes those as Medium rather than stretching the rule.
  • Where the desk is weakest. NHL prices come from points percentage alone and disagree with the published market by up to 21 points. MLB prices use regular-season records with no pitcher information. Darts, rugby league, rugby union, handball, volleyball, Australian rules and Gaelic football rely on evidence captured earlier in the day and are capped at Medium.

Flagged before you bet

The >70% rule and the -250 floor overlap only between 70.00% and 71.43%. A zero-vig fair price of -250 is exactly 1.400 decimal, or 71.4286%. Any selection above that probability prices shorter than -250 and fails the requested window; any selection at or below 70.00% fails the probability rule. The desk therefore treats the window as the binding constraint and publishes bigger favourites as Medium with the exclusion reason stated. A 5% bookmaker margin makes the overlap disappear entirely on offered prices, so the band test uses the zero-vig fair price and the offered price is shown separately. All 14 flagged items →
