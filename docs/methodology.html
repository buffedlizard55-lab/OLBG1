Methodology — how every number was produced

Originated fair price

For a 2-way winner market with k tips for the leading selection from n total tips:

  • Model probability p = (k+1)/(n+2) (Laplace smoothing toward 50%, so 1/1 cannot produce a fake 1.00 price)
  • Originated fair decimal price = 1/p; opponent price = 1/(1-p)
  • Handball 3-way: p = (k+1)/(n+3). Multi-runner outrights/races: no win price — tip-share index only. Zero-tip rows: no price.

Winner pick rules

  • Winner-market leader → that side. Set-score leader → implied winner (Low cap). Handicap leader → "lean only", never sold as a winner bet. Totals leader → no winner pick. 1st-set leader → "1st-set lean only".

Confidence rubric

Verification standard

  • Every row links to its OLBG event page (source of record for tip data). High picks were additionally checked on official sites (NBA, ICC, PDC, WTA).
  • OLBG tipster comments are user opinion. They were never copied as facts; two were found wrong/contradictory and are flagged (F-04, F-05).
  • Bookmaker odds were not visible on fetched OLBG pages, so that field is blank on every row by design.
  • Reproduce everything: python3 scripts/build_data.py && python3 scripts/build_site.py in the repo. Full text: research/methodology.md, research/verification-log.md.
