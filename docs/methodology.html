How this was built

Methodology & OLBG reverse-engineering

1. Reverse-engineering OLBG.com

  • Sitemap index: https://www.olbg.com/sitemap.xml → sub-sitemaps including sitemap-betting-tips.xml.
  • Sport hub pattern: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id} (e.g. /betting-tips/Basketball/4).
  • Event pattern: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{Region}/{Competition}/{Match}/{market_id}?event_id={id}.
  • 20+ sports identified with stable IDs: Football 1, Horse Racing 2, Tennis 3, Golf 5, Rugby Union 9, Rugby League 10, American Football 11, Baseball 12, Ice Hockey 13, Motor Racing 14, Darts 15, Boxing 16, Cycling 17, Handball 20, Volleyball 21, Aussie Rules 22, eSports 23, Gaelic Football 25, Greyhounds 28.
  • Per-sport market maps were extracted from OLBG's own help text and event pages (e.g. a tennis event page exposes Win Match, Set Betting, 1st Set Winner, Games Won handicap and Total Games markets). Full map: data/olbg-structure.json.
  • Harvest: each covered sport's hub page was read on 2026-10-21 05:00-08:00 UTC and every listed event recorded with its OLBG event URL.

2. Verification standard

  • verified - fixture confirmed on an official source (league/tour/federation/club site) whose content we captured.
  • partial - competition/date confirmed via multiple independent listings with an official league page linked; some detail (exact tip time, standings) not captured.
  • unverified - could not be confirmed from an official source: data fields left blank and the row flagged. We never infer or fill values.
  • Every fact used in a written prediction carries its own source link. Facts that only appear in secondary coverage (e.g. NHL team records from game previews) are marked as such in notes.

3. Originating our own lines

  • We do not copy bookmaker or exchange prices. Lines are fair (vig-free) estimates produced from verified inputs: official standings and records, recent verified results, head-to-head records, seedings/rankings, and home advantage.
  • Method per market: win probability from a simple power rating built on the verified form above; expected margin → handicap line (rounded to market-standard increments); expected scoring → totals line. Decimal prices = 1 / estimated probability.
  • Where verified inputs are thin (season openers, minor leagues), lines are labelled as indicative and the pick confidence drops accordingly - that is deliberate.
  • Outright markets (golf tournaments, F1 race/championship) use verified form (defending champions, standings, seedings) and are always low-to-medium confidence.

4. Confidence rubric

  • High - multiple verified facts point the same way with a demonstrable gap (used for 9 picks; each has a written prediction).
  • Medium - verified evidence favours the pick but with real competitive uncertainty.
  • Low - thin evidence, coin-flip matchup, long-dated outright, or unverified fixture context. No write-up.

5. Limitations

  • Snapshot dataset: captured 2026-10-21 05:00-08:00 UTC; fixtures/results move fast - always confirm at the linked official source.
  • NBA openers have no 2026-27 form data yet (season began 20-21 Oct 2026), so those picks are capped at medium/low.
  • OLBG's own displayed times are unreliable (see flagged irregularities); all times here are from official sources where captured.
  • Generated predictions are betting analysis, not guarantees. 18+; bet responsibly.
