How a row reads

Two outputs per match, never mixed up. The match-winner call is the desk’s view of who wins, and it carries a grade on every priced match — including the 31 where the desk refuses a tip because no line inside the window clears a coin flip. The desk selection is the one bet the desk would actually put up: it only exists when a match-winner or handicap line is both inside −250 to +10,000 and at least 52% on our model. Where it is a handicap, the winner is still named in the call column.
