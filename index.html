What the data says

Two thirds of the card has no real edge

15 of 49 fixtures carry a sourced strength input on both sides; only 6 measure both participants outright. Everything else is a baseline price. Publishing a confident pick off a 0–0 NBA record or an unretrievable PDC Order of Merit would be fabrication dressed up as analysis, so those rows are labelled low and left honest.

Where our model disagrees with OLBG's crowd

OLBG's front page carried one in-scope pick for these fixtures: Washington Wizards money line against Milwaukee, with 9 of 11 tips (82%) behind it. Our model also makes Washington the home favourite — but only at 56%. Same side, wildly different conviction. That gap is the thing worth studying, not the pick.

Fatigue, not form, decides Game 2

Milwaukee closed out the Dodgers on 14 October and had nine days to set a rotation. Tampa Bay needed six games and a postponement to get past Cleveland, finishing on 19 October. The official postseason log makes that gap checkable rather than anecdotal.

Nine sports are a genuine coverage gap

Gaelic football has a verified negative — the GAA calendar shows nothing between 21 and 25 October. The other gaps (rugby league, handball, volleyball, tennis, AFL, esports, greyhounds) are unfilled because no official source was retrieved, not because nothing is scheduled. They are listed on the Flagged page.
