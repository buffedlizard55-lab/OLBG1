Why 8 picks, not 75

The brief asks for five picks a day in each of fifteen sports. That requires five fixtures per sport that are both confirmed by a governing body and carry enough official form data to price. This session confirmed 8 such fixtures across two sports; every other OLBG row is a listing we could not prove, so it sits on the watchlist with no price attached.

We did not fill the gap with invented form, assumed line-ups or a favourite's short price dressed up as confidence. The alternative would be a full-looking table and a worthless one.
