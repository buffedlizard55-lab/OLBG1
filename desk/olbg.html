OLBG structure

What the site exposes, and where it misleads

Reverse-engineered from the fifteen public sport hubs on 24 October 2026. OLBG is used for discovery only. It is never treated as an official fixture or statistics source.

Routes

Every sport follows /betting-tips/{Sport_Slug}/{sport_id}. Slugs are case-sensitive; a wrong case returns a soft error with HTTP 200. Event pages add competition and market segments, for example /betting-tips/Ice_Hockey/NHL/NHL/NY_Rangers_@_OTT_Senators/13?event_id=200008. The event_id is the stable key and is what this desk records.

Five behaviours that change what you see

  1. Times are unlabelled and inconsistent. Baseball rendered "Today 20:00" for a 00:00 UTC start; snooker rendered "Today 14:00" for a 13:00 UTC start. Treat every OLBG clock as an advertisement, not a timestamp.
  2. Zero-tip fixtures disappear. OLBG hides events with no open community tips. Rugby League currently shows only 2027 outrights for that reason; it is not evidence that no match exists.
  3. Hubs truncate. The default listing stops at twenty rows before "Load More Tips", so a hub is not a complete slate.
  4. Tip percentages are votes. "15/26 Win Tips · 58%" is a share of community selections, not a probability. This desk does not use it.
  5. Golf can be empty. The golf hub returned "No tips found" even though tours are running, so OLBG emptiness is a fact about OLBG, not about the sport.

What this means for the brief

The brief asks for matches "listed on OLBG" and for those matches to be priced. Those two requirements diverge: OLBG lists many events its own tipsters have not verified, and it hides others entirely. This desk uses OLBG only to enumerate what is advertised, then insists on a governing-body confirmation before any price is attached. That is why the Matches page has two tables and why 78 advertised rows carry no price.

What this is. Independently calculated experimental prices, not bookmaker offers. No staking advice, no guaranteed returns. Not affiliated with OLBG.
