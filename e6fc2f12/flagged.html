5. Model-risk register

  • Small samples. NHL clubs have played 7–12 games. Shrinkage mitigates this but cannot remove it.
  • Poisson independence. Correlated scoring, empty-net goals and overtime rules are not modelled. Totals near the ladder's edges are the most exposed.
  • Rugby standard deviations. 13.5 (margin) and 16.0 (total) are assumptions. Handicap and total prices move a lot if they are wrong.
  • No home edge. Home teams are, if anything, underrated by this desk.
  • Stale-by-design snapshot. Every price is tied to a stated read time. A later lineup or injury changes the picture and this snapshot will not know.
