Finding 1 — the brief's band and the brief's threshold cannot both be generous

At a probability of 70% the fair decimal price is 1.4286, which is −233 American. At −250 the implied probability is 71.43%. So the instruction "greater than 70% chance to win" combined with "odds between −250 and +10000" defines a window of roughly 1.4 percentage points. This desk applies both conditions literally, which is why 18 selections qualify rather than several hundred. The alternative would be to label heavy favourites "High" and quote them at prices the band forbids — the failure mode several earlier desks in this repository were flagged for.

Finding 2 — coverage is uneven and the desk says so

Broad, machine-readable official feeds (NHL, MLB, URC) produced 73 priced fixtures. Client-rendered or consent-walled official pages produced no fixture at all for eleven categories. Publishing an unpriced fixture list would have been easy; it would also have been guesswork about start times and participants, so those categories are gaps instead.

Finding 3 — the model is honest about what it is not

  • No home advantage is inserted, because no official home/road split was read. That biases estimates toward 50%, which suppresses High labels rather than manufacturing them.
  • Beta(2,2) shrinkage stops a 3-0 start reading as a 100% team. It is a disclosed choice, not a sourced fact.
  • Independent Poisson goals is a stated approximation. Real hockey goals are not independent and empty-net effects are not modelled.
  • The rugby-union margin and total use a normal approximation with standard deviations of 13.5 and 16.0 points. Those two numbers are assumptions and are labelled as such everywhere they appear.
  • No market quote exists in this dataset. "Edge" is therefore unknowable here, and the desk does not claim any.

Finding 4 — where the earlier desks and this one disagree

A prior desk in this repository carried a High pick on the New York Islanders moneyline at a fair −250. Recomputing from the same official NHL records read today (Islanders 5-3-1, 27 goals for, 19 against) does not reproduce a 70%+ win probability for that fixture in this model. The discrepancy is a modelling difference — different shrinkage and no home edge here — not proof that either number is wrong. It is recorded on the Flagged page as an open review item rather than silently overwritten.
