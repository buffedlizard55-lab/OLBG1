How the price is built

Win rates are Beta(2,2)-shrunk and combined with log5. Hockey and baseball totals and handicaps come from independent Poisson scoring counts; rugby union handicaps use a normal approximation with a disclosed standard deviation. Fair decimal is 1/p with no margin, and no market quote is invented.
