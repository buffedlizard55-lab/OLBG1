Methodology

The exact arithmetic, the assumptions, and the parts that are guesses labelled as guesses.

The rule set

  1. Discovery. OLBG's sport hubs are used only to know which categories exist and to link back for comparison. No OLBG number is treated as a fixture fact.
  2. Verification. Every fixture, record and start time comes from the league, tour or governing body. If a field is not in the payload it stays blank and the row is flagged.
  3. Origination. The desk prices its own lines. It never copies a bookmaker price, and it publishes no "market quote" field it did not read.
  4. Grading. High = model probability above 70% and a fair price inside −250…+10000. Medium = 65% or better. Low = everything else.
  5. Publication. Every High selection carries a unique prediction of at least 30 words naming the selection, the participants, the current form and the recent results.

Mathematics

strength = (wins + 2) / (games + 4) # Beta(2,2) shrinkage
p(A beats B) = pA(1−pB) / [pA(1−pB) + pB(1−pA)] # log5, no home edge
λ_home = ½(goals_for_home/G + goals_against_away/G) # Poisson rate
fair decimal = 1/p ; American = −100p/(1−p) for p ≥ 0.5

Handicaps are derived from the same scoring distributions: the probability that the home side wins by two or more goals is the Poisson mass where home goals minus away goals is at least two. Rugby union uses a normal approximation on the expected margin and total, with standard deviations stated in the risk register.

What "High" does and does not mean

It means the model puts the selection in the 70.0–71.4% slice and the price the brief asks for can express that. It does not mean the desk has beaten a market: no market price was read, so no edge was measured. It does not mean the model is calibrated: no out-of-sample scoring has been run on this model version. The label is a threshold, transparently applied.

Exclusions

Soccer, American football, boxing, UFC/MMA, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials are excluded by the brief and appear nowhere in this desk. The GAA check found only a Shinty-Hurling International, an excluded sport, so Gaelic football has no in-scope fixture in this window.
