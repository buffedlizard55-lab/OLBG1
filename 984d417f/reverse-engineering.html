1. URL & Next.js SSR Architecture

Every sport hub resolves at /betting-tips/<Sport_Name>/<sportId> with competition sub-trees under /betting-tips/<Sport>/competitions and match pages keyed by internal numeric event IDs. Hurling and Gaelic Football share /betting-tips/Gaelic_Games/25, requiring fixture-level code filtering to exclude Hurling cleanly.

2. Crowd Vote Ratio vs Calibrated Probability

OLBG's "Confidence %" divides raw tipster count on one side by total tips placed on that market (e.g., 10/13 = 77% on a near-pick'em NBA game), conflating popularity bias with win probability and ignoring implied vig completely.
