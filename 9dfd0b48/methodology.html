Methodology

Harvest. The 17 in-scope OLBG hubs were read in one pass (see Sources). Event IDs, listed times and the visible tip consensus were recorded verbatim. OLBG times render in US Eastern (UTC-4) without a label; every conversion was cross-checked against an official local start time.

Verification. A fixture is published only if the pairing and start were read from the governing body's own page: ATP orders of play, the WST match list, the EuroLeague Game Center and the MLB Stats API. Anything else is blank and flagged.

Tennis. P(A) = 1 / (1 + (ptsB/ptsA)^0.55) from the 19 Oct ATP list. Game handicap steps with the favourite's probability (1.5 → 5.5); total games 22.5 leans under as the match gets more lopsided. No surface, injury or head-to-head adjustment is made because none was read from an official source.

Snooker. Same power-ratio form on official ranking money (exponent 0.45). Players outside the 36-name list are priced at the 36th-place ceiling, so the favourite's probability is a lower bound. Where neither player is on the list the market opens even and the pick is Low.

EuroLeague. Margin = 0.6 × (home point differential per game − away differential per game) + 3.0 home edge; win probability from a normal with σ 11.5; spread rounded to the half point; total from the two teams' average combined scoring.

MLB. Pythagorean win rate (exponent 1.83) from 2026 regular-season runs, combined by log5, plus 3.5 points for the home side; run line at −19 points; total 8.5 leaning with combined runs per game.

Prices. American odds with a 4.5% overround split across both sides. Confidence: High ≥70%, Medium ≥58%, otherwise Low. If a High pick's money line is shorter than −250 the featured selection moves to the handicap so that every written-up play sits inside the −250/+10000 window.

Limits. This is a model, not a market. Prices are estimates for comparison, not quotes. See Flagged for Review for everything that could not be verified.
