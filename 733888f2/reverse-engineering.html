Inside OLBG

How the hub pages behave, read live at 2026-10-24T22:28-22:44Z, and what that means for anyone trying to find out what the matches are.

Route pattern

Each sport hub is https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<sport_id>, and each event carries its own route of the form /betting-tips/<Sport>/<Group>/<Competition>/<Home>_vs_<Away>/<sport_id>?event_id=<n>. The hub shows only one selection per event — the most-tipped outcome — with a tip count and a percentage of tips, never a price history.

The five things that make it confusing

  • The tip percentage is a share of member votes. It is not a probability and it is not odds.
  • Times are printed on a geo-adjusted clock ("Today", "Tomorrow") with no timezone, and NHL games all print the same 19:07.
  • Finished matches can still be listed as upcoming: both darts ties offered on the hub had already been played on the PDC's own schedule (F-05).
  • Handball's most popular selection is "Tie" on most fixtures (F-06).
  • Four hubs served no events at all tonight: Golf, Snooker, Motor Racing and Rugby League (the last has outrights only).

What this desk takes from OLBG, and what it refuses

OLBG is used for one thing: naming the events and the markets that exist. Every number published here comes from the league, tour, club or governing body, linked on the row. Tip shares never enter a price.
