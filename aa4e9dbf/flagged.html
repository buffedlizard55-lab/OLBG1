Site irregularities found while reverse-engineering OLBG

  • OLBG start-time bug (systematic): OLBG's displayed event times are inconsistent across sports. Examples captured on 21 Oct 2026: NBA Bucks@Wizards shown "Today 19:10" vs official 7:00 PM ET (= midnight UK); NHL Flyers@Sabres shown "Tomorrow 00:37" vs official 7:30 PM ET (= 00:30 UK); F1 US GP shown "25 Oct 16:00" vs official 20:00 GMT; darts shown "Tomorrow 13:15" vs ~18:00 UK session start; snooker shown "05:00" vs 10:00 UK. By contrast, Austrian ICEHL and several tennis times matched UK time exactly. Treat OLBG times as unreliable; all times on this site come from official sources.
  • Expired/in-play listings: the OLBG tennis hub listed matches with tip times that had already passed at capture (site has a "Show Expired Events" toggle). Statuses are marked accordingly.
  • eSports hub empty: OLBG's eSports page showed "No tips found" on 21 Oct 2026 - no esports events included.
  • Rugby League: no match-level events listed; only 2027 futures (Super League, NRL, State of Origin).
  • EuroCup fixture conflict: OLBG lists U-BT Cluj-Napoca vs London Lions on 21 Oct while contemporaneous EuroCup schedules list London Lions vs Cedevita Olimpija that evening - flagged for review.
  • OLBG Motor Racing content claim: OLBG's own F1 calendar text states the Bahrain Grand Prix is staged at Sepang in Malaysia for 2026 - an unusual claim we did not attempt to verify (irrelevant to the US GP pick) and flag purely as site-content irregularity.
  • OLBG odd club name: OLBG lists a PlusLiga volleyball side as "Jelcz Laskowice", which does not match known 2026/27 PlusLiga club names - fixture identity flagged.
