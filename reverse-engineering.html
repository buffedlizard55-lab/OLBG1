What the public interface exposes

The inspected betting-tip sitemap supplies sport routes with category IDs and slugs. Event cards link using an event_id query parameter. A discovery key combines sport ID and event ID; a label is never official fixture verification.

/betting-tips/{sport}/{sport_id}
/betting-tips/{sport}/{competition}/{sport_id}?event_id={event_id}

Competition routes and pagination must be followed when explicitly linked. A hub is not the full competition universe. The bounded collector records unvisited links; blocked or JavaScript-only extraction does not prove zero fixtures.

Tip shares are not probabilities

The observed darts card displayed Gerwyn Price with 16 of 20 win tips (80%), and Niels Zonneveld with 14 of 18 (78% rounded). These are community selections, not calibrated likelihoods or bookmaker prices. They never feed the model.

Respect robots

The read policy disallows /api/, /storage/app/json_data/, private routes and Python-urllib. The Python client advertises and honors that identity. It declines denied requests rather than replacing its user agent or using a hidden endpoint. Retrieval-tool observations are a selected historical sample, not an automatic-refresh claim.
