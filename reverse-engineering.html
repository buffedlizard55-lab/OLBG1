How OLBG is laid out, and where the picks are

Where the picks live on OLBG

A sport hub is a list of event cards. Each card is one match, carrying a competition line, a selection the community is backing, and a tip-share such as 5/5 Win Tips · 100%. Every card links to its own event page through an event_id query parameter, and that event page is where the individual tips and comments sit.

/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id}
/betting-tips/{Sport}/{competition}/{Sport}/{match_slug}/{sport_id}?event_id={id}

The hub is a discovery surface, not an authority. The percentage on a card is the share of that site's members who backed the selection, not a price and not a probability, and it is never used as either in this project.

How to read this desk instead

  1. Start on Today's summary: the high-confidence picks are the first table on the page.
  2. Open Written predictions for the full analysis of every pick.
  3. Open Every match to see all fixtures, including those we refused to price.
  4. Open Flagged for review for everything that is missing.
