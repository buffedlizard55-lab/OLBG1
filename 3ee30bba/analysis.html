Resolving the >70% vs −250..+10000 High-Confidence Problem

A zero-vig probability above 70.00% corresponds to fair decimal odds shorter than 1.4286 (−233 American), whereas the −250 odds floor corresponds to 1.4000 decimal (5/7 = 71.4286%). Systems that equate High confidence with >70% win chance flood their picks with −350 to −1200 moneylines.
