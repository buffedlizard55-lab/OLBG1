Why OLBG itself is not a source

OLBG is the site being reverse-engineered: it is how the sport taxonomy and the event list were discovered (olbg.com/sitemap-betting-tips.xml and the 24 numeric sport hubs). It cannot verify a participant, a venue or a start time, so it never appears in a row’s source cell.

What counts as certified

  • Fixture facts — participants, competition, venue, start — read from the governing body.
  • Form inputs — ladder position, win-loss record, ranking, seed or points differential — read from the same source and quoted verbatim in the row.
  • Prices — the desk’s own estimate, never sourced, always labelled as such.

One desk, one snapshot. Every row links to the official league, tour or governing-body page it was read from. Prices are the desk's own fair-price estimates, not quoted market odds. Nothing here is a tip-off service or a guarantee.
