Methodology

1. Discovery

OLBG.com's 15 in-scope sport hubs were harvested on 2023-10-23 ~19:30Z via the search/render layer. OLBG tip shares are votes, not probabilities, and OLBG's clock renders Eastern Daylight Time to this session. OLBG is discovery only: every published fact is checked against an official governing body, league, tour or team source this session.

2. Fixture verification

Participants, competition, venue and start times were confirmed on the official source listed per row. Missing values stay blank and are flagged — never inferred or filled in. Fixture evidence expires six hours from the capture time.

3. Originated lines

Every market price is our own zero-vig fair price (1/p). No bookmaker quote, margin, clipping or missing-input default is used. Confidence tiers: High-A = ≥70% and fair US price in −250…+10000; High-B = 62–70% in band; Medium = 50–62% or price-capped chalk above +10000/−250; Low = below 50% or without two official inputs.

4. Line setting

The desk sets its own spread and total lines: the line is chosen (half-point search) so the model's cover/over probability sits at 0.66, the midpoint of the High window. Winner prices come from official win percentages and rankings; sport-specific markets use official records, series results and Order of Merit / world ranking data.

5. Predictions

Every High pick carries a unique hand-written prediction of at least 30 words: team, player, current form, recent match history, verified match statistics and injuries, with an obvious selection (win / over / under). Sources are linked per row.

6. Limits

Coverage is partial: no claim of worldwide fixtures, every bookmaker market, calibrated sport-specific models, current player availability or verified bookmaker odds. Unsupported values remain blank and flagged.
