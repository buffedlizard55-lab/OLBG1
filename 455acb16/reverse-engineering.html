Market investigation—not assumed availability

OLBG’s public guides describe winner, handicap and totals for several team sports; tennis adds set betting, first-set and games markets; darts documents legs/sets, correct-score and 180s; field sports use outright and placement markets. These descriptions are not evidence that any particular line is currently open.

Each listing’s market panel separately records the missing inputs for winner, handicap, total and sport-specific derivatives. Golf/F1/greyhound fields are never replaced by a fabricated two-participant contest. Team totals or player props are not labelled OLBG-supported without a live event check.

All 15 main hubs plus Hurling, NHL and MLB subroutes were actually checked. The retriever exposed no live card rows. The cause is unknown; a location, tip-count or hidden-feed explanation has not been established.
