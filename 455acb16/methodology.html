Methodology & limits

Source observations expire six hours from 21:34:10 UTC, not from the time this page is opened.

Evidence has expired. Pre-match estimates and selections have been withheld. Official links remain for rechecking.

Reproducible calculations, deliberately conservative evidence gates, and no hidden fallback ratings.

What is—and is not—supported

  1. Public OLBG pages discover routes and documented market types. Empty retrieved cards do not prove a zero-event schedule, market closure, or a zero-tip suppression mechanism.
  2. Only non-null fields transcribed from actual official reads are accepted. Unlabelled clocks and Not Before bounds never become exact UTC. A season schedule is not automatically a current match-state check.
  3. Evidence lasts six hours from the lower observation bound, statistics 24 hours. A price ends at the earliest fixture deadline, statistical deadline or match start. Rebuilds do not refresh evidence.
  4. NHL winner uses Beta(2,2) shrinkage plus log5 on compatible W/L/OTL. No home advantage or injury coefficient is guessed. Handicap/totals remain withheld without an appropriate score/settlement treatment.
  5. MLB research uses independent Poisson score rates averaging a team’s own runs/game and its opponent’s runs-allowed/game, conditional on no tied final score. All MLB prices require ifNecessary=N. Fixed ±1.5 run lines and a mechanical three-point half-run totals ladder are research lines, not available quotes.
  6. All originated estimates are Low. Medium/High require a future version with measured temporal hold-out calibration, full current match context and confirmed placement/quote evidence—not a boolean flag or price alone.
  7. Any future High write-up must be unique, at least 30 words, name the betting selection and participants, support current form/recent history/statistical/player claims and relevant injuries with official references, and omit internal processing terminology. Missing information blocks prose; it is never filled to reach a word or match quota.
  8. This namespace is a selected session import, not a daily automated feed. Browser expiry is automatic. The repository’s existing scheduled refresh remains separate and partial. No promise of five picks or complete continuous coverage is made.

Unknown values are null in JSON and blank in source-value cells, with explicit flags. CSVs are dated as-of artifacts; live claims require a new official observation, not a file timestamp.

Source and collector boundaries

No OLBG private API, restricted JSON storage, proxy, agent impersonation or credential collection. No paid feed or API key is required.
