Price and confidence interpretation

Fair decimal = 1 / p; American is derived from the unrounded chance and rounded only for display. The target window is 1.40…101.00 decimal (−250…+10000 American). Nothing is clipped or renormalized to get into that window.

With an additional p > 70% requirement, the overlap is only 70%…71.4286%. This desk does not invent an alternate spread, generic scoring total or small variance to hit that narrow range. The strongest estimate can remain Low or lie outside the target band.

There is no edge calculation without an available, time-stamped quote and matching settlement. A blank quote is not zero margin or a recommendation to bet.
