Methodology

How this desk avoids invented certainty

The desk is deliberately narrower than a global fixture feed. It records what the official pages actually named during one window, makes source limits visible, and does not convert tip-share popularity into a price or probability.

Inclusion

  1. An exact fixture/event is retained only when an official page named it.
  2. The source page and observed-result note accompany each row.
  3. Publisher time labels stay unchanged when no timezone was stated.
  4. OLBG-discovered rows have a separate discovery label and can never become official through repetition.

Price and confidence

  1. Markets are investigated individually: winner, handicap/spread, total and any sport-specific family seen on the event page.
  2. A blank market means no price/pick was produced; it does not mean the market is unavailable.
  3. The single Snooker fair display is an explicit 60/40 research construction from stated WST results, with no vig and no bookmaker claim.
  4. High requires more than a 70% number and an in-band American display: it also needs current context, credible inputs and market eligibility.

Scope and expiry

Source observation window: 24 Oct 2026, 17:09 UTC (single session). This is not a promise that a schedule, player availability, market, tip count or result remains current after that window. The desk contains no injury assertion unless an official source item was read; no such item was used in the published Low note. It provides no staking instruction, quote comparison, execution path or personalized advice.
