F-321-01 — The brief's two pick rules cannot both be met outside a 1.4-point window high

The brief asks for selections with a greater than 70 per cent win chance AND a price between -250 and +10000. A 70.0 per cent chance is a fair price of -233; a -250 price is a 71.4 per cent chance. So the two rules together admit only probabilities between 70.0 and 71.4 per cent. On today's readable data no selection lands in that band, and we publish that rather than relabel a shorter price as compliant.
