How each family is built

Match winner (moneyline)

NHL: a logistic blend of each club’s official points percentage and goal differential, shrunk toward .500 by eight games of prior, plus a fixed home-ice term. MLB: the log5 formula on the official home and away win percentages. Both are then compared with any published price.

Handicap / puck line ±1.5

A Poisson goal distribution per side, with the two scoring rates scaled until the moneyline matches the line above, so the handicap cannot contradict the winner market. Both sides are published.

Match total 5.5 and 6.5

Taken from the same Poisson distribution. We publish both sides of both lines and do not cherry-pick the number that looks best.

What we did not originate

Player props, correct score, first goalscorer, quarter/half markets and anything needing lineup or injury data. No official lineup or injury feed was readable, so those families are withheld rather than guessed.
