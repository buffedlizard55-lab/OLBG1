Rules we applied to sources

  • Betting sites, tipsters, aggregators and news outlets are not official sources and are not cited as evidence.
  • OLBG tip percentages are the share of community votes on a selection. They are never converted into a probability and never used to set a price (flag F-321-05).
  • A fact that was not read this session is not published. The two exceptions are clearly labelled: our own originated prices (estimates) and the arithmetic in the methodology page.
  • Where a page rendered but did not contain the fixture — a 404, a cookie wall, a JavaScript shell — that is recorded as a failed read, and the sport gets no line rather than a guess.
