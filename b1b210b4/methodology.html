How the prices are made

The rule, in one line

High is 60% or better, Medium is 55–60%, and a pick must sit between −250 and +10000. Nothing shorter than −250 is published, which is the brief's objection to short-priced favourites turned into a filter. The chance shown is the originated probability, not a bookmaker's, because no bookmaker price was read.

Method

Version

originated-1.0

Band

A selection is in band when its fair American price is between -250 and +10000, i.e. an implied probability from 0.0099 to 0.7143. Nothing shorter than -250 and nothing longer than +10000 is published as a pick.

Tiers

High: originated probability >= 0.60 and in band. Medium: >= 0.55 and < 0.60. Low: >= 0.45 and < 0.55. Below 0.45 no pick is published.

Pick Rule

For each priced fixture the pick is the in-band outcome with the highest originated probability; if no outcome reaches 0.45 the fixture carries no pick and says why.

Team Model

Scoring rates are shrunk toward a sport prior before use: rate = (published total + prior_games * prior_rate) / (games played + prior_games). NHL prior 3.00 goals in 10 games; MLB prior 4.50 runs in 20 games; handball prior 30.0 goals in 5 games. Basketball uses the published win-loss record shrunk to .500 with a 10-game prior.

Margin Model

Expected goals or points for each side = mean of that side's shrunk scoring rate and the opponent's shrunk conceding rate, plus or minus half of the published home edge. NHL 0.30 goals, MLB 0.20 runs, handball 1.20 goals. The margin is the difference; a normal distribution gives win, handicap and total probabilities. Score standard deviations: NHL 1.8, MLB 4.2, handball 8.0; totals 2.3, 4.6 and 9.0.

Mlb Note

MLB run line is fixed at 1.5 runs, which is the standard posted line.

Withheld

Player props are withheld on every fixture because no confirmed lineup or availability list was read. Correct-score and period markets are withheld because no per-period distribution was read. NBA pointspread and game-total markets are withheld because no official NBA points-for or points-against table was captured; the NBA rows therefore carry money-line prices only.

Honesty

Every number quoted in a prediction appears on the official page linked on the same row. Where a page did not publish something - NBA start times, NBA points, injuries, lineups - the field is blank and the gap is named on the fixture row and the Flagged for review page.
