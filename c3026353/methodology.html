Methodology

Discovery

The 15 requested sports map to OLBG hub slugs Tennis/3, Basketball/4, Golf/5, Cricket/7, Snooker/8, Rugby_Union/9, Rugby_League/10, Baseball/12, Ice_Hockey/13, Motor_Racing/14, Darts/15, Handball/20, Volleyball/21, Gaelic_Football/25, Greyhounds/28. Hub cards supply the candidate list only. Hub quirks are documented on the reverse-engineering page.

Confirmation

Each fixture/fact is checked against a governing-body page (league, tour, federation, board, club for club championships). Media and betting sites are never treated as sources. Read method and time are logged per source; rows whose official body could not be read carry flags and blank values rather than memory-filled guesses.

Pricing (our own lines)

Winner probabilities come from small documented engines per sport: log5 on source-checked records (NHL, NBA with Beta(2,2) shrinkage for the 2-3 game sample), logistic strength gaps (club/player sports, scale documented per fixture), Gaussian margins (rugby, gaelic, RLWC, cricket run margins), best-of-n frame rates (darts, snooker), and normalised weight fields (golf, motor racing, greyhounds). Fair decimal = 1/p. Handicap and total lines are desk estimates on documented scales. No bookmaker price enters the priced tables; partner odds that appear on official league pages are quoted only as market context on the analysis page, with their official URL.

Confidence gate

  • High — p > 0.70 AND fair American inside −250…+10000 AND an official source read this session.
  • Medium (chalk cap) — p > 0.70 but the fair price is shorter than −250; published with its probability, never promoted.
  • Medium / Medium (evidence gap) — 0.55 ≤ p ≤ 0.70 (or in-window p above 70% without a read source).
  • Low — below 0.55, or field-market selections at ordinary probabilities.

The odds-window arithmetic (flag R-C-01)

The brief asks for High picks above 70% whose odds sit between −250 and +10000. Those constraints overlap only in a fair-price band 1.4 American points wide (−250 to −233.33, p from 70.0% to 71.43%). Rather than quietly relax either rule, this desk publishes strict-gate High picks in that band and shows every stronger selection below it as chalk-capped with the probability spelled out.

Time handling

OLBG renders times on an unlabelled UTC−4 edge clock (and occasionally in a different clock again — flag F-C-12). Discovery times were converted (UTC = displayed + 4h) and then checked against official schedules wherever an official page carried a time. Official time always wins; conflicts are listed on the flagged page.
