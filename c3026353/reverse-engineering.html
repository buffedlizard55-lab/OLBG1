OLBG.com reverse-engineering notes

Site shape

  • Hub pages: olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<id> — slug routing is case-sensitive (OLBG-QUIRK-01).
  • Event cards: /betting-tips/<Sport>/<Tour>/<Event>/<Name>/<market_id>?event_id=<id> — stable numeric event_id used as our discovery key.
  • Markets named on cards include: Win Match (2-way), Win Match, Money Line, Pointspread, Game Totals, Win Race, Fastest Qualifier, Win Tournament, To Win, Win Match (cricket), Daily Races, Win Tournament/Grand Final Winner.
  • Cards show a most-tipped name with a share percentage — these are tipster votes, not prices or probabilities; they are discovery context only on this desk.
