Reverse-engineering OLBG

OLBG publishes server-rendered hub pages with no public JSON API. Sport hubs follow /betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<sport_id>, the slug is case-sensitive, and the id is the taxonomy key. The fifteen required sports map to the routes below.

What the routes yielded

  • Both hubs read this session returned "No tips found" for the current window. The Italian SuperLega hub was empty and the volleyball id resolved, so the route exists and the emptiness is a content gap, not a dead link.
  • Hub listings are paginated and truncated behind a "Load More" control, so an empty first page cannot be read as an empty sport. Football, American football, boxing, MMA, hurling, cycling and horse racing were deliberately skipped per the brief.
  • OLBG's clock renders in the reader's local zone with no zone label. Times are never taken from OLBG here; every start is republished from the official page.

Consequences for this desk

Because discovery returned nothing usable, fixture enumeration was driven by the official competition calendars for the fifteen sports, and OLBG's role is reduced to the taxonomy map above. That is disclosed rather than papered over: the desk does not claim to have found these fixtures on OLBG.
