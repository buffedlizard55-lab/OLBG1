3. Known irregularities in the source material

  • Premiership Rugby. premrugby.com/fixtures-results is client-rendered and returned zero fixture rows. All five rugby union rows are cross-checked against the competition's official broadcaster and are marked accordingly. They should be re-checked against the club sites before being promoted to verified.
  • PDC. pdc.tv serves a cookie-consent interstitial in place of article bodies. The European Championship first-round draw was read from the publisher's own indexed copy of the PDC schedule release. The eighth Thursday tie (Ross Smith v …) was truncated and is therefore omitted rather than guessed.
  • Snooker session times. WST renders times in the viewer's local timezone. Only the date is recorded; the displayed times are reproduced verbatim with no timezone claim.
  • Snooker venue. The Waterfront Hall is the Northern Ireland Open's long-standing venue but is not stated on the fetched schedule block. It is flagged on every snooker row.
  • Formula 1. Round 18 (United States Grand Prix, 23–25 October) is verified, but the circuit name and race start time were not captured. Both are left blank rather than assumed.
  • NBA. The eleventh game on 21 October (Sacramento at LA Clippers) had its game ID and arena truncated on the source page. The fixture is verified; the ID is not, so the row links to the day's games page instead.
  • MLB Game 5 onward. Games 5–7 are conditional (“if necessary”) and are excluded from the priced set.

4. Judgement calls

  • Unranked snooker players. WST publishes a top-36 table on the fetched page; everyone below it is placed in one bounded bucket at rank 60. This is a conservative bound, not a measured ranking, and it is disclosed on every affected row.
  • Match totals. Every total line is the league baseline assumption. It is priced pick'em precisely because no match-specific scoring input was sourced. Do not read those prices as a view.
  • Confidence tiers are sport-relative. A 59% baseball favourite is tiered high; a 59% basketball favourite is not. The thresholds are printed on the Methodology page.
