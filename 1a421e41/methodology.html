How every number on this site was produced, and which numbers are ours rather than sourced. The distinction is the whole method: facts come from governing bodies, probabilities come from us, and the two are never blurred.

Ladder gridhalf-point onlyHandicap and total rungs sit on a half-point grid only, so no rung can push. Handicap rungs step 1.0 from 0.5; total rungs step 1.0 either side of the centre rounded to the nearest half point.

Pricing maths

decimal  = 1 / p
american = -100 * p / (1 - p)      when p >= 0.5
         = +100 * (1 - p) / p      when p <  0.5
5% book  = the same formula applied to p * 1.05  (printed, never gating)

handicap rung h, margin centre m, sigma s:
  p(home covers -h) = 1 - Phi((h - m) / s)
  p(away covers +h) =     Phi((h - m) / s)

total rung x, total centre t, sigma_t s:
  p(over  x) = 1 - Phi((x - t) / s)
  p(under x) =     Phi((x - t) / s)

What we refuse to do

  • No value is inferred, interpolated, or carried over from a bookmaker. If an official body did not publish it, the cell is blank and the row is flagged.
  • No media outlet, aggregator, reference site or betting site is treated as official, even when it is the only source that has the number.
  • No prediction is written over evidence weaker than Class A. Prose over thin evidence reads as confidence and is not.
  • No official-vs-official disagreement is silently resolved. Both values are published, the reconciliation is shown, and the one that survives arithmetic is the one used.
  • No rung is placed on an integer line, because an integer line can push and a push is not a priceable outcome.
