The -250 to +10000 high-confidence band

Why the band is only 0.0143 wide in probability, how we resolved the contradiction, and what it rejects.

The brief asks for high-confidence picks on matches priced between -250 and +10000. On a zero-vig basis that is a band only 0.0143 wide in probability, and the two tests pull against each other. This page shows exactly how we resolved it and why the outcome is what it is.

The contradiction, and how we settled it

At zero vig, p = 0.700 prices at -233 and p = 0.714286 prices at exactly -250. So “greater than 70%” and “between -250 and +10000” intersect in the band [0.7000, 0.7143] — about 17 American points wide.

At a 5% overround the two tests cannot both bind: p = 0.700 books at -277, already outside the window. We therefore gate on the zero-vig price, which is the price we originate ourselves, and print the 5% book equivalent beside it on every table for comparison. Nothing is ever graded on the book price.

The four gates

4WindowOur zero-vig price must satisfy p ≥ 0.700 and land between -250 and +10000. Among qualifying rungs the tightest to our own centre wins.

Why almost nothing clears it — granularity, not conviction

A handicap rung steps probability by 0.3434 / sigma per half point. Whether any rung lands inside a 0.0143-wide band is therefore governed by the sport’s scoring variance, not by how strong the favourite is. This is the single most important structural finding on the desk.
