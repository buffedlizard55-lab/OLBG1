F-1A-GRAN-01 high confirmed model property all

The high-confidence window is granularity-limited, not conviction-limited

The window p >= 0.70 AND price >= -250 spans only 0.0143 of probability. A handicap rung steps probability by 0.3434/sigma per half point, so whether any rung lands in the band is governed by the sport's scoring variance rather than by how strong the favourite is. At sigma 11.5 (basketball) a qualifying rung exists almost always; at sigma 2.30 (darts legs), 2.0 (snooker frames) or 1.15 (volleyball sets) it almost never does. This is why every High selection on this desk comes from basketball, while genuine 0.75-0.79 favourites in darts and snooker are rejected for landing a few American points short of -250.
