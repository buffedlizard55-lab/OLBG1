What OLBG.com actually is, structurally, worked out by harvesting it rather than by reading documentation. Everything here was observed directly on 2026-10-22T07:24:00Z.

URL architecture

sport hub        https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<sport_id>
competition      https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<Comp_Slug>/<id>
tip card         https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<Comp_Slug>/<Event_Slug>/<id>?event_id=NNN

example          https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/Basketball/4
                 https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/Snooker/8
Requesting /Snooker/1 is served /Snooker/8: the numeric id in the path is the sport id, and OLBG redirects a wrong id to the right slug rather than erroring. The betting-tips sitemap is dominated by Horse Racing and the excluded sports and is a dead end for enumerating fixtures — the sport hubs have to be harvested one at a time.

Card field semantics

Field on the cardWhat it actually meansObserved behaviour
Day labelRelative day bucket, not a dateValues are Today and Tomorrow; no absolute date is published anywhere on the hub.
ClockLocal start time in US Eastern Daylight Time, unlabelledProved by an exact −6h offset against official CEST kick-offs on all 8 EuroLeague fixtures (F-1A-CLOCK-04).
CompetitionFree-text league or tour nameNot a controlled vocabulary; the same competition is spelled differently across sports.
FixtureParticipants separated by vs, v or @All three separators occur. @ appears on North American sports and reverses the home/away order.
Market chipThe market the consensus tip refers toEmpty on all 6 Gaelic Football cards (F-1A-MARKET-01), so the tipped market is not machine-readable there.
Win tips / Total tipsCount of tips on each marketCounts, not prices. OLBG publishes no odds on the hub.
Consensus sharePercentage of tips on the leading selectionPrinted blank, not zero, whenever the win-tip count is 0 (F-1A-SHARE-01).
Value RatingOLBG’s own 1–5 star scorePer OLBG’s hub copy: consensus share compared against bookmaker implied probability.
ExpertsTipsters with a high strike ratePer OLBG’s hub copy: high strike rate plus profitability over the current month and the last six months.

The tip-gated blind spot

An OLBG card exists only once at least one tipster has posted on that fixture. A fixture with zero tips is invisible — there is no empty card, no placeholder, and nothing in the page source to enumerate. OLBG is therefore not a fixture list; it is a list of fixtures somebody has tipped.

Proved twice against official slates on this harvest:

  • Ice hockey. NHL’s official schedule API reports numberOfGames: 12 for 2026-10-22. OLBG’s Ice Hockey hub renders 7 cards, all SHL or Liiga, and none of the 12 NHL games. (F-1A-NHL-01)
  • Basketball. EuroLeague’s official Round 7 has 9 upcoming games. OLBG renders 8; Paris Basketball v Valencia Basket, the round’s closing game, is absent. (F-1A-BBALL-01)
  • Two whole sports. The Aussie Rules hub (id 22) and the eSports hub (id 23) both render “No tips found.” and no fixtures at all. (F-1A-EMPTY-01)

Practical consequence for anyone consuming OLBG programmatically: absence of a card is not evidence that a fixture does not exist. It only means nobody has tipped it yet.

Empty-hub rendering defect

On a hub with no fixtures, OLBG’s sports-menu chip falls back to the literal text All Sports instead of naming the sport, so the page does not identify itself. (F-1A-CHIP-01)
