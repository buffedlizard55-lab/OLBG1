Analysis

What the snapshot actually contains, measured rather than asserted. Every figure on this page is computed from data/final/bundle.json, the same file the tables are rendered from, so the analysis and the numbers on the other pages cannot drift apart.

Headline observations

  • 10 of 120 priced matches reach High confidence (8%). The spread is not even: Rugby League 3, Aussie Rules 3, Basketball 1, Handball 1, Ice Hockey 1, Darts 1.
  • 31 matches produce no bet (26%) because their best in-window price is at or below a 52% coin flip. By sport: Tennis 6, Snooker 6, Volleyball 6, Gaelic Football 4, Cricket 3, Basketball 2, Darts 1, Handball 1, Ice Hockey 1, Rugby Union 1.
  • The ladder is the same shape in every sport: 15.3 handicap rungs and 7.3 total rungs per priced match, 26.5 prices per match on average. Only the units change (points, goals, runs, sets, frames, legs).
  • 2254 of 3176 prices are on the underdog side of a coin flip, which is what a ladder looks like: every rung has two sides and the long side is quoted, not hidden.
  • 2,881 of 3,176 prices sit inside the brief window (91%). The ones outside it are shorter than −250 and are published for reference only; they can never become a selection.
  • Data completeness: 99 rows were read from an official page on 22 October, 28 are carried from 21 October, and 40 rows carry an explicit flag. 14 rows have no UTC clock because the governing body publishes a date or a local time without a timezone.

5. What this analysis does not say

  • It is not a profitability study. No bookmaker price is loaded anywhere in this repository, so there is no comparison between our fair price and a market price, and no return on investment can be computed.
  • It is not a settlement record. None of these fixtures has been played at snapshot time, so no selection has a result.
  • It is a completeness and consistency study: whether the markets, the window, the grades and the sources all agree.

Per-match detail is on all matches; every individual price is on markets; the archived 21 October board keeps its own analysis page for comparison.

One desk, one snapshot. Every row links to the official league, tour or governing-body page it was read from. Prices are the desk's own fair-price estimates, not quoted market odds. Nothing here is a tip-off service or a guarantee.
