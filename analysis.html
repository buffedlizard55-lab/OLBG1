Methodology and line-by-line findings

Fixture facts are read from governing-body / league / tour sources (links on every row). Prices are independently originated: a logistic rating from official standings/rankings inputs, Gaussian margin projection for handicaps, Poisson-derived totals — calibrated so fair decimal = 1/probability. No bookmaker prices are copied and no missing input is defaulted.
