Method, prices and the band

How every number on this desk was produced

Ice hockey

Goals are treated as two independent Poisson counts. Each club's goals-for and goals-against rate is first regressed onto the league average of 3.00 goals a game with twelve games of prior weight, because a rate built on eight to twelve games is noisy:

rate = (rate × games + 3.00 × 12) / (games + 12)

The home side's expected goals are then the average of its own scoring rate and the visitor's conceding rate, times 1.03; the visitor's are the mirror, times 0.97. From that grid we can price the moneyline, ±1.5 and ±2.5 puck lines, −1/+1 and −2/+2 Asian handicaps with pushes voided, and totals from 4.5 to 7.5 — all from one set of expectations, which is why the total and the moneyline never contradict each other.

Basketball

Only wins and losses were captured, so the rating is a shrunk win rate with a ten-win, ten-loss prior at .500, and the win probability is a logistic function of the rating gap plus a 3% home edge. The expected margin is the probit of that probability times a 12.5-point standard deviation, and the spread ladder comes off the same distribution. Totals are withheld — see Flagged.

Prices and the band

Every market is normalised so its two sides sum to 100%. That fair probability is converted to a American price and then checked against the brief's window:

Why high picks are scarce, in one paragraph The brief asks for two things at once: a selection with more than a 70% chance, and a price inside −250 to +10000. A 70% chance is −233. The band stops at −250, which is 71.4%. The overlap is 1.4 points wide. Of the 208 prices this desk set, exactly three land in that sliver, and all three are published as the high-confidence picks. Nothing was re-priced by hand to manufacture more.

Grading: High above 70%, Medium 60–70%, Low 52–60%, and any selection priced shorter than −250 is shown but never picked.

What would change the answers

  • Confirmed starting goaltenders for either crease. None was read, so none is used; a backup start is the single biggest unmeasured input on every hockey row here.
  • Injury reports for hockey, cricket, volleyball or handball. The NBA filing is the only availability document on the desk.
  • Score-level scoring data for the NBA, which would let the totals market back on to the board.
