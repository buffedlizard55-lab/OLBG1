Medium OLBG data quality · F-04

Medium Method · F-11

The brief's two High-pick rules barely intersect

A selection with a 70% chance has a fair price of about -233; the band's shortest allowed price, -250, implies 71.4%. Requiring both 'more than 70%' and 'inside -250..+10000' therefore leaves a 1.4-point window, and only three of this desk's 88 originated outcomes land in it. The three High picks here are those, plus any other line that lands there after normalisation; nothing was moved into the tier by hand.

Sources: NHL

Medium Model input · F-12

Basketball records are two games deep

Every NBA club has played between one and two games. Records that short carry almost no information, so each is shrunk toward .500 with a ten-win, ten-loss prior before it is used. That is why no basketball pick reaches the medium tier on record alone and none reaches high at all.

Sources: NBA

Low Provenance · F-13

OLBG tip shares are not probabilities and were not used

OLBG prints the share of member tips on each selection (for example '10/12 Win Tips, 83%'). Those are votes, not prices, and no number on this desk is derived from them. They appear on the sport pages labelled as OLBG's own tally so the reader can see the difference.
