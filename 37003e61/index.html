How to read a pick

Selection is the exact bet, including any handicap line, so “Edmonton Oilers -0.75” is not shortened to “Edmonton”. Fair price is our own zero-vig price. Chance is the probability implied by that price. Tier is High only when the chance is above 70% and the fair price is inside -250 to +10000.

The two rules intersect in a 1.4-point window, so High prices cluster between -235 and -248. That is a property of the brief, disclosed in F-3700-01.
