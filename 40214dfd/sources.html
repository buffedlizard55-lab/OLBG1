What “official” means here

The publisher must be the league, tour, governing body or event organiser itself: NHL.com, MLB.com, EuroLeague Basketball, the World Snooker Tour and the GAA. A page that merely reports on those bodies does not qualify, however reliable it is, because the desk cannot verify that the report matches the primary record.
