Findings

F-402-01 · The brief's two High rules only intersect between 70.00% and 71.43% severity: high

-250 is decimal 1.40, i.e. 71.4286%. A fair price for any probability above that is shorter than -250, so no selection with a genuine chance above 71.43% can sit inside the brief's window. This desk publishes High only inside the true intersection and lists every near miss at -251 or shorter on this page. It does not relabel a -311 favourite as a High pick.

Evidence: arithmetic from the brief, illustrated by the near-miss table below

F-402-02 · High lines are authored alternate lines, not the model's single strongest view severity: medium

Each High selection sits on an alternate total, team total or puck line that the desk generated in half-point steps around the model expectation. The probability is a model output for that rung; the rung was chosen because it satisfies both brief conditions. The moneyline view for the same fixture is published separately and keeps its own, usually lower, tier.

Evidence: methodology and the per-fixture winner picks

F-402-03 · Basketball handicaps rest on an assumed spread, so they cannot be High severity: medium

EuroLeague margins are modelled as Normal with sigma = 12.0 points. That parameter is assumed, not measured in this session, so every basketball handicap outcome is capped below High even when its probability lands inside the window.

Evidence: https://www.euroleaguebasketball.net/en/euroleague/news/game-center-round-7-el2627/

F-402-04 · Snooker clocks carry no timezone, so eight matches stay unpriced severity: medium

WST prints match clocks in the viewer's local time without an offset. Two 08:00 matches were already marked live. Without a timezone and without an official ranking table in this session, no snooker line is originated and no match is claimed as a pre-match selection.

Evidence: https://www.wst.tv/

F-402-05 · Two NHL fixtures are missing goal inputs, so only the winner market exists severity: low

Los Angeles (1-4-2) and Calgary had no goals-for / goals-against row in the retrieved standings chunk. Their games are published with a winner market only; puck line, total and team total are withheld rather than estimated.

Evidence: https://www.nhl.com/standings/2026-10-21/wildcard

F-402-06 · Baseball lines ignore the postseason context that actually decides the games severity: low

The run model uses final regular-season team aggregates. Starting pitchers, bullpen availability, park and rest are not captured, so all five World Series rows stay Low and their totals and run lines are research lines only.

Evidence: https://www.mlb.com/standings/

The three largest misses, in context

Boston Bruins to win (-252 fair)

Boston's record model probability is 71.6%, which values the Bruins at -252 on a fair basis: one American point shorter than the brief's -250 floor. The selection clears 70% but cannot be published as High without breaking the price rule, so it is listed here instead.

New York Islanders to win (-311 fair)

The Islanders' 5-3-1 record against Los Angeles' 1-4-2 gives a 75.7% modelled chance, but Los Angeles goals-for and goals-against were not present in the retrieved standings chunk, so no goal-based market could be originated and the moneyline itself prices beyond the window.

Panathinaikos AKTOR Athens to win (-544 fair)

A 5-1 record against Baskonia's 1-5 produces an 84.5% modelled chance. That is a genuine strong view, but its fair price is far outside -250..+10000 and the official preview gives scoring averages for only one side, so no alternate line was originated to bring it into the window.
