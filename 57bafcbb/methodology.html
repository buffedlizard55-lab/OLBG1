Desk 57bafcbb - methodology

How the lines, the confidence labels and the market families are built.

Line origination

For every priced fixture the desk reads the official record and form line for both sides and writes its own estimate of the probability that the selected side wins. The estimate is then converted into an American price with p / (1-p), so the price is the desk's own and no bookmaker odds are read anywhere in this pipeline. The two-way book is completed by giving the other side the remainder, which keeps the book fair: the sum of the two implied probabilities is exactly 1.

Confidence labels

  • High - the originated estimate is at or above 60% and the fair price sits inside the -250 to +10000 band. This window produced 5 such pick(s).
  • Medium - the originated estimate is at or above 55% and the fair price is inside the band.
  • Low - estimate below 55%, or a fair price outside the band. A price below -250 is downgraded to Medium even when the estimate is very high, because the band is the desk's discipline against backing extreme chalk.

Markets

Three families are investigated per priced fixture. The win market carries the full estimate. The handicap line is -1.5 for baseball and ice hockey and -1.0 for tennis, darts, snooker, cricket and rugby league, priced at a reduced share of the win estimate because a handicap has to cover more than the outright. The total line is set at the venue scoring average for the sport and priced at 55/45. Where the official source does not give the desk a basis for a market, the cell is left blank rather than filled in.

Discovery-only fixtures

A fixture found on the OLBG hub but not backed by an official feed the desk could read is published as a directory entry. It carries a link to the official page a human should open and appears on the flagged page. It is never priced, because pricing it would mean inventing a number the desk cannot support.

What this desk did not do

  • It did not read bookmaker odds. Every price is arithmetic on the desk's own estimate.
  • It did not fill in a statistic it could not read. Blank cells stay blank.
  • It did not treat the OLBG hub as an official source. The hub finds fixtures; official pages confirm them.
  • It did not price the motor-racing or golf outrights as two-way books. They are multi-runner markets and are published as Low confidence outrights with the field-level estimate stated.
  • It did not claim a fixture was confirmed when the official feed only rendered a JavaScript shell. Those are on the flagged page.
