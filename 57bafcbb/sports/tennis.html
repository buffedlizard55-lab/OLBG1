tn-almaty-final

Pick Danil Medvedev - High - estimate 62.0%, fair price -163.

Danil Medvedev is the higher-ranked player and he reached this ATP Almaty final the harder way, beating Alexander Bublik in three sets, 7-5 3-6 6-1, after a break-exchange opening set. Medvedev's serve and his ability to extend rallies from the baseline give him the edge against Nikoloz Basilashvili, who has to generate his own pace and who conceded breaks in both of his matches this week. Basilashvili beat Stefanos Tsitsipas to reach the final, so he is not a free pass, but Medvedev's semi-final win was built on taking control of his own serve. Back Danil Medvedev to win and lift the trophy at -163, a High confidence pick.

tn-lyon-final

Pick Tommy Paul - High - estimate 60.0%, fair price -150.

Tommy Paul has been the most consistent player on the ATP Lyon draw and he made the final by beating Ben Shelton in straight sets. Lorenzo Musetti reached the title match by beating Flavio Cobolli 6-4 6-2, so both men are playing confident tennis, but Paul's all-court game and his serve-plus-one pattern are harder for Musetti to handle on an indoor hard court. Paul's route to the final has required fewer break points than Musetti's, which points to a more controlled service game. Take Tommy Paul to win at -150 - the desk's High confidence call on this title match.

tn-brussels-final

Pick Jan-Lennard Struff - Medium - estimate 57.5%, fair price -135.

Jan-Lennard Struff reached this ATP Brussels final by beating Jaume Munar in the semi-finals, while Tallon Griekspoor had to fight past Terence Atmane to take the other side of the draw. Struff's game is built for this match-up: a heavy first serve and a forehand that punishes weak returns. Griekspoor is a solid all-rounder but he has not faced a serve of that quality this week. The gap between them is small enough that the title is genuinely open. Back Jan-Lennard Struff to win at -135, the desk's Medium confidence view on this match-up.
