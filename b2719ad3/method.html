Auditable prices.

All prices are fair estimates originated from the listed evidence. They are not copied odds, OLBG community support or claims of market availability.

01

Two-way winner screen

For official games played G and wins W, each team receives a Beta(2,2)-shrunk rate:

r = (W + 2) / (G + 4)

The home rate rH and away rate rA enter log5 without a hidden home adjustment:

pH = rH(1-rA) / [rH(1-rA) + rA(1-rH)]

NHL games played includes overtime losses, matching the league’s win-percentage basis. NHL moneyline includes overtime; its scoring derivatives use regulation-only independent Poisson scores.

02

Scoring & derivative screens

home mean = (home GF/G + away GA/G) / 2
away mean = (away GF/G + home GA/G) / 2

Independent Poisson score matrices produce three-way outcomes, home handicaps and totals. The total line is rounded to the nearest half point. This is an approximation: no unlisted home, lineup or injury adjustment is inserted.

Volleyball winners use match records. Set derivatives use separately shrunk set shares and best-of-five enumeration. Baseball averages overall, Pythagorean and venue-split differentials equally; named starters remain context rather than an undocumented adjustment.

03

From probability to fair odds

p ≥ 0.5: -100p / (1-p)
p < 0.5: 100(1-p) / p

American prices are rounded to the nearest whole number after calculation. The High gate is applied to that originated price; no probability or price is clipped to make a selection qualify.

04

High means every gate

  • Probability strictly above 70%
  • Fair American odds from -250 through +10000
  • At least six comparable games per participant
  • Confirmed future UTC start
  • Official availability context
  • Unique natural analysis of at least 30 words
  • No prohibited prediction language

Medium requires at least six games per side and a selected probability of at least 55% unless all High gates pass. Everything else is Low. At most five High rows per sport are allowed; there is no minimum.

Scope discipline

What is not priced

Started events are excluded at the snapshot. Unconfirmed clocks remain blank. Missing goals, complete runner cards, comparable records or confirmed pairings suppress the affected market. One- and two-game samples cannot be High. OLBG pagination and later Load More content are not claimed as an exhaustive global fixture census; the official rows captured here are shown line by line with their limits.
