Model

Canonical evidence-v1.0, inherited unchanged: Beta(2,2) shrinkage of sealed official season win/loss records followed by log5 for two-way winner markets; MLB score markets use paired official regular-season run rates with independent Poisson scores conditional on no tie. Fair decimal = 1/p, no margin. American odds are derived from the unrounded decimal.

Confidence gates applied by this desk

GateRule
ProbabilityStrictly >70% (0.7000 exactly fails)
Price windowOriginated American price between −250 and +10000 inclusive
CalibrationValidated out-of-sample calibration required — evidence-v1.0 has none, so High/Medium cannot be issued
ContextVerified player availability, current form and recent match history required — not sealed in the snapshot, so this gate also blocks High

All priced selections therefore remain Low. This is a deliberate evidence decision, not a missing feature: raising a tier without calibration would be exactly the >70%-shortcut the canonical methodology rejects.
