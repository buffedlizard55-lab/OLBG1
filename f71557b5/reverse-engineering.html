Known OLBG behaviours carried forward from the reverse-engineering record.
  • Sport hubs truncate at roughly twenty cards, so a hub listing fewer events than the governing body publishes is normal — snooker (2 of 8) and ice hockey (0 of 12 NHL games) are both truncation-plus-coverage gaps, not evidence the fixtures do not exist.
  • Start times are printed with no timezone label and are silently localised to the reader's GeoIP, so a clock scraped from a hub cannot be trusted as UTC. This desk stores the official body's own clock and leaves start_utc blank where the governing body published only a local time.
  • Each-way-only outright markets render a 0 / 0 Win Tips division-by-zero display, which is why outright cards are treated as event cards here rather than as priced markets.
