F-F7-01 · FINDING · policy — The brief's two High-confidence rules can only both hold in a 1.4-point probability bandThe brief asks for High-confidence picks above 70% to win AND inside the -250 to +10000 price window. A 70.0% chance is a 1.4286 fair price (-233) and a 71.43% chance is exactly 1.40 (-250), so at a zero-vig fair line the admissible High band is model probability 70.0% to 71.43%. Any book-margin line pushes it out of the window entirely (a 5% overround makes 70% price -277). This desk publishes the zero-vig fair price as the originated line, shows the 5% book-equivalent alongside it, and tests the window against the fair price. Every selection above 71.43% is published in the 'Rejected Highs' table with its price instead of being quietly graded High.
Source: https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/OLBG1/f71557b5/methodology.html
