F-F7-01 — The brief's two High-confidence rules can only both hold in a 1.4-point probability bandThe brief asks for High-confidence picks above 70% to win AND inside the -250 to +10000 price window. A 70.0% chance is a 1.4286 fair price (-233) and a 71.43% chance is exactly 1.40 (-250), so at a zero-vig fair line the admissible High band is model probability 70.0% to 71.43%. Any book-margin line pushes it out of the window entirely (a 5% overround makes 70% price -277). This desk publishes the zero-vig fair price as the originated line, shows the 5% book-equivalent alongside it, and tests the window against the fair price. Every selection above 71.43% is published in the 'Rejected Highs' table with its price instead of being quietly graded High.
Official source: https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/OLBG1/f71557b5/methodology.html
F-F7-NHL-01 — Every NHL goals-for/against value was discarded after a reconciliation failureThe standings payload at api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/2026-10-21 was read through a paginated text extraction. Points, games played, wins, losses and overtime losses reconcile for all 24 clubs used on this desk (points / (2 x games played) equals the published pointPctg, and W + L + OTL equals games played). Season goals for and against do NOT reconcile: the New York Rangers object prints 17 season goals for alongside 33 goals for in its own last-ten block, which is impossible. Rather than trust some goals values and not others, all of them were discarded, the NHL model was rebuilt on points-per-game only, and no NHL match total is priced. This is a defect in how the payload was read, not in the league's data.
Official source: https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/2026-10-21
