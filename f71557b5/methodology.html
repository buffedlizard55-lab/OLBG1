Methodology, constants and the price-window policy

How every price on this desk was produced, and which numbers are sourced facts versus labelled modelling choices.

1. The five rules

  • Official sources only. A fixture, record or ranking counts once it has been read on the governing body, tour, federation, league or club's own page. 100 such pages were read on 2026-10-22.
  • Blank beats wrong. Any value not read on an official page is left empty and flagged — see Flagged for Review.
  • Assumptions are labelled. Home-advantage constants, spread values and format assumptions are printed in full below and repeated in every market's basis string. They are never presented as statistics.
  • One-sided ratings are refused. Where only one participant has a sourced input the line falls back to a neutral baseline and confidence is capped at Low. That is why the four RLWC2026 fixtures, both NBA games, the EHF Kolstad tie and two cricket rows carry no High pick here.
  • Every High pick gets its own written preview — 30 words minimum, naming the side, its form and its recent results, and the selection itself.

2. Line origination and the price window

The originated line is the zero-vig fair price, 1 / p. The 5% book-equivalent 1 / (1.05 p) is printed beside it for comparison only.

Why zero vig. At a 5% overround a 70% chance prices at 1.3606 (−277), already through the brief's −250 floor, so "above 70%" and "inside −250…+10000" cannot both bind on a margined line. At zero vig, 0.700 prices at −233 and 0.7143 prices at exactly −250, so the two rules are jointly satisfiable in the band 0.700 ≤ p ≤ 0.7143. This desk tests the window against the fair price and publishes the margined equivalent next to it. Recorded as F-F7-01.

High confidence requires all four: row status verified · a sourced rating on both sides · model probability ≥ 0.700 · fair price inside −250…+10000.
Medium = in-window and ≥ 0.55. Low = in-window and ≥ 0.52, or any row whose ratings are one-sided. No bet = nothing in-window reaches 0.52.
Rung choice: among in-window rungs at or above 0.700 the tightest line wins, not the highest probability — a 71% call on a 3.5-point handicap is a real bet, a 71% call on a 66.5-point alternate is an artefact of the ladder. No model-calibrated alternate lines are generated.

4. Markets investigated, and which are priced

Priced: match winner (or draw-no-bet where the competition can level), handicap ladders on the sport's own unit — goals, points, runs, frames, legs — and match totals for the NHL and MLB only, because those are the only competitions where both sides' scoring rates were read on an official page.

Investigated, not priced (missing official input named per sport on the coverage page): match totals everywhere else; correct score and frame/set score; first-set, first-try, first-touchdown and player props; golf, Formula 1 and eSports outrights. Each is left blank with the exact missing input recorded rather than filled from a league-average constant.

Rejected rungs: any handicap rung whose model probability falls outside 5%–95% is dropped, so no degenerate price is published on either side of a mismatch.
