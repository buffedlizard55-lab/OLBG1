The −250 … +10000 window, enforced in code

This page is the answer to the brief's central complaint: that most "high confidence" picks are simply anything above 70%, which prices shorter than −250 and carries no value. Here is the arithmetic, and both sides of the ledger.

The window arithmetic. At a zero-vig fair line a probability of 0.700 prices at 1.4286 (−233) and 0.7143 prices at exactly 1.40 (−250). So the two rules the brief states — above 70% to win, and inside −250…+10000 — can only both hold in the band 0.700 ≤ p ≤ 0.7143. Add a bookmaker margin and the band empties completely: at a 5% overround 0.700 prices at 1.3606 (−277), already through the floor. That is why this desk originates at zero vig and publishes the 5% equivalent alongside rather than pretending both rules can bind on a margined line. Recorded as finding F-F7-01.
