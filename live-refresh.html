What this refresh does and does not claim

  • Refreshed: OLBG's own tip counter and most-tipped selection for every card that joins to a dataset row. Each refreshed row stores olbg.captured_utc and the superseded value in olbg.consensus_prior.
  • Unchanged: kick-off times, venues, records, ratings, prices and confidence tiers. Those still come only from the governing-body sources recorded on each fixture.
  • Not claimed: that an upstream-only card is real, scheduled, or correctly named. OLBG is a discovery surface, not an official source, so those rows stay unverified and unpriced.
  • Known upstream limits: OLBG truncates each hub at roughly 20 cards behind a "Load More Tips" control and renders US-Eastern clock strings without a timezone label, so "not in capture" is logged as a coverage gap, not as an expiry.
