Event pages

  • Hub pattern: /betting-tips/<Sport>/<id> — e.g. /betting-tips/Tennis/3.
  • Event pattern observed on the tennis hub: /betting-tips/Tennis/All_Tennis/All_Events/<Player_A>_vs_<Player_B>/3?event_id=902207 — a sport slug, an “All_Events” bucket, the two names, then a numeric event_id.
  • Each listing shows a headline market label (“Win Match”), an n/m Win Tips count with a percentage, and a comments count. The percentage is the share of member tips on that selection — a popularity count, not a win probability — and several Basel listings showed “100%” from a single tip. This desk never converts it into a price.
  • Hub clocks are display artefacts: every Basel first-round match was stamped “26 Oct 06:00”, and one listing (“Ben Shelton vs Casper Ruud”) did not match the ATP's official draw at all (flag F-FF-10). Fixtures, pairings and times on this desk therefore always come from the official source, never the hub.

What we take from OLBG, and what we never take

  • Taken: that a match and a market type are being offered for tipping (so the selection is genuinely “available on OLBG”).
  • Never taken: scores, records, odds, tip percentages, tipster comments — none of them are official facts, and none appear in any price or prediction here.
