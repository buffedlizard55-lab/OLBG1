United States GP - Circuit of the Americas, 56 laps

▶ Pick: Kimi Antonelli to win (Race winner)

Kimi Antonelli to convert pole position into victory in Austin, a low-confidence play because even a polesitter wins only about a third of modern Grands Prix. The Mercedes man took pole with a 1:34.035, nearly two tenths clear of Max Verstappen, and arrives as championship leader with a chance to settle the title on Sunday. Leclerc's practice pace and Verstappen's racecraft keep this honest at a plus price.

Australian GP - Phillip Island

▶ Pick: Marc Marquez to win (Race winner)

Marc Marquez to win at Phillip Island after one of the great wet-weather qualifying laps. Marquez crashed early in the session, sprinted back for his spare Ducati and then produced a 1:34.203 that left brother Alex 1.494 seconds behind, with title rival Jorge Martin only fourth. The Spaniard has won this race five times, but a slippery island circuit keeps even a lap like that below evens.
