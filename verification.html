Verification

What “verified” means here, which page was read, when it was read, and what this integration pass could and could not check for itself.

Two different things are being measured.
Verified (99 rows) — the participants, competition, venue and start were read from an official page on 22 October 2026 by the session that built this snapshot.
Carried (28 rows) — the row keeps the official URL it was read from on 21 October, but was not re-read for this snapshot. Carried rows can never be graded High.

This pass’s own limits

No official page was re-fetched during the integration pass. The sandbox used to consolidate this site can reach github.com, api.github.com and the package registries only; every league, tour and governing-body host was unreachable. Nothing on this site is presented as re-certified by this pass. What was checked offline, and is re-runnable, is:
  • every price reproduces from the model and the exported per-row assumptions;
  • US odds are the exact conversion of the decimal beside them, and no High pick sits outside −250 to +10,000;
  • every one of the 127 rows carries at least one HTTPS link to a host on the official allowlist;
  • every high-confidence prediction is a unique 30+ word preview whose figures appear in that row’s sourced input;
  • all 3,182 prices and every internal link on every page resolve.
