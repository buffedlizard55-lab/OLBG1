Verification contract

  1. OLBG routes are discovery only.
  2. A fixture is published only after its fields are observed on an official league, tour, federation or governing-body page.
  3. Unknown values remain blank and carry a coverage note or flag.
  4. Model-derived values are estimates, never sourced facts.
  5. Expired desks are historical leads, not fresh evidence.

MLB independent Poisson v1

For away team A and home team B, λA = ((RS_A/G_A)+(RA_B/G_B))/2 and λB = ((RS_B/G_B)+(RA_A/G_A))/2. Independent Poisson score grids produce winner, nine-inning, run-line, match-total and team-total probabilities. Nine-inning ties are split by expected-run share for the match-winner estimate. Integer total prices condition on no push.

No pitcher, park, weather, lineup, rest or postseason adjustment is inserted. Conditional Game 5 is not priced.

EuroLeague log5 v1

Official records are Beta(2,2) shrunk, then combined using log5. No home advantage is inserted. Exact 50/50 games show No winner preference. Spreads and totals are withheld because paired margin/scoring inputs are incomplete.

Price and confidence arithmetic

Fair decimal = 1/p. American prices derive from the unrounded probability with no margin or clipping. High requires p > 0.70 and -250 ≤ fair American ≤ +10000. Because fair prices shorter than −250 correspond to probabilities above 71.4286%, the literal High band for favourites is narrow.

The strongest qualifying outcome per fixture is featured. Winner preference has its own Low/Medium/High label; derivative High selections do not change that winner label.

Prose gate

Every featured High has a unique 30+ word preview. Automated checks require team, player, current-form and recent-history evidence categories, an obvious selection, official fact links and no repeated eight-word passage across previews. Roster presence is never described as confirmation of a start.
