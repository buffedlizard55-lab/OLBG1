Route grammar and scope

The sitemap exposes numeric sport IDs and /betting-tips/<sport>/<id> hubs. Event links add competition path segments and event_id. Cards expose tip counts and market labels; those are community inputs, not official fixture evidence or bookmaker odds.

The visible page includes a Load More Tips control, so one extraction is not treated as a complete event census. No hidden/private API or access-control bypass is used.
