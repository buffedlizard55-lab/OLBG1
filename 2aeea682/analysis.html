Model risk

The 8.7531 expected total is calculated from regular-season scoring and allowance rates. Starting pitchers, bullpens, lineups, park, weather, rest and postseason behaviour are not in the model. The 70.04% result should be read as a fragile model output, not a verified 70.04% real-world chance.
