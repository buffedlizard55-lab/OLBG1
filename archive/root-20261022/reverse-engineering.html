Evidence-led snapshot · 2026-10-22 UTC

How OLBG discovery was mapped.

An empirical reading of the public taxonomy and rendered category content, not a claim to have replicated OLBG’s private systems.

Observed public surface

All 17 eligible category hubs were opened at their numbered routes. Rendered cards supply event labels, competition context, displayed times and tip or market snippets. The route IDs and official review destinations appear on Coverage. Load More and unread chunks mean the captured front page is not the complete event database.

Extraction contract

  1. Use the category route as discovery, never as an official record of a participant’s form.
  2. Resolve the actual event and participants against a league, governing body or official tournament source.
  3. Record field-specific evidence, observation precision, competition format, timezone and source limitations.
  4. Do not interpret tip shares as independent win probabilities or call a fair model line a bookmaker quote.
  5. Keep actual market availability and settlement separate. A winner, handicap, total or player prop needs its own matching contract; discovery text alone is insufficient.

Known failure modes

OLBG display clocks disagree across pages and greyhound event labels. Aussie Rules and eSports returned “No tips found”; this does not show that no real fixtures exist. Some official destinations return navigation, consent screens or broken dynamic lists. These are recorded as blockers, not successful verification.

Excluded categories

Soccer / football, American football, boxing, UFC / MMA, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials / entertainment are excluded from canonical sport coverage. Gaelic football remains included as its distinct OLBG category, as does Australian rules.

Collection limits

Private endpoints, pagination payloads, bookmaker integrations, every odds market and OLBG’s internal ranking / tipster algorithms have not been fully reverse-engineered. The current release does not claim otherwise. Public evidence is collected without bypassing access controls.
