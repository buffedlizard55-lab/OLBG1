Executive summary

Do not rely on T496 pick TN04 or its four High EuroLeague picks BK07–BK10. T496 lists Leylah Fernandez v Marta Kostyuk as an upcoming Ningbo semifinal and recommends Over 17.5. Official WTA reporting says Kostyuk retired in the second round on 21 October; a separate WTA report says Iva Jovic—not Kostyuk—beat Jasmine Paolini 6-4, 6-3; the WTA semifinal preview dated 23 October lists Fernandez v Marie Bouzkova and Rybakina v Shnaider. T496's TN04 prose both names the wrong opponent and assigns a result to the wrong player. Separately, the official EuroLeague calendar has Round 7 on 21–23 October and Round 8 on 27–28 October; the four T496 Round 7 fixtures are not on the official 24 October schedule, and their cited injury URL returns 404. See F-53917-01 and F-53917-14.

Other high-priority findings:

  • T496 publishes 51 High labels, but only 3 are strictly above 70%; 2 are exactly 70%; 46 are below 70% (62–<70%). All 51 displayed fair American estimates are in the stated band, but the task's >70% threshold is not met by most of the set.
  • GH01 is recorded as starting at 20:13Z but was observed at 20:30Z and still marked upcoming; its generated market/match pages retain it as upcoming. The linked GBGB schedule confirms the event date, not the exact 20:13Z timestamp.

  • CR02 and GS05 link only to sports-media publishers in their fixture rows. Official PCB and Limerick GAA pages read in this audit provide suitable governing-body review links; the T496 desk should add these rather than treating media links as official evidence.
  • The T496 source register has 44 entries but no source-level retrieval time, HTTP status, captured excerpt or content hash. That is an auditability gap, not proof the listed pages were never read.

Line-by-line findings

F-53917-01 — Critical: TN04 is a stale fixture and the prediction misattributes a result

  • Repository rows: data/496eaf02/fixtures.json#L113-L145 records Fernandez v Kostyuk as an upcoming 24 October semifinal. data/496eaf02/predictions.json#L41 is its prediction; matches.csv row 5 marks Over 17.5 High at 64.73% / −184.
  • Official WTA evidence: 21 Oct: Kostyuk retired against Alexandrova; 22 Oct: Jovic beat Paolini 6-4, 6-3; 23 Oct: WTA's Ningbo semifinal preview lists Fernandez v Bouzkova and Rybakina v Shnaider.
  • Disposition: withdraw TN04 and its prediction/markets from active selection views. Rebuild only from an official current order of play. This audit does not edit the peer-owned desk.

F-53917-14 — Critical: four High EuroLeague picks are absent from the official 24 October schedule

  • T496 lists BK07–BK10 as EuroLeague Round 7 games on 24 October, all High, with predictions about “Saturday” fixtures. The four fixture records begin at fixtures.json#L2988, #L3034, #L3079 and #L3125; the featured rows are matches.csv#L79-L82.
  • The EuroLeague official full-season calendar PDF, linked from the official schedule announcement, puts Round 7 on 21–23 October and the next round on 27–28 October. Actual Round 7 pairings include Efes–Besiktas, Bayern–Dubai, Partizan–Crvena Zvezda, Panathinaikos–Baskonia, and Olympiacos–Fenerbahce; none matches BK07–BK10, and there are no EuroLeague games listed on 24 October.
  • The current official Round 7 injury report confirms that T496's injury statements refer to different fixtures: Efes absences were reported for Efes v Besiktas; Fenerbahce was full-strength for Fenerbahce v Olympiacos, whose report listed Fournier and Dorsey out and Montero doubtful. T496's cited Round 7 URL returned a 404 page in this audit.
  • Disposition: withdraw BK07–BK10 and their markets/predictions. Rebuild only from a current official fixture schedule and the corresponding per-game injury report. This review does not edit the peer-owned desk.

F-53917-02 — High: most “High” labels fall below the >70% brief threshold

  • Recomputed from data/496eaf02/matches.csv and markets.csv: 3 >70%, 2 exactly 70%, 46 <70%, among 51 High-labelled featured picks. The code explicitly promotes 0.62–<0.70 outcomes to high (scripts/se496eaf02_model.py#L22-L29, #L67-L90); the dashboard says “62% or more” (496eaf02/high-confidence.html).
  • Disposition: if the brief's strict >70% threshold governs High, relabel/suppress all below-threshold picks. The two 70.00% rows do not satisfy a strict “greater than 70%” condition. T496's disclosed High-B policy is different from the requested threshold.

F-53917-03 — High: GH01 is marked upcoming after its stored start

  • data/496eaf02/fixtures.json#L1913-L1949 records GH01 start 2026-10-23T20:13:00Z, observation 2026-10-23T20:30:00Z, state=upcoming, and price expiry 02:30Z. The generated matches.html and sports/greyhounds.html still display the row as upcoming.
  • GBGB's 2026 Category One schedule confirms Friday 23 October at Romford but does not give the 20:13Z start. Its Champion Stakes preview also says the final is Friday, without that exact time.
  • Disposition: withhold GH01 from “upcoming” and active markets. If the exact time cannot be confirmed from an official schedule/racecard, blank it and leave status unknown; if confirmed at 20:13Z, classify it elapsed.

F-53917-04 — High: TN03 has a populated time the record itself says is unverified
