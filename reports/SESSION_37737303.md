## 3. Pricing and grading, in one paragraph

NHL: rating = point percentage + 0.035 × goal difference per game; home ice = +0.15 logit; money line =
logistic(3.2 × rating gap + 0.15); totals from each side's goals for/against per game with a 1.45-goal standard
deviation; puck line = money-line probability − 0.22 in log-odds, floored at 30%. Tennis: set from the two
semi-finals read on the ATP page. Baseball: conservative home-field estimate, because only one game of
postseason form exists. Everything else: the OLBG crowd split shrunk 40% toward a coin flip and labelled
`sentiment`, never `official`. Confidence: High needs ≥60% on our numbers, an official form input and a price
inside the band; Medium needs ≥55% with an official or publisher input; everything else is Low. No bookmaker
price was copied, and no injury or team-news claim is made anywhere, because no official team-news page was
readable.
