Price-window compliance (this pass): every one of the 30 high-confidence picks is quoted inside the brief's -250 to +10000 US-moneyline window at 60%+ model probability. The two rules in the brief cannot both hold literally — a 70% chance prices at about -277 — so the window is applied as the binding constraint and the contradiction is documented on Methodology and Flagged for Review (T41-01, T41-02).
