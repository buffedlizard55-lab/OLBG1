Methodology (zero-vig band model v1)

High-confidence gate (all four required)

  1. Fixture verdict verified (governing body / league API read today);
  2. both side ratings measured from official-tier sources;
  3. model probability of the published selection ≥ 70.0%;
  4. its zero-vig fair price inside −250..+10000 US (fair decimal ≥ 1.40 for favourites).

Book-style prices at a symmetric 5% overround are published beside the fair lines for comparison only; compliance is tested on the vig-free number (T54/T55 consensus). Reason: at any positive margin a 70% pick is shorter than −250, so testing on the book price would make the brief arithmetically unsatisfiable.

Markets originated

  • Match winner (3-way with draw where the sport draws; Draw-No-Bet alongside);
  • Handicap ladder (main + alternates), walked outward in half-point steps to hunt an in-band rung;
  • Match totals where a competition baseline exists (documented assumption constants from the shared engine);
  • Darts/snooker/tennis rungs use their sport's Gaussian margin model; event listings (golf, F1, greyhound race cards) are published unpriced with flags rather than forced numbers.

Verification tiers

  • verified: governing body / official API read today (NHL, MLB, NBA, EuroLeague, ATP, WTA, WST, PDC news, URC, LNR, club pages, EHF, county GAA, CA/cricinfo);
  • cross-checked: official page consent/render-blocked in this environment; value confirmed against ≥2 consistent sources incl. official snippets (PDC OoM money table, WST world-ranking bracket numbers, EHF group tables, SHL table, ICC ratings via the root desk's verified ledger); caps at Medium;
  • pending: no official confirmation read; values blank & flagged; caps at Low.

Discipline notes

  • No bookmaker or aggregator odds were used as modelling inputs anywhere.
  • Unrated sides fall back to the neutral venue baseline and cap the row at Low.
  • Favourites breaching the −150 niche / −190 general caps on the 5% book price are graded Low (above-window Exception per T55).
