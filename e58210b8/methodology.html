What counts as a fact

A value appears in a table only if it was read from an official publisher — the league, tour or governing body — during this session, at the timestamp recorded in the source register. OLBG routes are discovery: they tell us where a sport lives, never what happened in a match. Media and aggregator pages are not used at all. Anything not read stays blank and is listed on Flagged for review.

How a price is originated

Two estimators are computed per club from the official standings and then averaged:

  1. Binary record share — wins ÷ games played, with overtime and shootout losses counted as defeats.
  2. Pythagorean expectation — goals for² ÷ (goals for² + goals against²), exponent 2.

The two sides are compared with log5, (pa − pa·pb) ÷ (pa + pb − 2·pa·pb). No home-ice edge is inserted, no default rating is substituted for a missing club, and no bookmaker quote is used anywhere. Fair decimal is 1 ÷ p with no margin; the American display follows from the decimal, so a −250 fair price is exactly a 71.43% probability.

Handicap and totals

Expected goals per side come from the two clubs' official scoring rates, again with no venue adjustment. A Poisson goal distribution gives the −1.5 cover chance and the 5.5 total. Overtime and shootout goals are excluded, which is disclosed on every totals row.

Confidence tiers and the price window

High means a probability of 70% or more. Medium is 60–70%. Everything else is low. The −250…+10000 window is reported as a separate flag on every call, because on this slate the four high calls all price shorter than −250 and nothing above 70% lands inside the window. Publishing that conflict is the point: it is the honest reading of the two rules together.
