How to read the confidence column

High — our probability is 70% or more. Medium — 60% to 69.9%. Low — below 60%.

Confidence describes the size of our number, not how much we trust the sample. Sample sizes are printed in the basis column; a 70% estimate built on two matches is still a 70% estimate built on two matches, which is why every sample size is disclosed rather than hidden behind the label.
