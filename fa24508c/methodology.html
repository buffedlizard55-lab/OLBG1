Methodology

The contract this desk works to, stated plainly enough to be checked.

Evidence rules

  • A fixture is verified only when its participants, competition and published start were read on the organiser's own site during the observation window.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed, the cell stays empty and an entry appears on the flagged page. Nothing is inferred, interpolated or filled from memory.
  • OLBG is discovery only. A tip share is a crowd percentage and is never treated as a probability.
  • Hashes and timestamps record what we read. They prove integrity, not truth.

Price construction

  • Fair decimal = 1 ÷ p, with no margin, no clipping and no overround.
  • American prices are derived from the decimal and are shown to the nearest integer.
  • Records (MLB, NHL) use Beta(2,2) shrinkage of win percentage and a log5 combination. No home-field term is added by default; the exception is the NBA, where a single league-average 0.55 home prior is used and disclosed as such.
  • Rugby union uses the mean points margin over the matches actually read, shrunk by n ÷ (n+4), fed into a normal model with σ = 13 points. Handicap lines are set to our own expected margin.
  • Totals (MLB, NHL) use independent Poisson scoring rates derived from goals/runs for and against per game. MLB totals are not pitcher-adjusted.
  • Every basis string on the markets page states the calculation and the sample size.

Confidence rules

  • High: p ≥ 0.70. Medium: 0.60 ≤ p < 0.70. Low: p < 0.60.
  • The brief's odds band is checked separately and shown in its own column. High-plus-in-band requires p between 0.700 and 0.714; today nothing lands there (F-13).
  • Confidence labels describe the estimate, not the sample. Sample sizes are always printed alongside.

Prediction rules

  • At least 30 words, unique wording, no template reuse.
  • Must name the competition, both participants and the selection.
  • Every factual clause must be traceable to a source read in this window, linked beneath the write-up.
  • No claim about injuries, team news or player availability is made unless an official page published it. Today no official page did, so no injury claim appears anywhere on this site.

Known limitations

  • Coverage is what one person could read in roughly thirty-five minutes; it is a sample of the day, not the day.
  • No bookmaker price was read, so no edge, no overround and no availability claim is made.
  • Model lines (handicaps and totals) are ours alone and do not correspond to any published market.
