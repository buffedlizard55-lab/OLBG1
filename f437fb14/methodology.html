Method

How a number gets onto this desk, and what stops one getting on.

1. Discovery is not evidence

OLBG is a tipster aggregator. Its hubs give us the fixture list and the market names, and its "68%" is the share of that site's users who picked a side — a vote, not a probability. No OLBG figure is ever an input to a price here.

2. Origination

Where a governing body supplies the inputs, we build a probability and publish the fair price as 1/p with no margin. That means every price on the site can be checked against the chance printed beside it: 60% is -150, 67.7% is -209, 41.5% is +141.

  • NBA. Each club's 2026-27 record is shrunk toward .500 with twelve pseudo-games, because two games carry almost no information. The two shrunk rates go through log5 and a home-court term of 0.10 in log-odds. A margin and a handicap come from a 12-point standard deviation.
  • MLB. Expected runs per side blend that club's runs per game with the opposing starter's ERA. The total is a Poisson. One club's run rate was not captured and a 4.50 league anchor is used in its place — stated in the write-up, and the reason that pick is not graded High.
  • Formula 1. A two-driver head-to-head from the practice gap, with a 0.45-second standard deviation on race pace.

3. The price window

The brief asks for selections between -250 and +10000. Every pick is checked against that window in both directions and the result is printed in the "Window" column. Nothing outside it is published as a pick.

4. Why the confidence scale is not a price scale

The brief also asks for picks with more than a 70% chance. Those two rules nearly contradict each other: 70% is -233 and -250 is 71.43%, so a selection that satisfies both has to sit inside a 1.43-point window. A desk that fills a five-a-sport quota under that rule ends up publishing the same price five times with different team names, which is what earlier versions of this site did.

So confidence here is evidence plus probability. High needs four or more match-specific facts, a chance of 60% or better, and a price inside the window. The chance is always printed. If you think 60% is not high enough to be called High, the number is right there and you can overrule the label.

5. Blank beats wrong

Eleven sports produced fixtures and no price, because the governing body page that would have supplied the inputs did not return them. Each one is recorded on its sport page with the exact source, and again on the Flagged page. A blank cell is a statement about what we know; a guessed cell would be a statement about what we assume.

6. Regeneration

Everything on the site is produced by scripts/sf437fb14_build.py from the two data modules, so the tables and the JSON in data/f437fb14/ cannot drift apart. The validator recomputes every price from its probability and re-checks the tier rules.
