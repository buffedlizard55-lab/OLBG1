What this desk will not do

No invented prices. Eleven sports have fixtures and no price. The alternative — pricing them from a tipster vote or from recollection — would make the table look fuller and be worthless.
No >70% shortcut. The brief's window is -250 to +10000. A 70% chance is -233 and -250 is 71.43%, so "above 70% and inside the window" is a 1.43-point sliver. Every previous desk that filled a High quota landed on the same price. This one grades on evidence instead and prints the probability so you can overrule it.
