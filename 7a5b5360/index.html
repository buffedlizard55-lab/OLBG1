How to read the numbers

Model is the probability this model assigns to the selection. Fair price is 1 ÷ that probability, i.e. our line with no margin. Book price adds a 4.5–7.5% margin so the number can be compared with a real market. Nothing here is a quote from a bookmaker.

Tiers: High ≥ 70%, Medium 55–69.9%, Low < 55%. The tier describes probability, not stake. Rugby union is capped at Low because no official league table could be read (F-7A-04).
