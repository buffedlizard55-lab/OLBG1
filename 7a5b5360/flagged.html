F-7A-01 The brief's -250 floor and its 70% threshold cannot both be satisfied once a bookmaker margin exists
highbrief-level, not a data error

A selection with a 70% chance prices at 1.4286 fair, i.e. about -233. Add any margin and it quotes at roughly 1.37, about -270. So a pick that is genuinely 70%+ almost always prices shorter than the -250 floor the brief asks us to stay above. This desk publishes the probability, the fair decimal price and an in-band yes/no for every selection rather than silently dropping either rule. Of the 26 selections at 70% or better on this board, 16 price shorter than -250.
