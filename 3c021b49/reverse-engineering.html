Observed platform quirks

  • OLBG's clock renders Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4) to this session with no timezone label; cross-checks against official UTC feeds are required. Confirmed this session: MLB World Series Game 1 shown as "Today 20:00" = 2026-10-24T00:00Z; ATP Brussels Fils v Michelsen "Not Before 18:30" Brussels = 16:30Z.
  • OLBG suppresses fixtures with zero open community tips, hiding entire active championships (Rugby League World Cup Round 2 men's and women's matches were found only on nrl.com, not on OLBG).
  • Hub listings cap at 20 rows and truncate ("Load More Tips"), hiding sub-route fixtures.
  • Name errors: 'Skanderborg Handbold' vs EHF's 'SAH Aarhus'; Waterford SFC Final date error on Gaelic Football hub.
  • Each-way outright markets render 0/0 consensus when only each-way tips exist.
