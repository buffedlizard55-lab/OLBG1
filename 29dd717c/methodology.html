How the desk works

Pricing & evidence method

Facts and estimates are separate fields. Official sources establish the fixture and input facts; the desk originates its own probabilities and fair prices. OLBG supplies discovery listings and tip-share metadata only.

1 · Discovery

Read all 15 relevant OLBG hubs and preserve event IDs, listed market labels and vote shares. A listing never confirms a sporting fact.

2 · Official check

Confirm participants and scheduling with league, federation, broadcaster-owned competition or official data endpoints. Record URLs, timestamps, external IDs and read limitations.

3 · Publication gate

Exclude events whose official start precedes 2026-10-24T22:43:09Z. Blank start fields are permitted only with a review note; started events cannot receive picks.

4 · Independent prices

Winner prices use current records, rankings, scoring or recent results with shrinkage and venue adjustments. Handicaps use explicit sport-specific margin distributions. Totals use Poisson or normal assumptions only where an official scoring baseline exists.

5 · Zero-vig presentation

Outcome probabilities in each published market sum to 100%. Decimal price is 1/p. American price is -100p/(1-p) at 50% or above and +100(1-p)/p below 50%. These are fair estimates, not offers.

6 · Confidence

High: at least 70%, fair price no shorter than -250 and at least 30 unique written words, with no lower evidence cap. Medium: generally 56% upward or a shorter-than-window price. Low: close matchups, underdogs or materially incomplete evidence.

Sport-specific assumptions

  • NHL: points share, team GAA and home advantage; margin SD 2.15; Poisson totals from combined team GAA.
  • NBA: heavily shrunk early records, home advantage and official availability; margin SD 12. Totals stay blank without official scoring averages.
  • MLB: season offence, run prevention and probable-starter ERA; Poisson total and a 3.6-run margin SD.
  • Volleyball: round-one sets/points with heavy one-round shrinkage; 1.25-set margin SD and explicit best-of-five total-set assumptions.
  • Rugby and handball: table points, scoring differential and venue; normal margin/total assumptions are printed beside each market.
  • Tennis: ranking, surface form, tournament/head-to-head history and semi-final workload; 5.4-game margin SD.
  • Gaelic sports: official final history and recent margins; totals stay blank where only margins, not scoring averages, are available.
  • Cricket: only supported series/form/home information. Runs handicaps, totals and player props stay blank without an official scoring distribution.

Known boundaries

Small samples are not presented as established trends. Listed goalies are not called starters unless officially designated. Silence on an injury page is not a clean bill of health. Dynamic shells, unavailable rankings and unread brackets create blanks, not guesses. Prediction prose never uses the two prohibited publication terms.
