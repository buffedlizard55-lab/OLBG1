Method

Independent line origination, source-first facts and the strict high-confidence gate.

Evidence order

1. OLBG sitemap, all-sports hub and 15 sport hubs are read to locate events, event IDs, market families and tip labels. 2. Only upcoming events are kept. 3. Each fact is checked against an official league, governing body, tour, promoter or club page. 4. If an official page does not publish the value, the field is blank and the gap is flagged. 5. Our own line is then produced from the remaining official inputs.

How the price is made

A deterministic evidence score uses official standings, seedings, recent official results and scoring rates. The score is translated into a 0–1 chance. The fair American price is the reciprocal of that chance: for chances above 50%, price = -100 × p/(1-p); below 50%, price = +100 × (1-p)/p. OLBG prices, tip percentages and bookmaker quotes are not inputs.

Strict high-confidence gate

High means our own chance is greater than 70% and the fair American price is from -250 through +10000. A short market-price favorite alone does not qualify. Medium is 62%–70%; low is below 62%. All 75 written picks carry their chance, price, confidence and exact selection.

Blank-and-flag rule

Exact UTC times, recent results, team statistics and availability are left blank when the official source read does not publish them. Predictions say when a field is blank; the review page groups those gaps. No value is inferred from OLBG's unlabeled times or tip shares.

Publication checks

Every table row has an official source link. Predictions are at least 30 words, name the selection, avoid internal build vocabulary, and share no repeated 25-word passage. Internal links, fragments, source hosts, confidence tiers and price arithmetic are checked by scripts/s0239d718_validate.py and its unit test before merge.
