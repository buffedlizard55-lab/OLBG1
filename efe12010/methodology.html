Methodology

How every price, band and written call on this desk is produced, and the rule that keeps unconfirmed values blank.

10 priced markets6 bands

Every number on this desk is reproducible from the official inputs listed beside it. No price is copied from a bookmaker, from OLBG, or from any aggregator. Where an input is missing, the market is left blank — there is no default rating anywhere in this pipeline.

The evidence rule

A fixture is marked confirmed only when its start time and both competitors were read from the governing body, league or tour that owns the competition. OLBG can put a fixture on the desk; it can never confirm one.

A value that an official source did not publish is rendered as a dash, listed on the flagged page, and excluded from every calculation that depends on it. Colorado's goals for and against are the clearest case: the fixture is confirmed and the money line is priced from the points column, but the total and the puck line are blank because the scoring rates were not in the portion of the official report that was read.

Confidence bands

The brief fixes the eligible window at −250 to +10000, which caps probability at 71.43%. Anything shorter is a better bet and a worse price, and the desk says so rather than quietly publishing it as a High.

Pricing formulas

All ten are implemented in scripts/sefe12010_engine.py and scripts/sefe12010_lines.py and are re-run from scratch on every build.

Margin

Each market shows two prices. The fair price is 1 / p with no margin at all, and it is the one the confidence band is measured against. The quoted price adds a flat loading — 4.5% on two-way markets, 6.0% on three-way, 8.0% on outrights — so a two-way book here totals 104.5%.

Markets where more than one selection can win at once, such as podium and points finishes, are marked as non-exclusive and their columns deliberately do not total 100%.

Writing rules for published calls

Every written call is at least thirty words, is written by hand rather than from a template, and names the selection in plain language so there is no ambiguity about what is being backed. Each one cites current form, the recent record and at least one fixture-specific statistic taken from the official source listed beside it.

Injuries are reported only where an official source published them. None of the feeds read for this desk carries an injury or availability list, and every call says so explicitly rather than reaching for a secondary report or an untraceable recollection.

Two words are banned from published call text and a test in the repository enforces their absence on every build.

Reproducing this desk

python3 scripts/sefe12010_build.py
python3 -m unittest tests.pipeline.test_desk_efe12010 -v
python3 scripts/validate.py

The build is deterministic and dependency-free. The data module holds the harvest and every official input as literals with their read timestamps, so a reviewer can diff the inputs independently of the arithmetic.
