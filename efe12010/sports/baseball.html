Tampa Bay Rays @ Milwaukee Brewers — World Series Game 1

2026-10-24 00:00Z

SelectionTampa Bay Rays +1.5 -185 64.9% · quoted -211

Milwaukee were the best regular-season team in baseball at 103-59 and they open the Series at home, where they went 55-26. Tampa Bay are no soft touch though: 98-64 with a 43-38 road record and only eighty-six runs of separation across the whole year. Opening nights are rarely blowouts, so take the Rays with a run and a half of insurance.

Team news: The schedule endpoint returned no probable starting pitcher and no injury list for Game 1, so neither rotation nor bullpen availability is priced in.
