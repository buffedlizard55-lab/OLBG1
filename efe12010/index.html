What this desk did

  1. Harvested all fifteen OLBG sport hubs and recorded every card, its clock, its tip counts and its consensus percentage.
  2. Worked out the clock. OLBG renders times in the viewer's zone; for this session that is Eastern Daylight Time, so every OLBG clock here is converted at +4 hours to UTC. The proof is on the reverse-engineering page.
  3. Re-sourced every fixture against the governing body, league or tour that owns it — NHL, MLB, EuroLeague, ATP, World Snooker Tour, Formula 1 and Cricket Australia.
  4. Originated our own prices from those official inputs, across win, handicap, total, team total, run line, puck line, set betting, frame handicap, podium and points markets.
  5. Published the gaps. Nine sports could not be confirmed on an official source; their rows ship blank with the exact URL and failure recorded.

Reading the prices

Headline prices on this desk are fair prices — the straight translation of our own probability, with no margin at all. That is the number the confidence band is measured against. A quoted price is shown beside it, carrying a flat loading so a two-way book totals 104.5%.

A selection is only eligible for the High label if its fair price sits between −250 and +10000. Anything shorter than −250 is held at Medium with a visible price-cap note, and the desk publishes an in-band derivative instead. That is why several high-confidence calls below are totals or handicaps rather than straight winners.
