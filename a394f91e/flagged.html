Type keys

  • coverage — no market or no official input existed, so nothing could be priced.
  • capture — data exists somewhere but was not read this session.
  • source — the official page was fetched and returned nothing usable.
  • discovery — OLBG's own listing is odd, stale or ambiguous.
  • method — a deliberate decision not to publish a number.
  • time — a timestamp or timezone problem.
  • derived — arithmetic done here on a published value, labelled as such.
