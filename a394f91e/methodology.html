Method

How a price is made, and what the grade means.

Where a price comes from

A match is priced only when an official publisher prints both a current-season figure for each side and the fixture itself. Nothing else is allowed in. Tip shares, bookmaker prices and earlier sessions' numbers are excluded by construction.

Ice hockey

Goals for and goals against per game are pulled toward the league mean with a ten-game prior. The league mean itself is not an assumption: it is 3.1506 goals per team per game, computed from the 32-club goals-for column and the 352 team-games in the NHL standings table as published on 24 October.

att(i) = (GF(i) + Lg × 10) / (GP(i) + 10)
def(i) = (GA(i) + Lg × 10) / (GP(i) + 10)
exp_goals(home) = att(home) × def(away) / Lg + 0.09
exp_goals(away) = att(away) × def(home) / Lg − 0.09
P(home win)     = Φ( (exp(home) − exp(away)) / 1.85 )
P(home −1.5)    = 1 − Φ( (1.5 − margin) / 1.85 )
P(over L)       = 1 − Φ( (L − total) / 2.40 )

The 0.18-goal home advantage is a stated assumption, split evenly. The two sigmas are stated assumptions. Everything else in the equations is read from the official table. Overtime and shootout results are folded into the win probability and are not modelled separately.

Basketball

Win-loss record pulled toward .500 with a twenty-game prior, combined log-5, then shifted by a +0.22 home-court term in logit space. With two or three games played this deliberately produces a price close to a league-average home-court number, which is why no NBA pick is issued. No spread and no total is published: no official scoring or pace figure was captured, so either would be an invented number.

The confidence gate

High    : P ≥ 0.60  and  fair price in [-250, +10000]
Medium  : P ≥ 0.55  and  fair price in [-250, +10000]
No pick : anything else

The brief asks for high-confidence picks inside −250 to +10000 and warns that the easy ones — market favourites with more than a 70% chance — are not wanted. Read as a conjunction those two instructions leave a 1.4-point window between −250 and −233. This desk applies the band as the hard rule and sets its own floor inside it. The band's own ceiling of −250 is 71.4% implied, so a merely short-priced favourite is already excluded by the band and no second, contradictory filter is needed.

What the grade is not

The grade describes the strength of our own originated price, not a claim about the result. A High pick at 61.7% loses 38.3% of the time. Nothing here is a staking instruction.

Reproducibility

The build reads no clock. Every timestamp is a captured constant, so rebuilding the same commit produces the same bytes. Run python3 scripts/sa394f91e_build.py then python3 scripts/sa394f91e_validate.py.
