Why the NBA games have no write-up

The five NBA fixtures carry originated prices but no picks, so there is nothing to write up. Three games into a season the only official input is the win-loss record, and the method returns a price within a few points of a league-average home-court number for every one of them. Writing a confident paragraph on top of that would be decoration. Flagged for review F06 records the decision and what would close it.
