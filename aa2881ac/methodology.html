Method

How prices and grades are produced.

What a pick is

For every officially confirmed fixture with official season figures for both sides we originate fair prices (no bookmaker margin) for the main result, a handicap and a total. The pick backs the side we make the winner. If that side's money-line price is shorter than -250, the pick moves to the same side's handicap.

Confidence

  • High: our probability at least 0.60 and fair price inside -250 to +10000.
  • Medium: 0.55 to 0.60, inside the band.
  • Low: 0.40 to 0.55, inside the band. Fixtures whose date is not on an official page are capped at Low.

Pricing by sport

  • Ice hockey (NHL, DEL): goals for and against per game, shrunk toward the league mean with a 10-game prior (NHL 3.152, DEL 3.0 goals per team-game), home edge 0.18 goals, normal spread of 1.85 on the margin and 2.40 on the total.
  • Baseball: season runs scored and allowed; the allowed rate is 5/9 the probable starter's runs per nine and 4/9 the club's. League baseline 4.642 runs per game (partial, see flags). Margin spread 4.3, total 4.5.
  • Tennis: ATP ranking points ratio for the match; the set handicap uses the per-set probability implied by a best-of-three match.
  • Handball: goals for and against shrunk with a 3-game prior (league 32.033 per team-game), home edge 1.5 goals, margin spread 5.5; the draw is the probability the margin rounds to zero.
  • Volleyball: share of rally points won, halved toward 50 percent, converted exactly to set and match probabilities.
  • Formula 1: share of 2026 race wins with half a win added per winner and two wins for the rest of the field.

These constants are our own settings, not figures published by any league.

Predictions

The five strongest picks per sport get a written prediction of at least thirty words. Every statistic quoted is in the linked official table. No injury report was read, so none is claimed.
