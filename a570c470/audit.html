LOW AUD-A-07 — Root-desk High picks quoted on a handicap rung sit inside the window while their moneyline does not

Where: data/lines.json · Status: disclosed

The brief asks for High picks between -250 and +10000. On the root desk, the published price of a High pick is the fair price on a rung chosen to land near 71%, which is inside the window; but the same fixture's moneyline is often far shorter (for example Panathinaikos at 1.12). The site therefore states both the window-compliant selection and the moneyline call so the reader can see the difference. This is a presentation risk, not a data error.

Official check: data/a570c470/lines.json
Effect: Disclosed on the Methodology and Flagged pages.
