Price-window compliance. A 70% probability is a fair price of 1.43 (-233), so a pick that is both "greater than 70% to win" and "inside -250 to +10000" has to sit in a narrow band. This desk originates the line, so for every strong favourite it quotes the handicap rung whose probability lands near 71% and prints that rung instead of the shorter moneyline. All 16 High picks are inside the window on their published price; each row also prints the moneyline call so the reader can see what was traded away.

How this desk was built

  • Harvest. All 27 OLBG sport routes were read live, including the competition sub-routes: see OLBG reversed for the URL grammar and the four upstream defects recorded.
  • Verify. Each fixture was then re-read on the governing body's own page. 45 official sources are catalogued; 116 rows carry two-sided figures and 25 rows are published as verified event cards with no price because no official strength rating exists for the field.
  • Price. One strength figure per side, one distribution per match. Winner, draw-no-bet, handicap and total markets all come from that single distribution, so the ladders cannot contradict the moneyline.
  • Grade. High needs a 70% model probability on a price inside the window; Medium 55%+; Low everything else. Sports with a one-match or one-round sample are capped and the cap is printed on every row.
  • Write. All 16 High picks carry a bespoke preview of 53–76 words naming the participants, the sourced form, the recent match history and the selection, with 0 repeated six-word phrases across the whole set.

Repository workspaces

This desk is additive. It does not edit any other session's dataset, script or page. The other boards in the repository are reachable from the root landing page and are named on the Repo Audit page, which also records the 8 defects found while reading them line by line.

Independent model output, not betting advice. Prices are our own fair-probability quotes with no margin; the 5% margin a bookmaker would need is shown beside them.
