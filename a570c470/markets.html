What each market means here

  • Match winner — the head-to-head result. Three-way in rugby union, rugby league, handball and Gaelic football, where a draw is a separate outcome.
  • Draw no bet — a two-way market that refunds the stake when regulation ends level; only in the four three-way sports.
  • Handicap — the margin market. Three rungs per fixture at roughly 50%, 60% and 71% cover probability for the favourite, each mirrored for the underdog. Units are the sport's own: goals, points, games, frames, legs or sets.
  • Total — over/under on the combined score, three rungs around the competition baseline. Published for reference and never used as the desk selection, because a total ladder can manufacture a 70% quote on either side at different lines.
  • Correct score — the full frame, leg or set scoreline grid, for the best-of-N sports only.
Totals are a modelling baseline, not a sourced figure. No governing body publishes an expected total, so the total line is a documented competition constant. It is printed as reference only.
