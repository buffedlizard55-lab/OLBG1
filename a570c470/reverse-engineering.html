All 27 OLBG sport routes and their competition sub-routes were read live on 22 October 2026. Nine of the exclusions in the brief map onto OLBG's own sport ids; the rest are handled as competition filters.

Tipster-consensus mechanics

MechanismHow OLBG computes it
Win-tip sharewin_tips / total_market_tips displayed as integer % on the most-tipped outcome
Each-way display bugWhen an outright/match market carries Each-Way (EW) tips with 0 straight Win tips (e.g. Cricket event 188350), OLBG renders '0/0' and blank '%' on the hub card.
Value Rating1-to-5 star rating comparing tipster consensus share (win_tips / total_tips) to bookmaker implied probability (1 / decimal_odds).
Experts filterRestricts consensus calculation to tipsters meeting trailing 30-day or 6-month sport-specific profit and strike-rate thresholds.
Blind-spot behaviourOLBG publishes match cards ONLY once at least one tipster posts a tip; major slates with 0 community tips (e.g. all 14 NHL games, all 9 AFLW Round 11 games, all 8 LoL Worlds Swiss R1 games, all 5 RLWC26 Round 2 match cards, and GBGB Category One Finals) return 'No tips found.' on OLBG.

Upstream defects recorded

  • Each-way division by zero. A market carrying only each-way tips renders 0/0 in the win-tip column and a blank percentage, so the consensus cannot be read at all. Observed on the Cricket hub.
  • Silent card suppression. OLBG publishes nothing for a fixture until a tipster posts. Four sports on this desk showed "No tips found" while the governing body listed a full slate.
  • Unlabelled local clock. OLBG prints a viewer-localised time with no zone. Every time on this desk is UTC and taken from the governing body, never from OLBG.
  • Neutral venues omitted. Maccabi Tel Aviv's home EuroLeague games are staged in Belgrade; OLBG lists the fixture without the neutral-venue note, which materially changes the home-advantage assumption.
