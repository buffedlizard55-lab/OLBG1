Material limitations

  • NHL starting goalies and complete availability were not captured. Montréal’s confirmed Suzuki absence is disclosed but receives no invented numerical adjustment.
  • MLB probable pitchers are named, but pitcher statistics are absent. Games 1 and 2 therefore share a team-total baseline and are not presented as pitcher-specific handicaps.
  • No independent price is compared with a real bookmaker quote. A fair probability alone does not demonstrate value.
  • Small early-season NHL samples are shrunk, not treated as settled team strength.

Timing and source irregularities

  • Cricket’s source-displayed “Your time” has no established zone.
  • Snooker’s direct and indexed official displays conflict.
  • Handball’s newer preview conflicts with older schedule snippets.
  • The volleyball request crossed league domains; its explicit fixture text is retained with a routing flag.
  • Exact per-URL network times were unavailable, so the registry uses a common post-read registration timestamp.

Disclosed constants

Team scoring and allowance rates are each shrunk toward a disclosed league-rate assumption with a five-game prior. Expected home/away scores combine the attack rate with the opponent allowance rate and disclosed scoring factors. Independent Poisson regulation scores are enumerated through 30. Tie mass is split evenly between a one-score home or away final margin and adds one score to the total. Winner markets include overtime/extra innings; handicap and total markets use the same disclosed tie approximation. These are experimental fair prices, not calibrated betting claims.
