METHODOLOGY

Reproducible, conservative and expire-aware

Run the data, lines and build scripts in that order. The generated site reads only committed local JSON; the browser makes no API calls.

1 · Evidence boundary

OLBG is a discovery surface. Official league, governing-body, tour, promoter and team pages are the only accepted sources for fixture facts, statistics and availability. Historical desk claims are leads, not fresh evidence. Unsupported fields remain blank and create review entries.

2 · Fair-line calculation

Team scoring and allowance rates are each shrunk toward a disclosed league-rate assumption with a five-game prior. Expected home/away scores combine the attack rate with the opponent allowance rate and disclosed scoring factors. Independent Poisson regulation scores are enumerated through 30. Tie mass is split evenly between a one-score home or away final margin and adds one score to the total. Winner markets include overtime/extra innings; handicap and total markets use the same disclosed tie approximation. These are experimental fair prices, not calibrated betting claims.

shrunk_rate = (official_total + 5 × league_assumption) / (games + 5)
expected_home = league_rate × home_attack × away_allowance × home_factor
expected_away = league_rate × away_attack × home_allowance × away_factor
fair_decimal = 1 / probability

The league-rate and home/away factors are modeling assumptions, not official facts. Every two-way market is zero-vig. Calculations use unrounded probabilities for gates and display rounded values afterward.

3 · High release gate

High requires an open non-expired OLBG market, official fixture evidence, sufficient official availability information, unrounded probability strictly above 70%, fair American odds from −250 through +10000, and a unique natural prediction of at least 30 words with participants, selection, current form and relevant history/statistics. Failure of any gate means no High release.

4 · Expiry

Evidence expires at 2026-10-24T03:36:17Z. A price expires at the earlier of that instant and its official UTC start. Browser-time code hides expired prices; source rows remain visible as historical observations. Rows without an established UTC start display the official local/date text instead of a fabricated timestamp.

6 · Interpretation

No odds are offered, no edge or return is promised, and no open wager is claimed. Research confidence is capped at Low or Medium because current market availability and complete availability packages are absent. High remains zero.
