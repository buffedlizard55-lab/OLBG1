Method

1. Fixture discovery

Official feeds come first: the NHL schedule and club summary, the EuroLeague standings and Round 7 Game Center, the AFLW fixture and ladder, and the MLB standings. OLBG discovery cards fill gaps in the other sports, but a card alone never becomes a priced row.

2. Originated markets

Every priced fixture gets a Match Winner market, a main handicap or points line, a main total, and — where one exists — a half-point rung that lands inside the brief's window. NBA-style whole-number ties are excluded from picks. NHL handicaps use ±1.5; basketball and AFLW use half-point lines.

3. Probability model

Team strength is the 50/50 blend of a side's own scoring rate and its opponent's conceding rate. Basketball margins use σ = 12 points and totals σ = 18; NHL margins use σ = 2.0 goals and totals σ = 2.4; AFLW uses the ladder-derived margin with σ = 18.5. There is no home-court or home-ice term anywhere, so home advantage is not double-counted.

4. Prices and tiers

Fair decimal is 1/p with no vig and no clipping; the American price is derived from the rounded decimal. High requires p > 70%, a fair price in 1.40–1.4286, official inputs on both sides and a unique hand-written prediction. Medium is a priced pick missing one gate. Carried inputs are capped at Low. Anything without a current input is blank.

5. What this is not

These are research lines, not bookmaker quotes and not tips copied from OLBG. OLBG's tipster percentages are recorded on the discovery rows for transparency but are not inputs to any model.
