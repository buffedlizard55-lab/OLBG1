Method

The tier rule, the pricing rule, and the four inputs that would unlock more High picks.

The confidence rule, in full

TierRequiresIn this snapshot
HighA 70%+ chance, a fair price no shorter than -250, and every input the selection depends on read from an official publisher.0 picks
MediumA 55-70% chance, or a 70%+ chance whose fair price is outside the band, or one unread input.2 picks
LowEverything else, including any pick resting on a single practice session or one side of a matchup.3 picks

Why the High tier is empty today

The brief sets two thresholds that nearly touch. A pick needs a chance above 70%, and it needs to sit between -250 and +10000. A fair price of -250 is a 71.43% chance, so any pick that satisfies both must land in a 1.43-point window between 70.00% and 71.43%. Our four verified sports produced chances of 75.6% (too short a price), 62.5%, 62.1%, 54.5%, 31% and 29% (too long). None lands in the window, and moving a number to make it land would be inventing evidence.

How a price is produced

  1. Read the official input. Club record, L10, home split, goals or runs for and against, session classification, seed and round result. Nothing else.
  2. State the formula. De-vigged provider pair, a seed-based logistic prior, or a stated conversion rate. The formula is printed in the basis column of Markets & lines.
  3. Convert. Fair decimal is 1/p with no margin and no clipping; the American price follows from the decimal.
  4. Grade. Apply the table above. If an input is missing, the pick cannot be High, whatever the number says.

Rules this desk follows

  • No value is filled in from memory, from a preview article, or from a sibling snapshot.
  • No pick is issued on a match whose start has passed.
  • No claim about injuries is made unless an official availability source was read; none was, so none is made.
  • Every written prediction is unique, at least 30 words, names its selection, and uses only the facts printed under it.
  • Two words are kept out of the published predictions by rule: the vocabulary of modelling and of verification belongs to this method page, not to the tip text.

What would raise the High count

Readable official goal rates for both sides of a hockey or handball match; a published total or handicap quote; official team news feeds for availability; and an order of play with start times for tennis. With those four inputs, a High pick becomes a matter of arithmetic rather than a search for a number that happens to land in a 1.4-point window.
