How the lines were built

Where an official quote exists

For the three NHL games with a readable price pair, we de-vig the pair: each implied probability is divided by the sum of the two. That removes the provider margin and produces a fair price. The original provider decimal is still shown in the quote column.

Where it does not

For the puck line, the race and the tennis final we build the number ourselves from the official inputs printed on the row and state the formula. These are research lines, not available offers — the same distinction the repository's own methodology makes.

Markets investigated but not published

  • Totals for the NHL game — no official total was quoted and San Jose's goal rate was unreadable, so a total would have been invented.
  • Handicaps outside the puck line — same missing input.
  • Player markets (first try scorer, top batsman) — no official participant list was read.
  • Injury and availability markets — no official availability feed was readable, so no injury claim appears anywhere on this desk.
