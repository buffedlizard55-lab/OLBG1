How to read this desk

1. The pick is the first column

The selection is written out in full — "Boston Bruins to win (moneyline)", not a bare "win". The market and price sit underneath it.

2. The chance and the price are ours

Every number in the chance and fair-price columns comes from our own line, built from the official inputs named in Markets & lines. It is an estimate, never a quoted bookmaker price.

3. The source column is the proof

Each match links to the league or tour page it was read from. If a field could not be read there, it is blank on the row and listed on Flagged for review.

4. Unpriced sports say so

Eleven of the fifteen sports have no priced selection today. The reason for each is printed on Sports; none of them was filled in to reach a quota.
