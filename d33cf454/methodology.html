How this works

Method and limits

The pipeline

  1. Discovery. The OLBG hub for each of the fifteen sports was read live and every open market recorded with its OLBG URL, selection and tip count. Tip counts are community votes and are never used as probabilities.
  2. Official confirmation. For each candidate, a governing body, league, tour or club page was opened. Only what that page states is carried into the row.
  3. Line origination. Our probability is built from the confirmed inputs: season record and splits for baseball and hockey, published availability for basketball, and practice/route evidence for motorsport and snooker. The fair price is 1/p with no margin, reported in American odds.
  4. Tiering. High needs p > 0.70 and a fair price between −250 and +10000. Medium is a defensible edge below that bar. Low is a weak opinion kept for completeness.
  5. Writing. Each pick gets a unique write-up of at least 30 words naming the selection, both sides, the competition, current form and the official fact it rests on.

Timezone rule

OLBG sport hubs render kick-offs in US Eastern (UTC−4). This was established by matching all eight NBA fixtures on the hub to the tip-off times printed in the NBA's official calendar for the same day. UTC times are therefore four hours ahead of the hub display. Any row whose time comes from that display is labelled derived and is never described as confirmed. Official times exist for MLB, NHL, NBA, Formula 1 and WST rows.

What "verified" means here

A fact is treated as confirmed only when an official publisher's page was opened during this session and it stated that fact. The word itself is kept out of the public predictions, and no value is ever inferred, interpolated or carried in from memory.
