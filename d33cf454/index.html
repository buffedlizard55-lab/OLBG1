Why some sports have no pick

1. The evidence gate

A pick needs three things: an open OLBG market, an official fixture confirmation, and at least one official form or availability fact. Sports where the second or third was missing produce zero picks, not a weak one.

2. High is not a label

No market produced a probability above 70% on the facts that were readable outside the three High picks shown. A 55% pick labelled High would be a lie with a number attached.

3. Official feeds are thin

MLB, the NHL, the NBA, Formula 1 and the World Snooker Tour publish machine-readable fixtures. Cricket, handball, volleyball, darts, Gaelic games and rugby league do not publish anything comparable that was reachable here.
