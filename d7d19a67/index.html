What this desk is

Every row below was read from an official source during this session - a league or governing body publishing its own fixtures and statistics - on 2026-10-22T12:55:00Z, and every price is originated here from those reads. OLBG.com was used for one purpose only: to enumerate which sports exist and what its hub URLs are, so the coverage table can be compared against the brief. No tip, no price and no probability was taken from OLBG or from any operator.

Scope: the seventeen in-scope sports. Excluded as instructed: soccer/football, American football, boxing, UFC/MMA, hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials. Fixtures are the ones dated 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-29 on the official page named in each row.

Read this before using any number here. Three models (nhl-poisson-v1, mlb-poisson-v1, el-log5-v1) are independent Poisson or log5 constructions built from official season totals. They are not calibrated out-of-sample, they carry no home-ice or home-field adjustment, and no bookmaker price was available to compare them with. A “High” label on this desk means our own price sits inside the brief’s −250…+10000 window and every input was officially read this session — it does not mean a measured 60 % hit rate. See Analysis and Flagged for review.

The brief’s literal rule, applied

The brief asks for High-confidence picks on matches between −250 and +10000 and notes that many High picks elsewhere are simply “greater than 70 % chance based on market prices”. Those two conditions overlap in a very thin band: a probability above 70 % is a price shorter than −233, so above 70 % and inside −250…+10000 leaves only −250…−233 (70.0–71.4 %). This desk publishes both readings rather than picking one silently:

How to read a row

  • Official source - the league, tour or governing body page the row was read from, with the read timestamp on the Sources page. Every row has one.
  • Blank cells - a value this desk could not confirm officially is left blank and flagged. It is never inferred from memory, from another desk or from a search snippet.
  • Prices - originated fair prices: decimal = 1 / probability with no margin, American derived from the unrounded decimal. “In window” means the fair American price expresses inside −250…+10000.
  • Tiers - attached to the winner market of each fixture, per the brief’s “pick a match winner and label each with a confidence level”. Derivative markets (3-way regulation, handicap, match total) are priced in the market explorer and explained in F-D7D-15.
  • Evidence expiry - fixtures expire 6 hours after the read, statistics 24 hours. This desk never re-stamps an old read as fresh; the whole desk expires with the timestamps shown above.
