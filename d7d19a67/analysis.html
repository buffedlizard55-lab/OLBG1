Analysis

Models and formulas, originated rates per fixture, an independent log5 cross-check, the arithmetic behind the brief's High-confidence window, and the desk's own weaknesses.

1. What was read, and what that makes possible

Sixteen official reads were made on 2026-10-22T12:55:00Z (see Sources). Three of them carried the statistics a line needs for both sides of a fixture: the NHL standings for 2026-10-21 (all 32 clubs, goals for and against, games played, points and streak codes), the MLB standings for the Rays and the Brewers (2026 regular-season runs scored and allowed), and the EuroLeague Round 7 Game Center (all 18 clubs' records plus points-for/against ranks, injuries and head-to-head notes). That is why 26 of 55 listings are priced and 29 are not: the other sports' official pages publish fixtures without any statistical input, and inventing one is exactly what the brief forbids.

2. The models

nhl-poisson-v1

Independent Poisson goal counts, 0…12 per club:

rate_for(A)    = goalFor(A) / gamesPlayed(A)          [official NHL standings, 2026-10-21]
rate_against(A)= goalAgainst(A) / gamesPlayed(A)
lambda_A       = (rate_for(A) + rate_against(B)) / 2
lambda_B       = (rate_for(B) + rate_against(A)) / 2
P(A wins incl. OT) = P(A > B) + P(A = B) * lambda_A / (lambda_A + lambda_B)

No home-ice advantage is inserted: none was measured this session. The overtime split is proportional to lambda, which is a disclosed model assumption, not an official statistic. Markets derived from the same matrix: 3-way regulation, A −1.5 / B +1.5, and totals at 5.5 and 6.5 goals.

mlb-poisson-v1

The identical construction on runs: rate = runs / 162 from the official 2026 regular-season totals (Rays 736 scored / 650 allowed; Brewers 832 / 618). Markets: winner including extra innings, −1.5 / +1.5 run line, totals at 7.5, 8.5 and 9.5 runs. No pitcher, park, lineup, rest or postseason adjustment exists in the inputs, so none is claimed - which is why all five World Series games originate the same line (F-D7D-13).

el-log5-v1

Beta(2,2)-shrunk win rates from the official Round 7 records, combined with log5:

p_shrunk(X) = (wins(X) + 2) / (wins(X) + losses(X) + 4)
P(A beats B) = (pA - pA*pB) / (pA + pB - 2*pA*pB)

Winner market only. Handicap and total lines are withheld because the official preview publishes no margin distribution for either club; the points-per-game ranks it does publish are quoted as context on the predictions page instead of being turned into a line.

5. Why the brief's two High rules barely overlap

“A lot of the high confidence picks are just picks that have a greater than 70 % chance to win based on market prices. We need to focus on generating high confidence picks for matches that sit between −250 and +10000 odds.” Those two conditions fight each other: 70 % is −233, and prices get shorter as probability rises, so the only prices satisfying both are −250 to −233.

7. Known weaknesses of this desk

  • Uncalibrated models: no out-of-sample hit rate exists for any of the three constructions, so a “High” label is a statement about inputs and price band, not a measured success rate.
  • Independent-Poisson artefacts: totals and handicaps inherit an expected total of 5.43–6.72 goals in hockey and 8.75 runs in baseball, which is higher than typical scoring environments; no correlation between the two sides is modelled.
  • Small samples: early-season NHL rates come from 7–12 games per club, and EuroLeague log5 from six rounds, which is why the Beta(2,2) shrinkage is applied to the basketball rows.
  • Two NHL rows have no away club (the official schedule chunk did not reach it) and all nine basketball rows have no venue (the official preview does not publish one).
  • No operator price was available, so no comparison of our lines to the market, and therefore no edge claim, is possible anywhere on this desk.
