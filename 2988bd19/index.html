How a High pick is earned here

01 · Official fixture

The game must be confirmed on the league's own feed with a venue and a UTC start — not on an aggregator.

02 · Our own price

Goals are modelled from official season rates; the fair price is 1/p with no margin. No copied bookmaker number.

03 · Both rules

Above 70% and fair price no shorter than −250. That intersection is only 1.43 points wide, which is why two picks survived.

04 · Written case

A unique ≥30-word prediction naming team, player, form and recent results — every claim tied to a page read this session.

Limits. The NHL model ignores goaltenders, injuries and rest. The MLB model uses regular-season run rates and ignores starting pitchers. NBA and EuroLeague fixtures are not priced. Read methodology and the flag register before using any number here.
