Methodology

How the numbers were made

One transparent model, one set of gates, and a rule that blank beats invented. Nothing on this desk is copied from a bookmaker or from another session's files.

The model

Goals are independent Poisson variables.

λhome = (home goals for/game + away goals against/game) / 2
λaway = (away goals for/game + home goals against/game) / 2

Every market is derived from the joint distribution of the two scores: money line (ties split 50/50 for NHL overtime), puck or run line at ±1.5 and ±2.5, match totals on half-point rungs, team totals, and a first-period total from λ/3. The fair decimal price is 1/p with no margin; the American price is derived from that probability, never from a bookmaker quote.

The gates

  • Official fixture only. No price is published on an event that was not read from a governing-body page.
  • High needs both rules. p > 70% and a fair price no shorter than −250, i.e. 70.00% < p ≤ 71.4286%.
  • High needs a written case. Named players, current form and recent results, each tied to a page read this session, in a unique piece of at least 30 words.
  • No defaults. An absent rating, an absent record or an absent fixture is left blank and flagged, not filled with a league average.

Known weaknesses

  • NHL: no goaltender, injury, rest, travel or home-ice term. Independent Poisson understates the effect of team quality on totals and overstates the chance of a tie.
  • MLB: regular-season rates only; starting pitchers, bullpens, park and weather are excluded. Later World Series games are conditional on the series continuing.
  • Basketball: no price published — the samples are too small for an honest rating.
  • Form: the NHL sample is 7-12 games per club, so a single hot or cold week moves the inputs noticeably. The sensitivity table on the analysis page quantifies that.

Reproducibility

The data is generated by scripts/s2988bd19_data.py and scripts/s2988bd19_model.py, written to data/2988bd19/desk.json, and rendered by scripts/s2988bd19_build.py. The validator scripts/s2988bd19_validate.py re-derives every price from the published inputs and fails the build if a link, a source or a High gate breaks.
