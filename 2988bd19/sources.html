Verification method

  • Fixtures: NHL schedule feed, MLB StatsAPI schedule, NBA schedule page. Venue and start time transcribed per game; no timezone guessed.
  • Team strength: NHL club summary endpoint, MLB season splits per club. Values transcribed, not recalculated from partial match lists.
  • Players: NHL skater summary, top three by points per club, used only where a High pick needed a player-level fact.
  • Results: NHL scoreboard feed for 19 and 20 October, used verbatim in the two predictions.
  • Discovery-only: OLBG hubs. Their tip percentages are votes; they are not probabilities and were not used.
