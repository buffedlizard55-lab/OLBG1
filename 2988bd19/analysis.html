Analysis

The window is 1.43 points wide

The brief asks for selections that are both above 70% and priced between −250 and +10000. Those two conditions overlap in a very small range, and that range — not a shortage of favourites — is what limits the High tier.

The arithmetic, worked in full

  • A 70% chance has fair decimal 1/0.70 = 1.4286, which is American −233.33. Anything more likely is a shorter price than that.
  • The brief's floor of −250 is a decimal price of 1.40, which is a chance of 1/1.40 = 71.4286%.
  • So the only probabilities that satisfy both are 70.00% < p ≤ 71.4286% — a band 1.43 percentage points wide.
  • +10000 corresponds to 0.99%. No two-way match market on this slate reaches it, so the upper half of the brief's range cannot bind for a selection that is also above 70%.

League context

Across the 26 NHL clubs whose totals were read, the average is 3.08 goals per team per game. A model line of 5.7 goals in a match is therefore close to league average, and the totals rungs around 5.5 and 6.5 are where most of the close probabilities land. The MLB model line is 8.75 runs, built from 162-game rates for both teams.

Why the NBA and EuroLeague are not priced

The NBA has played one game per club. A model built on that sample would be a default rating in disguise, which this repository's evidence rules forbid. EuroLeague has six or seven games per club — enough for a record, not enough for this session to confirm the fixtures themselves, because the official game centre rendered no schedule rows. Both are published as gaps with their official links intact.
