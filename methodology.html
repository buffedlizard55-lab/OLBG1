1. Facts, assumptions and offers are different things

Official schedules and statistics support only the fields actually observed. Display clocks without a timezone are not converted. Model probabilities, score means and fair odds are calculated estimates. Bookmaker quotes, settlement, lineup confirmation and head-to-head fields stay blank unless specifically evidenced.

2. Record-only match-winner baseline

For each team, use its observed games and wins, treating an NHL overtime loss as a loss in a two-way result. A symmetric Beta(2,2) prior gives r = (wins + 2) / (games + 4). Compare teams using p(A) = rA(1−rB) / [rA(1−rB) + rB(1−rA)]. Both sides must have a nonempty, consistent record. Missing inputs cause abstention, never a neutral 50% line.

This log5-style formula is a baseline assumption, not an empirically fitted skill rating. It ignores opposition strength, travel, injuries, starters and venue. NBA zero-game sides cannot be priced. MLB inputs are explicitly regular-season records, not postseason form. A sensitivity range varies the symmetric prior across 2, 4 and 8 games and a hypothetical home odds multiplier across 1.00 and 1.15; it is not a statistical confidence interval.

3. Illustrative handball regulation lines

For each side, average its observed goals scored per game with the opponent’s goals conceded per game. Assume two independent Poisson scores. Enumerate the distribution to obtain regulation 1X2 (including draw), half-goal handicaps, totals, team totals and the three most likely scores plus an all-other-scores outcome. A retained score grid is normalized only after its missing mass is below 10⁻¹⁰.

Total ladders are centered at floor(mean total)+0.5 and ±2 goals. Handicap ladders are centered on the mean difference at half-goal rungs, also ±2. No rung is tuned to reach 70% or squeeze inside a price window. The four-game samples, Poisson variance, independence and missing availability are material limitations; every handball line is capped Low.

4. Zero-vig odds and the High window

Decimal fair odds are 1/p. American odds are −100p/(1−p) above 50%, or +100(1−p)/p otherwise. Values displayed are rounded; gates compare the unrounded numbers. There is no artificial five-percent bookmaker margin, no −250 clamp and no market-implied tip probability substituted for an independent estimate.

At zero vig, a probability above 70% and fair odds no shorter than −250 intersect only at 0.70 < p ≤ 5/7. That narrow arithmetic interval is not evidence of forecast quality. A real quoted price can differ; an expected-value comparison requires a time-stamped quote for the same outcome and settlement. No comparable quote is approved here.

5. Confidence and abstention

  • Low: experimental winner estimates below 60% or with fewer than eight observed games on either side; all illustrative handball outcomes.
  • Medium: an uncalibrated record-baseline winner at least 60%, with at least eight observed games on each side. This is a descriptive tier, not a measured win rate.
  • High: disabled in this release. It would require independent held-out reliability evidence, fresh exact timing, a valid −250…+10000 quoted price with matching settlement, confirmed relevant form/history and a unique, evidence-grounded preview of at least 30 words. A high numerical model output alone is insufficient.

The user’s requested High board is therefore empty, not padded with trivial favorites or unsupported alternate lines. All approved selection fields are null.

6. Time, freshness and updates

The default build cutoff is actual UTC, not midnight on a hardcoded date. Starts at or before the cutoff are marked “start time passed”, which is not a fabricated result. Date-only rows are not declared currently upcoming. Manual date-only observations expire conservatively at the following UTC midnight; exact API observations expire after 24 hours. Browser-side expiry removes current projection cells after either start or expiry without changing archived calculations.

Building never renews observation dates. Optional automatic collectors cover only official NHL and MLB JSON, preserve raw responses, and leave old dates unchanged on failure. Other sports become visibly stale until genuinely reread. Automated scheduled publication updates the artifact, not repository branches.

7. What is not finished

Worldwide seven-day match coverage, all bookmaker markets, sport-specific validated models, current lineups and full historical backtests remain incomplete. The coverage and row-level review pages name the gaps. No site build, URL check, model formula or official homepage visit substitutes for this missing work.
