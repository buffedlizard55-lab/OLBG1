Line Origination & Verification Methodology

Transparent, zero-black-box mathematical specification of olbg-independent-logit-normal-v2.0 (`scripts/model.py`).

1. Governing-Body Strength Rating & Logit Win Model

Each team or player receives a documented 0–100 strength input directly from its governing body (points percentage in NHL/NBA/EuroLeague/MLB, log-rank in ATP/WTA/WST, tournament seed in PDC, official rating points in ICC/IRL, or league table points & goal difference in Top 14/EHF/AFLW). Given home baseline p_base (set to 0.500 on neutral venues):

logit(p_home_2way) = logit(p_venue) + (R_home - R_away) / scale

For 3-way sports (Rugby Union, Rugby League, Handball, Gaelic Football), regulation draw probability smoothly decays as mismatch widens: d = max(0.018, draw_p * exp(-((p_2way - 0.5)/0.34)^2)), with p_home + p_draw + p_away == 1.000000 strictly enforced before applying a uniform 5.0% bookmaker overround alongside exact 0%-vig fair odds and US moneyline prices.

2. Derived Spread / Handicap, Draw No Bet & Over/Under Totals

Expected margin mu = -sigma * Phi^-1(1 - p_home_2way) maps win probability coherently onto sport scoring units. Fixed-line sports (NHL Puck Line 1.5, MLB Run Line 1.5, Snooker Frames 1.5, Volleyball Sets 1.5) price exact normal cover probabilities at +-1.5; variable-spread sports round to the nearest half-point hook. Totals adjust competition baselines (e.g., NBA 228.5 vs EuroLeague 171.5 vs EuroCup 168.5; Men's T20I 339.5 vs Women's T20I 289.5 vs List A 529.5). A total is never invented: 1 multi-innings fixture has no source-backed competition baseline, so its total is blank and marked unverified.
