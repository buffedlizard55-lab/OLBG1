Methodology

Everything on this site is either a sourced fact or a labelled assumption. This page separates the two so you can audit the output without trusting the author.

1. What counts as a source

Only the governing body of the competition: the league's own API or site, the tour, the promoter, the federation. Betting sites, tipster aggregators, Wikipedia and news outlets are never used to confirm a fixture, a record or a ranking. Every row in every table links to the official page it was read from. The full register is on the Sources page.

2. Three verification states

StateMeaning
VerifiedThe value was read directly on an official page or official JSON endpoint.
Cross-checkedThe official page exists but did not render or publish the value. The row is corroborated by the competition's official broadcaster and is listed on Flagged for review.
UnverifiedNot confirmed on any official source. Published as blank plus a flag — never as an inferred value.

3. How the lines are originated

Each match is priced with a Bradley–Terry style model in log-odds space:

logit(p_home) = logit(base_home) + (rating_home − rating_away) / scale
odds(S)       = 1 / (p(S) × 1.05)        # 5% overround
handicap line = sigma × Φ⁻¹(p_home)      # expected margin, rounded to the nearest half
total line    = league baseline          # no match-specific scoring input is sourced

Ratings are only used when both sides have one. If a single participant is missing a sourced rating, the model refuses to carry a one-sided edge — using a real rating against a placeholder produces nonsense — and charges the sport's neutral home baseline instead. That is why the confidence split looks the way it does.

Rating inputs actually used

  • Baseball — each side's official 2026 home/road win percentage (MLB StatsAPI), scaled to 0–100.
  • Snooker — official WST world-ranking position, mapped by 100 − 70·ln(rank)/ln(60). Players outside the published top 36 are placed in a single bounded bucket at rank 60. That is a documented bound, not a measurement, and it is flagged on every affected row.
  • Ice hockey — team points percentage where captured. Only Boston carries one in this build, so all four NHL matches fall back to baseline.
  • Basketball, darts, cricket, rugby union — no rating source retrieved, so baseline only.

4. Confidence tiers

TierRule
HighBoth sides carry a sourced rating and the model probability clears the sport's high bar — Ice Hockey 0.58, Baseball 0.58, Snooker 0.62. High is relative to the sport's own variance: a 59% baseball favourite is a bigger edge than a 59% basketball favourite.
MediumBoth sides rated but below the high bar.
LowAt least one side unrated. Capped at low regardless of what the baseline implies.

Current distribution: 8 high · 3 medium · 38 low, across 49 matches.

5. What is deliberately missing

  • No match-total line is originated for snooker or darts: the governing body's match format was not verified, and a total-frames or total-legs line would rest on an assumption about format rather than a fact.
  • No correct-score, first-scorer or player-prop markets. There is no sourced input for them.
  • No injury, team-news or lineup data. Not retrieved means not claimed.
  • No in-play pricing. Everything here is a pre-match opening line.
