F-8A-13infoEvery High pick publishes at the same fair price because the brief's two rules intersect in a 1.4-point windowThe brief asks for a fair probability above 70% and a price between -250 and +10000. With zero-vig pricing a -250 line is exactly 71.43%, so the only prices that satisfy both are 70.00%-71.43% (decimal 1.4000-1.4286). This desk therefore points every High selection at 70.5% fair (published as -239) and moves the *selection* rather than the number: a strong favourite is expressed on the handicap or total line that sits in the window, and the moneyline price that would have been shorter than -250 is still printed on the markets page for each fixture. Identical prices across 66 rows are a structural consequence of the rule, not a copy-paste error.—Source
