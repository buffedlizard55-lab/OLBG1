OLBG reverse-engineering

How the OLBG.com public surface is organised, what this desk extracted from it, and where its published data stops being usable as evidence.

Routes and identifiers

The sport hub is /betting-tips/{Sport_Slug}/{sport_id} and the canonical slug is case sensitive. The fifteen sports in this brief map to IDs 12 (Golf), 21 (Volleyball), 9 (Rugby Union), 7 (Cricket), 8 (Snooker), 15 (Darts), 12 (Baseball), 25 (Gaelic Football), 28 (Greyhounds), 3 (Tennis), 14 (Motor Racing), 20 (Handball), 13 (Ice Hockey), 4 (Basketball) and 10 (Rugby League) as published on the sitemap and in each hub's own navigation. Competition sub-routes add a league segment, and individual events are reached with ?event_id= on the event slug.

Platform behaviours that shaped the method

  • Unlabelled local clocks. Hub rows render start times in the edge's local zone with no timezone label, so the same fixture can appear to move by hours between two routes. Every time on this desk comes from the governing body, not from OLBG.
  • Hub truncation. A sport hub lists a bounded number of rows before requiring a load-more interaction, and fixtures with no open tips are suppressed entirely. Absence from a hub is therefore not evidence that a fixture does not exist.
  • No zero-tip fixtures. Competitions with no community tips do not appear at all, which is why the desks that rely on hub scraping systematically under-report.
  • Tips are votes, not probabilities. The published percentages are the share of community tips on a selection. They are not a market price and were not used to derive any probability here.
  • Redirects and soft 200s. Invalid slug/case combinations return pages that render 200 but are not the sport hub. Discovery links were only accepted after the official source confirmed the same fixture.
Consequence for this desk: OLBG supplies the competition list and the market vocabulary, and nothing else. Every fixture, start time, participant, stat and availability note published here traces to a governing-body URL listed on the Sources page.
