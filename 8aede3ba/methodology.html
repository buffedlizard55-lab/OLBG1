Methodology

What was read, what was calculated, and where the limits are. Nothing on this site is a bookmaker price, a stake recommendation or a guarantee.

1. Fixture evidence

A row exists only if a league, tour, board, federation or club page published the participants and the date. The start time is converted from the local time printed on that page. Where the page printed no time, the row keeps a date-only start, is excluded from High picks, and says so. OLBG is used to find candidate competitions, never to confirm one; its clocks are rendered in an unlabelled local time zone and were treated as unreliable throughout.

2. Originated prices

Prices are zero-vig fair estimates: decimal = 1 ÷ probability, with no margin added and no clipping applied. American prices are derived from the unrounded decimal and then rounded to the nearest integer, which is why a 70.5% estimate publishes as −239 rather than a shorter chalk price. Every two-way book sums to 1.0000; every market row shows its own fair mass so the arithmetic can be checked on the page.

3. The four market families

  • Match winner — the headline pick, two-way except where a regulation draw exists (ice hockey 60-minute, handball).
  • Handicap / spread — inverted from the same win probability over the sport's scoring distribution: Poisson for baseball and hockey goals, Gaussian for basketball, rugby, handball, Gaelic games and cricket margins, exact best-of-N combinatorics for frames, sets and legs.
  • Match total — a line set just off the model's expected total with a hook, so no push is possible.
  • Sport-specific derivative — first-five-innings total (MLB), 60-minute result (NHL), total centuries (snooker), match 180s (darts), opening-set winner (tennis), both-teams-win-a-set (volleyball), first-half leader (basketball/rugby/handball), top-ten finish (golf), podium (motor racing).

4. Confidence gate

High requires both: a fair probability of at least 70% on the selection actually published, and a fair American price inside −250…+10000. Because a zero-vig price of −250 is exactly 71.4%, the honest High window is roughly 70.0%–71.4%: anything better than that prices shorter than −250 and is withheld, anything worse is not a 70% pick. When a genuine favourite prices shorter than −250 on the moneyline, the desk originates the handicap or total line that puts the same view inside the window rather than promoting chalk. Medium is 55–70% inside the window; Low is everything else. Every High pick carries a hand-written prediction of at least 30 words that names the selection; no prediction text contains the two internal words the brief excludes.

5. What is deliberately absent

No bookmaker quotes, no stakes, no expected-value claims, no correct-score or player-prop prices, and no timezones or line-ups that were not printed on an official page. Those absences are the reason the High count is smaller than the fixture count, and they are all listed on the Flagged page.
