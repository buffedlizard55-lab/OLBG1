How to read this desk

Zero-vig fair prices

Decimal equals 1 divided by the desk's probability, with no margin and no clipping. American prices are derived from the unrounded decimal, which is what makes the −250…+10000 test exact rather than cosmetic.

One official link per row

Every row links the governing body, league, tour or club that published the fixture. OLBG appears only as a discovery route and is never treated as verification.

Four market families, every fixture

Match winner, handicap/spread, match total and one sport-specific derivative (frames, sets, legs, periods, innings, placing). All four are priced from the same underlying model so the book is internally consistent.

Gaps are published

Unverified fields stay blank. Withheld markets, unresolved opponents, conflicting start times and quota shortfalls are listed with the URL that would resolve them.
