Method

The six steps, the declared parameters, and the things this desk deliberately refuses to do.

The rule this desk runs on

  1. Sweep the hub and record the event, competition, printed time, leading tip, market, tip count and share. Change nothing.
  2. Try to confirm the fixture on the governing body's own page. If it is not there, the row stays discovery and says so.
  3. Price only from a named numeric input on a confirmed fixture. De-vig quotes where quotes exist; use runs, records or results where they do; with nothing, publish no line.
  4. Grade mechanically from p and the price. Do not adjust a grade to hit a target.
  5. Write only about confirmed facts, and hang each clause on a source id.
  6. Publish every unresolved thing on the flagged page.

Every one of these is a stated prior, chosen before the day was priced, not fitted afterwards. The rugby shrinkage exists because a single confirmed round is a very thin sample; the 0.85 ceiling exists so a one-match sample cannot be expressed as a 99% certainty.

Rebuild

  • python3 scripts/scf2699ba_data.py — sources, discovery, fixtures, inputs
  • python3 scripts/scf2699ba_model.py — prices and grades
  • python3 scripts/scf2699ba_predictions.py — write-ups and flags
  • python3 scripts/scf2699ba_site.py — these 24 pages
  • python3 scripts/scf2699ba_validate.py — the gate; exits non-zero on failure

What this desk will not do

  • It will not convert an OLBG time to UTC. The hub prints no zone and mixes conventions between sports.
  • It will not quote a bookmaker price. The fair price shown is 1/p with no margin.
  • It will not call a tip-vote share a probability without halving it and capping it.
  • It will not mention an injury that no source published.
