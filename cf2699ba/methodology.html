Method

Short enough to audit in five minutes. If a number on this site cannot be reproduced from the rules below and the linked source, it should not be trusted.

Discovery versus fact

OLBG says which matches exist. It is never the source of a date, a venue, a record, a price or a result. Every fact on this site traces to a source id on the Sources page, and the role column marks which of those are official.

Two input classes, never mixed

market class

An official page confirmed the fixture and supplied a quantitative input. Only these rows can be graded Medium or High. Today: two NHL games, one MLB game, three URC matches.

consensus class

No official input exists; the only available signal is OLBG's public tip share. The share is shrunk 50% toward 0.5, capped into 0.50–0.90, published with the word "indicative", and permanently graded Low. It is a map of the board, not a probability.

Price arithmetic

  • Fair decimal = 1 / p. American = −100p/(1−p) above 50% and +100(1−p)/p below it. No margin, no clipping.
  • Probability is never rounded before the American conversion, so the price band test uses the unrounded value.
  • NHL: the American-quote providers in the league feed (−170 / −170 / −162 against +138 / +142 / +134, and +105 / +105 / +106 against −129 / −125 / −128) are de-vigged pairwise per provider, then the median of the three normalised probabilities is taken.
  • MLB: expected runs for each side are the mean of that side's scoring rate and the opponent's concession rate, over 162 games. Independent Poisson scores, tie mass removed, then read off for the money line, the 1.5 run line and a half-run total centred on the expectation. A log5 calculation on season win percentages is printed alongside as a cross-check.
  • URC: each club's verified Round 3 margin is shrunk 25% toward zero, a declared three-point home advantage is added, and the result is mapped through a normal with a declared 14-point standard deviation. Output is capped at 85% so that a one-round sample cannot manufacture a false certainty.

Grading

High needs p ≥ 0.70, an American price no shorter than −250, and market-class inputs. Medium is 0.62–0.70 on market-class inputs. Everything else is Low. The rule is applied mechanically; there is no manual override and no discretionary promotion.

Expiry and staleness

This is a single snapshot taken between 16:17 and 16:40 UTC on 23 October 2026. Prices describe that moment. Fixtures move, starting pitchers change and lines shift. Every page carries the snapshot stamp; nothing here refreshes itself.

What is deliberately absent

  • Injury, suspension and team-news data: none was published on the pages reached, so none is asserted.
  • Bookmaker quotes, availability and edge: this desk publishes fair prices, not offers.
  • Staking advice, guaranteed returns, or any suggestion that a Low selection is a strong one.
