How a grade is earned

  • High needs three things at once: a confirmed fixture, a win chance at or above 70%, and a price between -250 and +10000. Because a 70% chance prices at about -233, the band squeezes the eligible window to 70.0–71.4%.
  • Medium is a confirmed fixture at 62–70%.
  • Low is everything else, including every indicative row, whatever its number.

Grades are recomputed from p and the American price by the build script and checked again by the validator, so a hand edit cannot smuggle a High through.
