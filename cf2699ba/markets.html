Markets

Eight market families were explicitly considered for every confirmed fixture: money line and match winner, handicap, run line, puck line, match total, game total, set and frame scoring, and outrights. A market is only priced when an official input supports it. Where it does not, the market is listed as withheld with the reason rather than filled with a plausible-looking number.

What is priced, and from what. NHL money lines come from the de-vigged median of the three mutually consistent quote providers embedded in the league's own schedule feed. The MLB game uses paired official regular-season run rates in an independent-Poisson run model with the draw removed. URC match winners come from one verified round of results with a declared shrinkage, a declared three-point home advantage and a declared 14-point standard deviation. Nothing else is priced on official input.
