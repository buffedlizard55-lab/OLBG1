Why the High bar could not be met

A 70% win chance is a fair price of about -233. A price of -250 is a 71.4% chance. So the two halves of the rule overlap only between 70.0% and 71.4%: any selection good enough to clear the floor is too short to clear the band, and any selection long enough to sit inside the band is, by construction, below the floor.

The practical effect is that High is reachable only by a genuinely marginal favourite — the sort of price a bookmaker would hang at -240 to -245. None of the six fixtures confirmed today produces one. The strongest, Edinburgh, comes out at about 85% and prices at -567.
