What this desk will not do

No invention

If a start time, venue, record or availability note could not be read on an official page, the field is blank and the row is flagged. Nothing is filled in from memory, from a preview article or from another tipster's opinion.

No quota filling

7 predictions were written because 7 selections could be supported by an official fact trail. Inventing 68 more to reach five per sport would have produced exactly the output the brief forbids.

Where the evidence ran out

Three of the fifteen sports produced a confirmed fixture.
