How to read a flag

A flag is not an error in the arithmetic. It is a statement that the underlying evidence is missing, contradictory or inaccessible. Some flags (F-CF-01, F-CF-03, F-CF-05) describe defects in the sources themselves; others (F-CF-08 to F-CF-13) describe barriers that stopped this session short. Fixing them is future work, not a correction to today's numbers.
