Methodology

Every constant, every formula and every limit of what this desk can honestly claim.

The whole model, in enough detail to disagree with it. Every constant below is a modelling choice made by this session; none of them is presented as a statistic.

The five rules

  1. Official sources only. A record, ladder position, rating, seed or draw comes from the league, tour, federation or organiser that publishes it. OLBG is used to find matches, never to verify one.
  2. Blank beats wrong. If a value cannot be confirmed it stays empty and is listed on Flagged.
  3. Assumptions are labelled. The constants on this page are ours, printed in full.
  4. One-sided inputs carry no edge. If only one side has a verified strength input the row is priced from a neutral 50/50 baseline, flagged, and capped at Low confidence.
  5. Model output is never a sourced fact. Every price is an estimate and is labelled as one.

How a price is made

edge  e = (rating_home − rating_away) + HFA          # rating points, HFA only when not neutral
win   p = 1 / (1 + 10^(−e / denom))                   # two-way probability
draw      a share out of the two-way split, widest at a pick'em (three-way sports only)
margin m = margin_scale × e                           # in the sport's scoring unit
rungs     seven half-lines, m's pick'em line ± three steps of at least 1.0 unit
cover     p = 1 − Φ((−h − m) / sigma)                 # h is the signed home handicap
totals    baseball: mean of both sides' runs scored and allowed per game, sigma 4.30
price     fair = 1 / p ; book-equivalent = 1 / (p × 1.05) ; US odds from the decimal

Sport parameters

denom is the rating spread of the logit, hfa the home edge in rating points, draw the peak draw share, margin the scoring unit per rating point and sigma the standard deviation of the margin in that unit.

Why only 9 High picks

The brief asks for picks above a 70% chance and priced between −250 and +10000. At fair odds a −250 price is a 71.43% chance, so the qualifying band is 70.00%–71.43%: 1.43 percentage points wide. A desk hanging seven handicap rungs per match will occasionally drop one inside that window, and that is exactly what happened on 9 of the 105 priced fixtures. Every other ≥70% selection is published with its true (shorter) price in the band-excluded ledger rather than mis-labelled as High. Anything else would have meant moving a line until a price landed in the band, which is not a model — it is a target.

What the model cannot do

  • It cannot price a match where only one side has an official strength input. It refuses to guess the other.
  • It cannot price totals outside baseball, correct scores, player props or field boards, because no official page gives it the inputs before the match.
  • It does not know about injuries, team selection or travel beyond what the governing-body page publishes.
  • It has not been back-tested on results. The probabilities are calibrated by construction, not by outcome.
  • It publishes 1575 markets from 105 fixtures, and the correlation between rungs of the same match is total — the High tier is capped at one rung per fixture for that reason.
