What this page is. One desk that brings the whole repository's in-scope coverage into a single, consistent view: 122 fixtures across all 17 sports the brief allows, priced with this session's own model, tagged with a confidence tier, and linked row by row to the official governing-body page the facts came from. Where a value could not be confirmed it is blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Where the price of a ≥70% pick falls outside the brief's −250…+10000 window it is shown in the band-excluded ledger instead of being quietly relabelled.

How to read a row

Confidence tiers

  • High both sides measured on an official page, model probability ≥ 70%, fair price inside −250…+10000. At most one per fixture and three per sport.
  • Medium both sides measured, probability ≥ 60% but outside the band — usually because the price is shorter than −250.
  • Low one-sided inputs, a neutral baseline, or probability below 60%.

Verification labels

  • re-fetched today this session re-read the official endpoint and compared it with the register value.
  • carried the row keeps the official link and read date recorded by the register it came from; it was not re-read in this pass and is never described as re-verified.

What changed in this pass

  • Re-read four official endpoints live: the NHL standings feed for 2026-10-22, the MLB StatsAPI 2026 standings, the EuroLeague 2026-27 standings and the official AFLW ladder. 24 row-side comparisons are published with their results on the Verification page.
  • Merged two source registers (and their other 14 sibling desks’ context) into one desk, reconciling club-name variants and logging 10 cross-desk disagreements instead of silently picking a winner.
  • Dropped 7 fixtures that had already started before the publish cut-off rather than pricing games already in play.
  • Priced 1575 markets with a documented model: match winner, three-way where draws exist, seven-rung handicap ladders and baseball totals. 17 cards are left unpriced with the reason stated.
  • Found 537 selections at or above 70%; 42 of those also sit inside the brief's price window. The 105 fixtures whose best ≥70% selection is priced shorter than −250 are listed separately on the Markets page.
