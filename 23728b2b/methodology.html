Methodology and confidence

The desk separates sporting evidence, editorial probability assessment and price arithmetic. No sportsbook price is copied and no missing sporting fact is silently filled.

01

Discovery and scope

Exactly 15 requested sport pages are built. OLBG hubs identify candidate events and market context. The full list distinguishes priced OLBG-listed fixtures, withheld OLBG-listed fixtures and official-context-only rows.

02

Official evidence

Teams, players, dates, results, form, statistics and availability come from official competition, governing-body, club or tour pages. Every match-table row and alternative-ladder row carries an official review link. A discovery link never substitutes for one.

03

Independent probability

Analyst probabilities weigh the displayed official evidence, venue, current form and availability where available. They are estimates, not guarantees. Unsupported totals, props, traps and exact times remain blank. Alternatives are separate research lines, not accumulator instructions.

04

Reciprocal fair price

For probability p, fair decimal price is 1 / p. If decimal is at least 2.0, fair American price is 100 × (decimal − 1); otherwise it is −100 / (decimal − 1). Displayed prices are rounded, but the High gate uses the unrounded value.

Example: p = 0.68 → decimal 1.470588 → American −212.5 → displayed −213.

05

Confidence gates

High: probability strictly greater than 70% and fair American price from −250 through +10000. Exactly 70% is not High. A probability above 70% with a price shorter than −250 remains Medium.

Medium: non-High selections at 55% or above, including short-priced candidates outside the High window. Low: below 55%.

06

Written analysis

Every primary prediction is unique prose of at least 30 words, including all High picks. High team/head-to-head selections also require source-bound evidence for both sides. The copy must state an explicit selection and acknowledge important contrary evidence or uncertainty.

07

Time and missing fields

The research cut-off is 2026-10-24T17:56:39Z. Events live at that cut-off are excluded. A missing exact start remains blank with a display label and review flag. Later injury, lineup, weather and schedule changes can invalidate a pre-match read.

08

Review and limitations

Flagged for Review records conflicts, unsupported discoveries, source-access limitations and evidence-capped coverage. Flags do not automatically invalidate unrelated supported rows. This dashboard is research for adults, not a promise of profit or a live betting service.

A 71.0% selection has fair decimal 1.408451 and fair American −244.83. It can be High because 71.0% is above 70% and −244.83 is inside the required window.
