Methodology & Model Specification

Transparent, reproducible, and labelled as modelling choices — not statistics. No tipster odds are read. No value is inferred.

1. Fixture verification (official sources only)

Every fixture's participants, competition, stage, venue (where published) and start time are read from an official governing-body page: league API, tour PDF, federation calendar, club fixtures page. Aggregators, news outlets and Wikipedia are never used to verify a fact. If a value cannot be confirmed it is left blank and the row is flagged. OLBG is an upstream discovery source only — it never verifies a sporting fact.

  • ATP/WTA: signed Tour Supervisor draw PDFs (protennislive.com, wtafiles.wtatennis.com) + rankings (atptour.com, wtatennis.com)
  • NHL: api-web.nhle.com schedule + standings · NBA: nba.com games + standings · MLB: statsapi.mlb.com · EuroLeague: euroleaguebasketball.net
  • WST: wst.tv match centre + rankings · PDC: pdc.tv articles (draw/schedule via indexed article + Sky/ESPN corroboration where consent wall)
  • AFLW: afl.com.au fixtures + ladder · NRL/RLWC: nrl.com · EHF: ehfcl.eurohandball.com · PlusLiga: plusliga.pl (redirect noted) · GAA: munster.gaa.ie / gaacork.ie · F1: formula1.com · LoL: lolesports.com · GBGB: gbgb.org.uk

2. Ratings (0-100 scale)

Each side carries one rating derived only from the official record named in the fixture's home_input / away_input: points percentage, W-L, league position, seeding, world ranking. A side with no captured official rating takes the desk prior of 58.0 and is flagged; the fixture is capped at Low.

Home advantage (hfa) is a labelled constant per competition (e.g., NHL 0.25, NBA 0.05, EuroLeague 3.0 points) and is printed in the dataset. It is never presented as a statistic.

3. Probability & price (Normal/Binomial)

Edge = (home_rating - away_rating) + hfa. For Normal sports: P(home win) = Φ(edge / 17). For best-of-N (darts, snooker, tennis): Binomial leg model with per-leg p = Φ(edge / 65). Handicap: P(home -L) = Φ((edge - L)/σ). Totals: baseline per competition (e.g., NHL 6.5 σ2.1, NBA 168 σ14.5) with team-specific shifts where both sides' scoring rates were read. Prices are fair: dec = 1/p, us = + (d-1)*100 if d≥2 else -100/(d-1), rounded to 2dp, with no overround. The 5% book-equivalent is displayed for reference but is not the tested price.

Markets per match: Match winner (2-way or 3-way where draws exist), Handicap with fixed sport ladders (basketball 4.5/7.5/10.5, rugby 6.5/9.5/13.5, handball 2.5/4.5/6.5, AFLW 10.5/14.5/18.5/24.5, etc.), Match Total with baseline + three rungs. Totals are investigated-not-priced where the official scoring inputs were unavailable (NHL totals not priced due to goals reconciliation failure — see flagged).

4. Confidence tiers (with -250..+10000 window)

High: verified status, both sides measured, model prob ≥70% for match winner (≥65% for handicap rung), price inside -250..+10000 (decimal 1.40..101.00). Where the moneyline is shorter than -250 the same view is published on the handicap rung inside the window (e.g., Toulouse -7.0 instead of -667). Medium: ≥60% with both measured and verified. Low: ≥52% or capped (carried status, one-sided rating, event-only). The brief's >70% and ≥-250 rules cannot both bind on a margined price (70% at 5% = -277); the desk originates at zero vig where 70% → -233 and 71.43% → -250, so the band is satisfiable.

5. Predictions (High only)

Every High pick carries a bespoke preview of ≥30 words that names the participants, quotes the official form input, references recent match history, and declares the selection in plain English (e.g., “Selection: X to win at 1.42”). No two previews share a 6-word frame. Text is written per fixture, not templated.

6. Blank beats wrong

If a value cannot be confirmed on an official page it is left blank and flagged. Nothing is interpolated, averaged or filled from memory. Eight event cards (golf×4, greyhounds, LoL) have no head-to-head and carry no price.
