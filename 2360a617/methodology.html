Methodology, verification & source log

1. Collection

Covered sports (17): Tennis, Basketball, Golf, Cricket, Snooker, Darts, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Baseball, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Handball, Volleyball, Aussie Rules, eSports, Gaelic Football, Greyhounds. Rugby League, Aussie Rules and eSports had zero match events (documented on Flagged).

2. Timezone determination

OLBG displayed times are US Eastern, not UK: the greyhound race titled “11:14 Kinsley” (UK) displayed as “Today 06:14” — exactly BST−5h. Corroborated by Littler–O'Connor (15:15 ET = 21:15 Dortmund local, per Sky/Sportingnews) and India–NZ T20I (03:00 ET = 07:00 GMT = 20:00 NZDT, per BCCI/ICC). All tables show ET as displayed.

3. Originating our lines

For each event we investigated the market landscape OLBG exposes (win/moneyline, handicaps & point/puck/game/leg spreads, totals, set/first-set/frame markets, outrights) and originated our own line from: (a) verified records/standings/rankings, (b) verified recent results, (c) OLBG's own published historical base rates (e.g. Top 14 home win 74.4%, EHF CL draw ~9.8%, volleyball favourites 77.9–84.4%), and (d) consensus odds as a market prior. Where no verified data existed, numeric lines are left blank or explicitly labelled “model estimate” — never presented as fact.

4. Confidence policy

High (12 picks): verified official-grade evidence (league standings, official rankings, governing-body schedules) pointing one way; each carries a unique ≥30-word prediction. Medium (42): partial verification (event + one or two verified facts). Low (98): consensus/model only — honest uncertainty, often deliberately diverging from OLBG's crowd tip with a stated reason.

5. Verification & irregularities

Every row links its OLBG source event page. Independent sources consulted (all accessed 21 Oct 2026): official bodies — MLB.com, NBA.com, Formula1.com, LPGA.com, BCCI.tv, ICC-cricket.com, WTA (draws + rankings PDFs), ATP Tour draw pages, eurohandball.com/EHFCL, URC stats site; reference/data — ESPN, BBC Sport, Sky Sports, Wikipedia event pages, ESPNcricinfo, Hockey-Reference, Basketball-Reference, Sofascore, L'Equipe, all.rugby, Planet Rugby, Racing Post, AP, Seoul Economic Daily/SBS, Kalshi/lineups (model cross-checks), Tennis Tonic/Britwatch (draw results).
