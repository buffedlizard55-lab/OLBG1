Sports where no market could be priced

Twelve of the fifteen sports produced no priceable match, because the official page we reached either served a shell, returned an error, or carried no competition table. Those sports are still listed in full on Every match, and each carries an official recheck link. The blocking page is named for each one on Coverage vs brief.

Markets we investigated and refused

  • Basketball totals — the NBA standings page read carries no points data, so a total would be invented.
  • Baseball run line and total — Milwaukee's season runs scored and allowed sat outside the retained slice of the standings response.
  • Correct score, frames, legs, 180s, first try, podium — these need sport-specific official feeds (PDC, WST, EHF) that did not return usable content.
  • Greyhound trap and sectional markets — no GBGB card was captured for any of the fifteen races listed.
