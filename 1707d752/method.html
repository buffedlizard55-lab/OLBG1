Method

Nothing here is copied from a bookmaker. Every price is derived from the official records named on each row, using the constants below, and every pick's probability is printed so you can disagree with it.

Pricing

  • Ice hockey. Each club's goals for and against per game are shrunk toward the league rate with an eight-game prior. Expected goals for the home side = home scoring rate × away concession rate ÷ league rate × 1.03; the away side gets 0.97. A Poisson grid over 0–12 goals gives the result distribution; regulation ties are split 50/50 between the sides for the money line, ties of one goal do not cover a −1.5 puck line, and the total is read straight off the grid at 5.5.
  • Basketball. Win rates are shrunk toward .500 with a Beta(12,12) prior, converted to logits, and a 0.20 logit home term is added; the difference is the money line. No handicap and no total are issued, because the NBA standings page publishes wins and losses but not points scored or conceded, and a points line cannot be derived from a win rate without inventing a margin distribution.
  • Baseball. log5 on the two 162-game records with a mild prior, plus a 0.16 logit home term (about 54%). Extra innings are part of the money line.

Prices

Fair decimal = 1 ÷ probability, with no margin and no clipping. The American number is derived from the unrounded decimal. A price is never rounded toward a more attractive number.

Confidence

  • High — our chance is at least 60%, the fair price is inside −250…+10000, every input was captured, and both sides have played at least 8 games (hockey), 20 (basketball) or 60 (baseball).
  • Medium — 53% to 60%, or a stronger chance that misses one of the High conditions. The missed condition is printed on the pick.
  • Low — below 53%.
Why the brief's two High tests barely overlap. A fair price of −250 is exactly 71.43%. A selection above 70% is therefore shorter than −233, so “more than 70% and between −250 and +10000” describes only the sliver from 70.0% to 71.4%. This desk publishes the occupied probability on every pick so you can apply either reading yourself. See F-17-01.

What a written prediction must contain

At least 30 words, the selection in plain language, both participants, the current record, the recent run, and at least one number specific to that match, all drawn from the official source linked beneath it. The shortest here is 62 words and no two share a repeated six-word phrase.

What is never used

OLBG tip percentages are votes, not probabilities, and are never converted into a price. No rating is defaulted for a team we could not read. No injury is asserted without an official report.

What this is. Our own prices, originated from official league records, and our own written analysis. Not a bookmaker, not a tipster service, not affiliated with OLBG. No selection is a guarantee. Where an input was missing it is blank and flagged, never filled in.
