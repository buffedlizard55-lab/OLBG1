How a price becomes a pick

Our own line, not a bookmaker's

Every price on this site is ours. Hockey uses each club's goals for and against per game, shrunk toward the league average, resolved through a Poisson scoreline grid; basketball uses conference records shrunk toward .500 plus a home term; baseball uses a log5 head-to-head of the 162-game records with a home-field term. Fair decimal is 1/p with no margin, and the American price is derived from that.

What we never do

  • No price without the official inputs both ways.
  • No injury claim unless an official report was read.
  • No High label bought with a short price.
  • No OLBG tipster percentage treated as a probability.
  • Blank cells stay blank and are flagged.

What this is. Our own prices, originated from official league records, and our own written analysis. Not a bookmaker, not a tipster service, not affiliated with OLBG. No selection is a guarantee. Where an input was missing it is blank and flagged, never filled in.
