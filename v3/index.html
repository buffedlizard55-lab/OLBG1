  • The band rule retired nineteen previously published High picks that sat shorter than -250.

How to read a row

Desk pick is the strongest selection on that match whose fair price still clears -250 (decimal 1.40). Model % is the desk's own probability for that selection. Confidence is High only when the probability is at least 70% and the price sits inside the -250 to +10000 band; below that band the pick is capped at Medium and labelled. Source opens the official page the participants, venue and start were read from.
