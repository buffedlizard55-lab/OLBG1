Methodology

How a row gets onto this board, and what the desk refuses to do.

Verification ladder

StepRule
1. SourceOnly a league, tour, governing body, club or tournament page counts. A search snippet is never a source for a value.
2. ParticipantsRead from the fixture list or draw. Nothing is inferred from a seed, a bracket position or last week's card.
3. Start timeConverted to UTC from the official local clock and the official timezone. Where the page does not publish a clock, the field is blank and flagged.
4. VenueRead from the official page. NHL rows leave the arena blank because the league schedule does not print it.
5. Form inputOne official number per side, printed verbatim in the row's market sheet.

Status labels

LabelMeaning
verifiedAn official page was read for this row on 22 October 2026.
carriedThe official link survives from the previous snapshot but was not re-read today. Capped at Medium.
flagA field is missing, a source disagrees, or a modelling default was applied.

Pricing

Match winner: p = Phi(edge / scale), with the edge built from the two official ratings plus a home-advantage constant set per competition. Handicap: the same Normal distribution shifted by the line. Total: a Normal around the desk's total line. Best-of-N sports: a binomial over legs, with the per-leg probability from the rating gap. Prices are fair to two decimals with no overround, and every market row shows the American price alongside the decimal one.

Limits: early-season samples (NBA has four games league-wide) make records noisy; seeds are a proxy for strength in darts, snooker and tennis; and totals are the least reliable market on this board because no venue or weather adjustment is applied.

What is deliberately absent

  • No bookmaker or tipster price is read, quoted or compared.
  • No soccer, boxing, MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling or specials.
  • No implied probability is presented as a fact about the future.
