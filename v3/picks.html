Why so few High grades?

Because the desk publishes fair prices, a selection priced at -250 to -238 is a 70.0% to 71.4% shot by construction. Requiring both a 70% model probability and a price of 1.40 or longer therefore leaves a very narrow window, and most strong favourites are capped as "below band". That is the honest consequence of the policy rather than a calibration trick, and it is the single biggest difference between this board and the previous snapshot, where nineteen High picks sat shorter than -250.
