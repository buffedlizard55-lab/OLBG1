What this desk is

Session 99582dd3 is an independent offline verification and reconciliation desk. Its sandbox can only reach GitHub, npm and PyPI; live probes confirmed that OLBG and every sporting governing-body host fail at TLS egress. It therefore cannot harvest new fixtures or re-fetch official pages. Instead it audits the evidence already captured and merged in this repository, principally the 8a900c04 desk's 2026-10-23T16:45Z snapshot, which is the only retained dataset covering five events for each of the fifteen brief-listed sports.

Gate definitions

  • G1 Provenance. Every slate row must cite at least one HTTPS URL on a curated official governing-body, league, tour, federation or organiser host. OLBG is discovery-only and can never verify a sporting fact. Unregistered hosts raise a manual-review flag; OLBG-only cards stay unpriced on the flagged page.
  • G2 Arithmetic. Each market must satisfy fair decimal = 1/probability (tolerance 0.011), two-way probability mass summing to 1 (tolerance 0.011), and an American price re-derived from the probability with a 1.5% relative tolerance for long shots. American prices are derived from the clamped probability, mirroring the originating desk, not from the rounded decimal.
  • G3 Grade and window. High confidence requires probability strictly above 70% and a fair American price between −250 and +10000 (decimal 1.40–101.00). Favorites above 70% priced shorter than −250 are chalk-suppressed to medium. Medium otherwise begins at 57.5%; below that is low. Every stored grade is recomputed and compared.
  • G4 Timing. Starts are compared to the build timestamp: today, upcoming or elapsed. Elapsed rows keep their pre-match lines as a historical record and leave the current executive summary.
  • G5 Prose. A released prediction exists, is at least 30 words, has an accurate word-count field, contains neither internal vocabulary term, states a selection, and names a participant. Corpus uniqueness scans for shared seven-word phrases.
  • G6 Direction. The explicit selection sentence is scanned for a non-favorite described as beating the priced match-winner favorite. Historical form clauses and distance phrases are excluded.
  • G7 Cross-desk reconciliation. Matchups are token-matched against the 429a54a2, 3ee30bba and 7f000cb6 desks. Opposite favorite directions are high-severity conflicts; same-side probability gaps above 15 points are medium divergences. Conflicted events keep a visible badge and are excluded from the clean executive summary rather than silently picked.

What is and is not verified

The audit verifies internal consistency, provenance shape, price arithmetic, grading rule compliance, prose quality and cross-desk agreement. It does not recertify that a remote official page still shows the same fixture, score, injury or statistic — that requires network access this session does not have. All factual claims remain attributed to the originating desk's captured official sources, whose URLs are attached to every row. Unsupported values are blank and flagged; this desk never infers, interpolates or remembers a fact.
