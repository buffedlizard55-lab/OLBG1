Confidence Grading & Odds-Window Discipline

  • High Confidence (44 of 75 picks): Requires (a) 100% confirmed participants & official UTC start, (b) BOTH fair American odds and target-book American odds strictly inside [-250, +10000] (1.40 ≤ decimal ≤ 101.00), AND (c) zero-vig implied probability ≤ 70.0% so heavy chalk is never lazily Badged High. Where a match winner is heavy chalk (<-250), we evaluate balanced Handicap or Total derivatives instead or cap the pick at Medium.
  • Medium Confidence (29 of 75 picks): Assigned where statistical edge is solid but an auxiliary input remains pending (e.g. MLB Games 3–4 probable starters or Cricket pre-toss XI) or variance is elevated.
  • Low Confidence (2 of 75 picks): Reserved for conditional series games (MLB World Series Game 5 ifNecessary=Y) or wide-open 54-hole senior fields.
  • Weekend DST Transition Audit: Europe (CEST UTC+2 → CET UTC+1) and UK/Ireland (BST/IST UTC+1 → GMT UTC+0) fall back one hour at 01:00 UTC on Sunday 2026-10-25, while US Austin/Eastern clocks remain on CDT UTC-5 / EDT UTC-4 until November 1 and New Zealand is on NZDT UTC+13.
