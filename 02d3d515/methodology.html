Method

How a pick gets made

Four steps, each one visible on the site: confirm the match, read the form, originate a price, write the reasoning. Anything that fails a step is dropped or flagged.

1 · Confirm the match on an official schedule

OLBG tells us which matches are on the board. The start time, venue and participants are then taken from the governing body: api-web.nhle.com for the NHL, statsapi.mlb.com for MLB, nba.com for the NBA, atptour.com for tennis, formula1.com for the Grand Prix, top14.lnr.fr for the Top 14, pgatour.com for the leaderboard, gaacork.ie for Cork finals and ehfcl.eurohandball.com for handball. A fixture that appears only on OLBG is not published as a match.

2 · Read form for both sides

For the NHL and NBA that means the official 2026-27 record, last ten and streak. For MLB it means the season record, run differential and the series result. For the Top 14 it means the live league position. For tennis it means the semi-final scoreline. If only one side can be read, the match cannot be a high-confidence pick — it may still appear with a lower tier, and the missing side is named on the Flagged page.

3 · Originate a fair price

Prices are ours. A probability p becomes a decimal price of 1 ÷ p, which is a zero-vig price: a two-way book made this way sums to exactly 100%. American odds are converted from that decimal. Nothing is copied from a bookmaker and no margin is added.

The odds window and the 70% question. The brief asks for high-confidence picks between −250 and +10000, and separately describes high confidence as a chance above 70%. Those two rules cannot both bind on a two-way market: a −250 price is a 71.4% chance, so the >70% rule leaves only a 1.4-point band (decimal 1.400–1.429, roughly −250 to −233) before the price drops inside the excluded floor. We apply the window as the binding rule, publish the probability for every pick, and label any pick that also clears 70% with a 70%+ note in the data. 2 of our 21 picks clear 70%; all of them sit inside the window.

4 · Tier the pick

High

Fair price inside −250…+10000, our estimated chance at least 58%, and official form for both sides. 7 today.

Medium

Inside the window at 53–58%, or a genuine edge priced shorter than the −250 floor. 7 today.

Low

Partial evidence, a coin-flip price, or a multi-runner market. 7 today.

What we will not do

  • We will not publish a record, a score, an injury or a statistic that we did not read on an official page.
  • We will not price a market without the inputs it needs. That is why no NBA spread, no tennis total and no handball line appears today.
  • We will not call a pick high-confidence when only one side of the match has been read.
  • We will not fill an injury field from memory. Every injury cell is blank and says so.
  • We will not copy a bookmaker price or present our estimate as a market quote.

What this site is. An independent research dashboard. Prices are our own fair (zero-vig) estimates, not bookmaker quotes. A pick is an opinion, not a guarantee. 18+.
