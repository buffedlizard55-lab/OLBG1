How to read a pick

1 · The selection

The bold text in the Pick column is the bet: a team to win, a total to go under, a driver to finish on the podium. The market and our fair price sit underneath it.

2 · The confidence

High, Medium or Low. High needs three things at once: a fair price inside the requested window, at least a 58% estimated win chance, and official form for both sides.

3 · The evidence

Every row links the official page the numbers came from, with the UTC time it was read. Where a value is missing, the cell is blank and the row is on the Flagged page.
