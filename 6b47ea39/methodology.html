Rules, arithmetic, and honesty about their limits

Method and limits

What this desk did, what it could not do, and the arithmetic that decides which picks are labelled High.

Egress limitation, stated first. This sandbox can reach GitHub, npm and PyPI only. `www.olbg.com`, `api-web.nhle.com`, `statsapi.mlb.com`, `www.atptour.com`, `www.pdc.tv` and `buffedlizard55-lab.github.io` all failed at TLS during this session. No official page was re-read and no OLBG page was re-harvested. Every fixture and fact therefore carries the URL and capture timestamp of the desk that did read it, and is described as inherited.

The band arithmetic

American −250 converts to decimal 1.40, and 1 ÷ 1.40 = 0.7143. So the brief's two conditions — implied probability above 70%, price between −250 and +10000 — can only both hold between 70.00% and 71.43%. That is the whole window: 1.43 points wide. It explains why the two freshest desks both produced clusters of probabilities at 0.7045–0.7140, why one of them could claim 51 High picks while only 3 of its own lines qualified, and why five genuine high-confidence picks per sport cannot exist inside that band. This desk publishes the band for what it is and separates genuinely strong favourites into a second, clearly labelled tier.

The seven selection rules

R1Fixture must be upcoming at 21:30ZA pick on a match that has started is retrospective, not predictive.
R2No cross-desk directional conflictIf two books call different winners, neither is trustworthy enough to publish.
R3A recorded fact note must existWithout a form, statistics or availability note, a write-up would be invention.
R4Estimated probability above 70%The brief's threshold.
R5Estimated probability at or below 88%Above that, the price pays nothing and the "pick" is meaningless.
R6Main lines onlyAlternate spreads where the underdog side carries +5.5 at 83% are artefacts of the line, not opinions.
R7One pick per fixture, duplicate bets collapsedTwo desks pricing the same bet must not look like two opinions.

How prices were produced and treated

  • Every line is this project's own estimate, not a bookmaker quote, and not a claim about the market.
  • Each American price on this site is recomputed from the decimal price rather than copied, so a stale or transposed figure cannot survive.
  • Where two desks priced the same bet differently, the freshest observation is used and the older one is shown beside it in the market data.
  • Two-sided books were checked to sum to 1.000 by the source desks; that check is inherited, not re-run.
