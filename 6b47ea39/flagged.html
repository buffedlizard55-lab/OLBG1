HIGHF02

The requested High window is only 1.43 probability points wide

The brief asks for picks with a better-than-70% win chance priced between -250 and +10000. -250 is decimal 1.40, i.e. 71.43% implied. Any selection that is both above 70% and inside the window therefore has to sit between 70.00% and 71.43% — a band 1.43 points wide. Five picks per sport inside that band cannot be produced from real favourites: the genuine short-priced favourites (Perugia at 89%, Ireland at 80%, Kiel at 82%) are all shorter than -250 and are outside the window. This desk therefore publishes both: in-window picks as High, and stronger favourites outside the window clearly labelled as out-of-window rather than quietly squeezed into the band.

HIGHF08

Race and stroke-play events are modelled as two-sided matches
