How the desk is built

1 · Reconcile

Same fixture spelled differently across desks is collapsed, with every alias recorded. 125 fixtures remain from 218 raw rows.

2 · Recompute

Every price is re-derived from its decimal value. Band membership is recomputed rather than trusted.

3 · Gate

Seven documented rules decide what is publishable. Each rejection is counted and shown.

4 · Write

29 predictions, all at least 30 words, no shared six-word phrase, no internal vocabulary.
