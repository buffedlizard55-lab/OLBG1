Route structure and market taxonomy

OLBG discovery

How OLBG is laid out, reconstructed from the routes and identifiers recorded by the retained desks. Nothing here was fetched in this session; the route structure comes from the URLs stored on the fixtures.

Route shape. Every sport is a numbered hub: /betting-tips/<Sport_Name>/<id>. Individual events hang beneath a competition path and end in an event id: /betting-tips/Ice_Hockey/NHL/NHL/MTL_Canadiens_@_CHI_Blackhawks/13?event_id=199984. The number in the path is the sport id, not the event id; the event id is the query parameter.

Known platform irregularities

  • Hub tip feeds returned "No tips found" on the golf hub and, at various points on 23 October, on several other hubs, while the same event's page still rendered full tip blocks. Discovery had to go through the sport menu and event pages.
  • Clock handling. OLBG renders unlabelled America/New_York times on the hub; one retained desk records a one-hour discrepancy between the OLBG clock and the LNH calendar for Nantes v Chambery, so every start time on this site comes from the official source where one exists and is flagged where it does not.
  • Two-sided markets on multi-runner events. Several retained rows price a greyhound or a driver against a constructed "Field" side. OLBG does not publish that market; it is a modelling artefact and is treated as one here.
