F-53917-01CriticalTN04 is a stale fixture and its prediction misattributes a result

T496 publishes Leylah Fernandez v Marta Kostyuk as an upcoming Ningbo semifinal and recommends Over 17.5. The accompanying prose says Kostyuk beat Jasmine Paolini 6-4, 6-3. Official WTA reporting dated 21 Oct says Kostyuk retired against Ekaterina Alexandrova in the second round; WTA reporting dated 22 Oct says Iva Jovic—not Kostyuk—beat Paolini 6-4, 6-3; the WTA 23 Oct semifinal preview lists Fernandez v Marie Bouzkova and Rybakina v Shnaider. The TN04 fixture/pick should be withdrawn and only rebuilt from a current official draw/order of play. This audit does not edit the peer desk.

F-53917-14CriticalFour High EuroLeague picks are for fixtures absent from the official 24 October schedule

T496 publishes BK07-BK10 as four High EuroLeague Round 7 fixtures on 24 Oct. EuroLeague’s official calendar places Round 7 on 21-23 Oct and Round 8 on 27-28 Oct; the 22-23 Oct pairings differ from all four T496 listings, and the schedule has no EuroLeague games on 24 Oct. The player-availability text also splices information from different real games: BK07 uses Efes absences for Efes v Besiktas and Fenerbahce availability for Fenerbahce v Olympiacos; BK09 combines Olympiacos absences from its Fenerbahce game with a Bayern v Dubai fixture; BK10 combines Paris information from Paris v Valencia and Partizan information from Partizan v Crvena Zvezda. T496’s cited Round 7 injury URL returns 404. Withdraw all four current picks and rebuild from the official round calendar and per-game injury reports.
