Methodology — our own lines, stated in full

Inputs

Fixture facts and strength inputs come from the primary desk's recorded governing-body sources, corrected where today's re-read superseded them (see Verification). A strength edge is applied only when both sides carry a measured official rating; otherwise the model uses the sport's neutral venue baseline and confidence is capped at Low. For re-verified rugby-league rows the rating is the official IRL ranking percentage read live today; for NHL/EuroLeague it is the official points/win percentage ×100.

Model (assumptions labelled)

Win probability: logit(venue base) + (rating gap ÷ sport scale), using the root model's published constants (scripts/model.py: per-sport p_base, scale, sigma). Margin ~ Normal(E[M], sigma); totals use the published assumed competition baselines — modelling assumptions, not sourced team statistics — so no total-market selection can be High on this desk. Half-point handicap rungs target 50/60/70/80% favourite cover with the exact probability recomputed at the snapped line. In ice hockey, +0.5 puck-line selections are model-margin lines (they win when the selection avoids a modelled defeat; settlement semantics would be regulation +0.5).

The −250…+10000 window and >70% — the arithmetic

Fair odds 1.40 = −250 and imply 71.43%. A strict High therefore needs an exact model probability in the narrow band 70.00%–71.43% at fair (0% overround) prices. With a 5% margin the band is empty (70% would price at 1.36/−277), which is why the root desk's 68%-rung ladder contains zero >70% picks. This desk originates fair prices as the headline and prints the 5%-vig equivalent next to every quote; band membership is judged on the exact probability (≤71.4286%), not on rounded odds — a 71.5% line that rounds to 1.40 is rejected (Panathinaikos and England (RL) failed on exactly this today and sit at Medium).

Confidence gates (all must pass for High)

1) fixture status verified on the primary desk; 2) measured ratings on both sides; 3) two-way win probability ≥60% (a coin flip cannot manufacture a High through a plus-handicap); 4) a favourite winner/handicap line in the exact band above (cricket: match-winner market only); 5) the row was re-read on an official page this session (confirmed or corrected). Medium = both measured, verified, clears the sport's root medium floor. Low = everything else. Each row on the picks page prints which gate failed.
