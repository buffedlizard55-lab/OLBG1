How this desk works

Five steps, in order, for every match. A step that cannot be completed stops the chain — it does not get filled in by hand.

1. Find the matches (OLBG, discovery only)

We read the public OLBG hub for each of the 15 sports you listed: golf, volleyball, rugby union, cricket, snooker, darts, baseball, Gaelic sports, greyhounds, tennis, motor racing, handball, ice hockey, basketball and rugby league. The hub gives the match name, competition where OLBG shows one, a clock with no timezone, the most-tipped outcome and the market label. That is all we take from it. Tip shares are votes and are never used as a probability.

2. Confirm the fixture (official source)

A fixture is only priced if a league, tour or governing body confirms it. Today that was possible for the NBA, the NHL and MLB. For the other twelve sports the card exists on OLBG but no official confirmation was read, so the match is listed and left unpriced.

3. Originate our own price

Every price starts from a sourced record and shrink toward .500 with a Beta(2,2) prior, so a 2-0 start is not treated as a 100% team. The two shrunken rates are combined with log5. No home-field edge and no league-average default is inserted: if a side has no record, the fixture is not priced at all.

Handicap lines come from a normal margin family around that probability, with a disclosed spread of 12.5 points for basketball and 2.0 goals for hockey. Those are assumptions, and they are stated here rather than hidden. Prices are margin-free: decimal = 1 / probability, with the American equivalent derived from the same number.

4. Grade the confidence

High needs a fair chance of at least 70% and a price no shorter than −250. Because −250 is 71.4%, that is a 1.4-point window. Medium is 55–70%, or anything stronger than −250 (which is outside the stated window). Low is a marginal edge. A price within five points of even gets no selection at all — we will not pick a coin toss to fill a quota.

5. Write it up, or leave it blank

Every published pick carries an original analysis of at least 30 words that names the selection, both participants, the current record and the specific gap. Where an input is missing — injuries, lineups, a ranking, a timezone — the sentence says so. The flagged page collects those gaps with links.

What we did not do

  • No tipster percentage was converted into a probability.
  • No value was copied from another desk in this repository without re-reading its source.
  • No clock was converted to UTC unless a league feed supplied one.
  • No pick was published without a written analysis.
  • No sport was padded to five picks when the evidence did not support one.
