OLBG.com Reverse-Engineering & Upstream Defect Findings

Structural audit of robots.txt, sitemap-betting-tips.xml, and all 17 in-scope OLBG sport hubs live-fetched on 2026-10-22.

1. Split-Timezone Rendering Bug (Unlabeled BST vs EDT)

Accessed from identical US-East headers (UTC-4), OLBG hubs Basketball/4, Rugby_Union/9, Ice_Hockey/13, Handball/20, and Greyhounds/28 output hardcoded Europe/London (BST = UTC+1) clocks, whereas Tennis/3, Snooker/8, Baseball/12, Motor_Racing/14, Darts/15, Volleyball/21, and Gaelic_Football/25 render viewer-local EDT = UTC-4 clocks — with zero timezone badge anywhere in the DOM. Prior desk 53674ac8 fell into this exact trap, copying OLBG's 17:45 BST Handball clock as 17:45:00Z (a 1-hour error vs official 18:45 CEST = 16:45Z).

2. Taxonomy Collapse & Active-Tournament Blind Spots

14 of 17 hubs flatten match URLs to /All_<Sport>/All_Events/<Match>/<id>, stripping tournament and round metadata. Meanwhile, Aussie_Rules/22 and eSports/23 display "No tips found." during active NAB AFLW Round 11 and LoL Worlds 2026; Rugby_League/10 lists only 2027 ante-post markets while missing active RLWC 2026 Round 2 matches; and Golf/5 omits the PGA Tour's active Butterfield Bermuda Championship.

3. Concrete Fixture & UI Defects Caught Live

On Gaelic_Football/25, OLBG lists the Waterford SFC Final Rathgormack v The Nire (event_id=3536) as 01 Nov 10:30, contradicting Munster GAA's official Sun 25 Oct 2026 14:30 fixture at Fraher Field, and reverses Cork GAA's neutral-venue order on Nemo Rangers vs St. Finbarr's (3530). On Cricket/7, T20 Blast 2027 (188350) renders an empty Win-Tip count/percentage box on an Each-Way-only card.
