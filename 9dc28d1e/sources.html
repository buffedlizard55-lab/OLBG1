Source discipline

  • No secondary aggregator, no social post, no memory: a value that has no row in the table above is not on the desk.
  • Where a feed does not publish a field — EuroLeague tip-off times, NHL venues for seven of twelve games, the Tampa Bay Game 1 starter — the field is blank and the gap is flagged, never filled from another source.
  • Prices are model output, not quotes. Nothing here implies a bookmaker would offer them.
