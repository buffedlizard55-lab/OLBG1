Inputs

Team-season totals only: games played, wins, losses, overtime losses, goals or points for and against. All from the league's own feed, all read in a single observation window on 22 October 2026.

Ratings

Attack and defence are shrunk toward the league mean with weight k = GP/(GP+K): K = 12 games for NHL goals, K = 8 for EuroLeague points. Every club has played 6–12 games, so an unshrunk nine-game goal rate would be mostly noise. Expected scoring for a side is its adjusted attack plus the opponent's adjusted defence minus the league mean, plus a home edge (0.10 goals NHL, 1.5 points EuroLeague).

Distributions

  • NHL: independent Poisson goals per side. Moneyline = regulation win plus half the tie mass. Puck line uses the regulation margin and converts a tie into a one-goal overtime win. Totals use a Poisson total; team totals use each side's own Poisson mean.
  • EuroLeague: margin and total modelled as Normal around the expected values, with dispersions of 12.0 and 15.5 points. Those are documented assumptions, not fitted parameters.
  • MLB: log5 on the two official winning percentages. No run line, no total.

Pricing

fair_decimal = 1/p; the American price is derived from the same probability. No margin, no clipping, no rounding inside the calculation. A zero-margin price is a statement of the model's probability, not a quote anyone is obliged to honour.

Confidence rule

High requires all three of: probability strictly above 70%; fair American inside [−250, +10000]; and a unique ≥30-word prediction supported by verified player-level facts. Medium is 60–70%, or an in-band selection without the third condition. Low is everything else. “Outside window” is a separate flag, not a confidence tier.

What this desk does not know

  • No bookmaker price, availability or margin was read. No edge is claimed.
  • No injury, suspension, lineup, travel or rest information was imported.
  • No backtest against settled results was run; the probabilities are not calibrated frequencies.
  • Postseason MLB form is not modelled; the records are regular season.
  • Early-season NHL and EuroLeague samples are small and the shrinkage is a judgement, which is why the sensitivity table exists.

Estimate, not advice. Every price on this desk is a zero-margin model line originated from official team-season inputs. No bookmaker quote, availability or edge is claimed. Blank means unverified. This desk is independent of OLBG and of the canonical dashboard build.
