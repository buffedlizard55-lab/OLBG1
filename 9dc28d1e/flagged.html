F-9DC-01 · HIGH

The brief's own High gate is a 1.43-percentage-point window

A probability above 70% can never price longer than -233.33 American, because -233.33 is exactly the 70% point. Requiring both 'more than 70%' and 'between -250 and +10000' therefore leaves only probabilities in (70.00%, 71.43%]. Every selection outside that sliver is either too likely to be inside the price floor or too unlikely to clear 70%. This desk applies the conjunction literally and publishes the excluded near-misses.

F-9DC-07 · LOW

Overtime is split 50/50 by assumption

NHL moneylines settle after overtime and the shootout. Regulation goals are modelled as Poisson and a regulation tie is allocated half to each side. That is an assumption, not a measurement; it moves the moneyline by at most half of the tie mass, roughly two points of probability in these games.

Sources: nhl-team-summary-20262027 · Status: documented assumption
