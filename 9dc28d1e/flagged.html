F-9DC-01The High gate can only ever return a 1.43-point band of probabilities
More than 70% implies a fair American price shorter than −233.33. Requiring that price to be no shorter than −250 leaves 70.00% < p ≤ 71.43%. The +10000 end of the stated range corresponds to 0.99% and is unreachable in a two-way market. The rule therefore selects on price proximity, not on confidence.		Every High on this desk.
