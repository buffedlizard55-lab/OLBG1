Why the shortfall exists

1. Quota versus evidence

A pick needs three things: an open OLBG market, an official fixture confirmation, and at least one official form or availability fact. Sports where the second or third was missing produce zero picks, not a weak one.

2. Official feeds are thin

MLB and the NHL publish machine-readable fixtures, and the NBA publishes a daily injury report. Cricket, handball, volleyball, darts and Gaelic games do not publish anything comparable that was reachable here.

3. High is not a label

No market today produced a probability above 70% on the facts that were readable. A 55% pick labelled High would be a lie with a number attached.
