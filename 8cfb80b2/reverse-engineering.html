Things that trip a reader up

Times carry no timezone

Hubs are US Eastern; greyhound cards are displaced by five hours from the time printed in the race name. Never treat a hub time as UTC.

Silent zero-tip suppression

A competition with no open tips disappears from the hub entirely. Absence of a fixture on OLBG is not evidence that the fixture does not exist.

Empty hubs

The golf hub returned "No tips found" at capture; the All Events route returned the same body with the footer attached.

Tip share is not probability

The percentage beside a selection is the share of community tips on it. It is a vote count, and this desk never converts it into a price.

What we treated as in scope

Golf, Volleyball, Rugby Union, Cricket, Snooker, Darts, Baseball, Gaelic Sports, Greyhounds, Tennis, Motor Racing, Handball, Ice Hockey, Basketball and Rugby League. Soccer, American football, horse racing, boxing, MMA, hurling and cycling are excluded.
