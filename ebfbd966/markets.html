OLBG reverse-engineered: site & market map

What OLBG is and how its tipping engine works, documented from its own pages on 2026-10-21 — the map we priced against. OLBG (Online Betting Guide, founded 2002 per its own history) runs a free tipster community across 20+ sports: every tip is tracked, tipsters are ranked by 12-month profit, and each sport feed surfaces the most-tipped selection per event with its market, tip counts, comment count and 'expert' flags (profitable tipsters). Its Value Rating compares a selection's tip share with the probability implied by its odds.

How to read an OLBG feed row (and its traps)

  • The highlighted selection is the most-tipped outcome in whichever market attracted interest — not necessarily the match-winner market. A 'Total Legs: Over 9.5' or 'Underdog +3.5' row must be decoded before it says anything about who wins. Our engine does this explicitly per market type.
  • Percentages without denominators mislead: '100%' on site often means 1/1 or 2/2 tips. Every row here shows the fraction.
  • In 3-way handball, the Tie is a perpetual long-odds magnet for tipsters hunting value ratings — four matches this week have Tie as the most-tipped selection despite a sub-10% base rate.
  • 'Expert' flags denote tipsters with strong recent profit/strike rate in that sport (OLBG's definition) — a useful quality hint, recorded on every row here.
  • Outrights (tournament winners, 2027 championships etc.) are listed in the same feeds. They are out of match scope and excluded from pricing; Rugby League currently has only outrights, hence zero matches.
