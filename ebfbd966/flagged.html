Dataset-level caveats for the reviewer

  • Seed/rank provenance: PDC European Championship seeds beyond Zonneveld (4th) and Humphries (12th) — both stated in the PDC's official draw article — were transcribed from published draw tables summarising that official draw. Snooker rank numbers (Pang 26, Davies 89, Higgins 7, J. Jones 30) come from a secondary listing of the WST draw. The PDC/WST pages are linked on every affected row for re-checking; the HIGH grade for SNO-02 depends in part on the 63-place gap and should be re-confirmed on the official WST ranking list.
  • No ATP/WTA ranking gaps, no league standings (EuroLeague, SHL, domestic handball/volleyball) were verified this pass. Consequence: no tennis pick above MEDIUM, and team-sport totals tagged MODEL-BASELINE. This is the top enrichment task for the next session.
  • OLBG 'Load More' tail: fixtures with zero tips may exist beyond the fetched feed pages. Our universe = events with tips open in the feeds as fetched. Stated on the Methodology page.
  • Times: OLBG displayed times without a timezone in fetched text; reproduced as shown. Do not use for scheduling without checking the OLBG event page.
  • NBA home/away: OLBG uses US 'Away @ Home' format for NBA/NHL/MLB; rows preserve OLBG's naming order and the model credits home edge to the '@' host. Spot-check BKB-01…06, ICE-06/07, BBA-01.
  • Handball competitions unnamed: OLBG's feed does not label each handball fixture's competition (Champions League vs domestic leagues); league field says so rather than guessing. EHF link on each row.
  • Gaelic club championships unnamed in OLBG's feed for the same reason; GAA link provided.
  • Cricket tri-series label for Afghanistan v Bangladesh (UAE window 17–21 Oct) rests on the international fixture list rather than an ICC match page fetched this session — ICC rankings link on the row.
